## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, effects start about 10–15 seconds after smoking and last up to 60 minutes; full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness and memory, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

They have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days); in a case series about 30 % of patients had psychotic episodes beyond five months. [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with these compounds raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]

## counselling

Ask what the person thinks they have: they can be present as adulterants in cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable effects. [f:jones2025]

Explain that some are up to 140 times more potent than THC; the UBC guidelines add that the packaging does not guarantee the content and that intensity is highly variable. [f:lobato-freitas2021] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about psychiatric history: the psychosis from these compounds can last longer than from cannabis; the UBC guidelines note that they can intensify anxiety and paranoia. [f:ricci2026] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
