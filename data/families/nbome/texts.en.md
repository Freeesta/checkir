## effects

The NBOMes are very potent agonists of the 5-HT2A receptor (EC50 0.04-0.5 µM) with high affinity for adrenergic α1 receptors; the profile predicts strong hallucinogenic effects like those of LSD and possibly more stimulant ones. [f:rickli2015]

In the cases with analytical confirmation, agitation (85 %), tachycardia (85 %) and hypertension (65 %) predominate, then dilated pupils (55 %), delirium, hallucinations and seizures (40 % each) and fever (25 %). [f:suzuki2015]

In a survey of users the peak appears about 2 hours after oral ingestion and 45 minutes after insufflation, and the duration ranges from 3 to 13 hours. [f:suzuki2015]

## risks

In the review of 20 patients with confirmed NBOMe, 3 died (15 %), 8 patients were admitted to intensive care and creatine kinase was raised in 9; death can occur even after a single intake. [f:suzuki2015]

In a review of 51 cases rhabdomyolysis was a relatively common complication of severe intoxications, possibly linked to seizures, hyperthermia and vasoconstriction. [f:halberstadt2017]

The NBOMes are also sold as LSD on blotter paper: their potency allows a dose to fit on a blotter, and people who know LSD may have a false sense of security; 4 of 20 patients thought they had taken LSD and 2 died. [f:suzuki2015]

## interactions

5-HT2A agonism contributes to serotonin syndrome; for the NBOMes this syndrome has been suggested in severe reactions, although the typical neuromuscular signs were rare. [f:suzuki2015]

A systematic review included 25I-NBOMe among the new psychoactive substances associated with serotonin syndrome. [f:schifano2021]

## counselling

If the sample is blotter paper sold as LSD, explain that the NBOMes are sold as LSD and that people who know LSD may underestimate them; the UBC guidelines describe them as sometimes passed off as LSD or synthetic mescaline. [f:suzuki2015] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Explain that with the NBOMes a new dose should never be taken on the same occasion: the effect can arrive after a long time and the UBC guidelines advise waiting at least 2-3 hours and advise against use. [f:suzuki2015] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about the route of use: in the review the intravenous case had the most complicated course and the authors advise against insufflating or injecting and against using alone. [f:suzuki2015]

Explain the signs to recognise: agitation, rapid heartbeat, fever, seizures; the UBC guidelines add cold fingers, blue lips and numbness from vasoconstriction and say to call an ambulance at once. [f:suzuki2015] [f:halberstadt2017] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about MDMA, amphetamines and MAO-inhibitor antidepressants: because of the serotonergic action the UBC guidelines point to them as dangerous mixes. [f:schifano2021] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
