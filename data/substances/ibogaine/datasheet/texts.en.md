## molecule

Ibogaine, (1R,15R,17S,18S)-17-ethyl-7-methoxy-3,13-diazapentacyclo[13.3.1.02,10.04,9.013,18]nonadeca-2(10),4(9),5,7-tetraene. Formula C20H26N2O, 310.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A pentacyclic indole alkaloid (the iboga skeleton, which contains a tryptamine unit); the PubChem entry is the (-)-enantiomer with defined stereochemistry. [f:mash2023]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines (indole alkaloid with a tryptamine unit). Expected effect: psychedelic; it is a hallucinogenic drug. The tryptamines family sheet is not attached: it covers classic hallucinogens that are physiologically well tolerated, while ibogaine carries a cardiac risk (QT prolongation). [f:mash2023] [f:brunt2026] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

An alkaloid with a long ethnobotanical use in African ritual contexts. [f:mash2023]

## metabolism

Ibogaine is rapidly demethylated by first-pass metabolism to the long-acting metabolite noribogaine. [f:mash2023] In 14 patients given a single dose, plasma kinetics of ibogaine, noribogaine and noribogaine glucuronide were highly variable and clearance of ibogaine correlated with CYP2D6 genotype. [f:knuijver2024]
