## molecule

Phenytoin (5-5-diphenylhydantoin), 5,5-diphenylimidazolidine-2,4-dione. Formula C15H12N2O2, 252.27 g/mol, CAS 57-41-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is an antiepileptic (hydantoin derivative) used as a medicine. Expected effect: none psychoactive. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant (reported in cocaine). This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:dilantin-label] [f:roldan2014] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a hydantoin anticonvulsant. [f:dilantin-label]

## metabolism

Phenytoin is metabolized mainly by the liver enzyme CYP2C9 and, to a lesser extent, CYP2C19; the hydroxylation system is saturable at high serum levels, so small dose increases can raise levels disproportionately. [f:dilantin-label]

Most of the drug is excreted in the bile as inactive metabolites, which are reabsorbed and excreted in the urine. The plasma half-life after an oral dose averages 22 hours (range 7 to 42). [f:dilantin-label]

## cuts

Phenytoin has been reported as a cocaine adulterant: a case report cites patients with phenytoin toxicity after smoking crack cocaine cut with phenytoin capsules. [f:roldan2014]
