## molecule

Butalbital, 5-(2-methylpropyl)-5-prop-2-enyl-1,3-diazinane-2,4,6-trione. Formula C11H16N2O3, 224.26 g/mol, CAS 77-26-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a barbiturate marketed in combination with acetaminophen and caffeine for tension headache. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:butalbital-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a barbituric acid derivative substituted at position 5 with an allyl and an isobutyl group. [f:butalbital-label]

## metabolism

Butalbital is eliminated mainly by the kidney (59 to 88% of the dose) as unchanged drug or metabolites. Urinary products include the parent drug (about 3.6%), 5-isobutyl-5-(2,3-dihydroxypropyl)barbituric acid (about 24%), 5-allyl-5-(3-hydroxy-2-methyl-1-propyl)barbituric acid (about 4.8%) and products with the barbituric ring hydrolysed, with release of urea (about 14%). [f:butalbital-label]

The plasma half-life is about 35 hours. [f:butalbital-label]
