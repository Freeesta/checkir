## molecule

Zopiclone, [6-(5-chloro-2-pyridinyl)-5-oxo-7H-pyrrolo[3,4-b]pyrazin-7-yl] 4-methylpiperazine-1-carboxylate. C17H17ClN6O3, 388.8 g/mol, CAS 43200-80-2. Identity checked against PubChem, where the entry has no stereochemistry. [f:pubchem]

A cyclopyrrolone: a carbamate of 4-methylpiperazine on a pyrrolopyrazinone ring, with no benzodiazepine ring. [f:pubchem]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a non-benzodiazepine hypnotic. [f:becquemont1999] [f:checkir-classification]


## metabolism

Zopiclone is extensively metabolized by the liver. In human liver microsomes it gives N-desmethyl-zopiclone and zopiclone N-oxide; CYP3A4 is the major enzyme in vitro and CYP2C8 contributes significantly to N-desmethyl-zopiclone, while recombinant CYP2C8 had the highest activity, followed by CYP2C9 and CYP3A4. [f:becquemont1999]
