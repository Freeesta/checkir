## molecule

25B-NBOMe, 2-(4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethanamine. Formula C18H22BrNO3, 380.3 g/mol, CAS 1026511-90-9. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the brominated member of the series: 2C-B carrying a 2-methoxybenzyl group on the nitrogen; PubChem also lists it as Cimbi-36. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: NBOMe (N-benzyl phenethylamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:suzuki2015] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The NBOMes were synthesised for research as potent agonists of the 5-HT2A receptor. [f:suzuki2015]

Several NBOMes have been on sale from online vendors since 2010, with numerous cases of toxicity and several deaths. [f:halberstadt2017]

Fatal intoxications with 25B-NBOMe and 25I-NBOMe were described in Indiana during 2014. [f:shanks2015]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of 2C-B (a phenethylamine of the 2C series). [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

25B-NBOMe is metabolised mainly by O-demethylation, O,O-bis-demethylation and hydroxylation, and combinations of these, and by glucuronidation and sulfation of the phase I metabolites: 66 metabolites were identified in the study. [f:caspar2017]

In an initial screening with human enzymes, CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 took part in hydroxylation and CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 in O-demethylation; the compound and its metabolites were detected in a human urine sample from an acute intoxication. [f:caspar2017]
