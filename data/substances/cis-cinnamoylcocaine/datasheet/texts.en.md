## molecule

cis-Cinnamoylcocaine, methyl (1R,2R,3S,5S)-8-methyl-3-[(Z)-3-phenylprop-2-enoyl]oxy-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane-2-carboxylate. Formula C19H23NO4, 329.4 g/mol, CAS 50763-21-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the (Z) isomer of cinnamoylcocaine; cinnamoylcocaine (the (E) isomer) has its own sheet and a different InChIKey. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tropane esters, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping, because it is an alkaloid of the coca leaf; it is not an opioid. Typical role in samples: residue of the plant. The effect axis follows cocaine: the pharmacology of the compound itself is not described in the sources consulted. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A minor alkaloid of coca leaf: cis- and trans-cinnamoylcocaine were measured with cocaine in leaf from Bolivia, Peru, Ecuador and Colombia and in cultivated Erythroxylum. Cocaine (see the cocaine sheet) is the main alkaloid of the same leaf. [f:moore1994]

The ratio of the cinnamoylcocaines (with tropacocaine and norcocaine) to cocaine has been used to compare illicit cocaine samples and to judge whether they share an origin. [f:janzen1992]

In oxidised illicit cocaine hydrochloride, cis- and trans-cinnamoylcocaine are the source of diastereomeric diol impurities, which are seen only in exhibits that have been oxidised. [f:casale2007]
