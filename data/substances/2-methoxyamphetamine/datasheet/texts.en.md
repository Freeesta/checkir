## molecule

2-Methoxyamphetamine (2-MA), 1-(2-methoxyphenyl)propan-2-amine. Formula C10H15NO, 165.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the ortho positional isomer of 4-methoxyamphetamine (PMA, own sheet). [f:pubchem]

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]
