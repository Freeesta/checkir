## molecule

25D-NBOMe, 2-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-methylphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethanamine. Formula C19H25NO3, 315.4 g/mol, CAS 1354632-02-2. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the 4-methyl member of the series: 2C-D carrying a 2-methoxybenzyl group on the nitrogen; PubChem also lists it as Cimbi-138. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: NBOMe (N-benzyl phenethylamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:suzuki2015] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The NBOMes were synthesised for research as potent agonists of the 5-HT2A receptor. [f:suzuki2015]

Several NBOMes have been on sale from online vendors since 2010, with numerous cases of toxicity and several deaths. [f:halberstadt2017]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of 2C-D (a phenethylamine of the 2C series). [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In the in vitro model with human liver microsomes, 25D-NBOMe gives 36 phase I metabolites; the main steps are oxidative deamination, oxidative N-dealkylation (also with hydroxylation), oxidative O-demethylation (also with hydroxylation), mono- and dihydroxylation and oxidation of alcohols. [f:grafinger2018]

The matching 2C-D metabolite was found, together with N-oxides and hydroxylamines; the data are in vitro (microsomes and the fungus Cunninghamella elegans), not from human samples. [f:grafinger2018]
