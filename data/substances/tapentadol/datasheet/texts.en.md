## molecule

Tapentadol, 3-[(2R,3R)-1-(dimethylamino)-2-methylpentan-3-yl]phenol. Formula C14H23NO, 221.34 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

The medicine is a Schedule II controlled substance in the United States. [f:nucynta-label]

## origin

A centrally acting, synthetic opioid analgesic. [f:barbosa2016]

## metabolism

About 97% of an oral dose is metabolised, mainly by conjugation with glucuronic acid: about 70% of the dose is excreted in the urine as O-glucuronide (55%) and sulfate (15%), and 3% as unchanged drug. N-desmethyl tapentadol (13%, by CYP2C9 and CYP2C19) and hydroxy tapentadol (2%, by CYP2D6) are also formed; none of the metabolites contributes to the analgesic activity. [f:nucynta-label]

The mean terminal half-life is about 4 hours after oral administration. [f:nucynta-label]
