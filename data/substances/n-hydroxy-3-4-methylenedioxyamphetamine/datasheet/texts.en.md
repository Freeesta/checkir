## molecule

N-Hydroxy-MDA (N-OH-MDA), N-[1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)propan-2-yl]hydroxylamine. Formula C10H13NO3, 195.21 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is MDA with a hydroxyl group on the nitrogen; MDA has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: methylenedioxy compounds. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound. It is also the N-demethylated metabolite of N-hydroxy-MDMA (N-OH-MDMA), a drug that circulated in Japan. [f:tsujikawa2011]

## metabolism

In rats given N-OH-MDMA by mouth, N-OH-MDA appeared as its demethylated metabolite; most N-hydroxylated forms were excreted as glucuronide conjugates, 1.1% of the dose in total, while the main urinary metabolites were MDMA (14.3% of the dose) and MDA (7.7%) within 24 h. [f:tsujikawa2011]
