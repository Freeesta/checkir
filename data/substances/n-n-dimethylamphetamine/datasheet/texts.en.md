## molecule

N,N-Dimethylamphetamine, N,N-dimethyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C11H17N, 163.26 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is methamphetamine with a second methyl group on the nitrogen (a tertiary amine); methamphetamine has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. In samples it is met as an impurity of methamphetamine rather than as an active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, reported as an impurity in methamphetamine made by both the Leuckart and the reductive amination routes; it is not specific to a route. [f:kunalan2009]
