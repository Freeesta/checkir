## metabolism

In human liver microsomes, the four compounds studied (25B-, 25E- and 25H-NBOH and 25iP-NBOMe) gave 70 metabolites in all; the main biotransformations were O-demethylation, hydroxylation, dehydrogenation, loss of the benzyl group, oxidation to ketone and carboxylate, and glucuronidation.
