## molecule

Phenibut (β-phenyl-GABA), 4-amino-3-phenylbutanoic acid. Formula C10H13NO2, 179.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a derivative of γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA), and no family sheet covers GABA derivatives, so none is attached. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (anxiolytic), acting mainly at GABA-B receptors. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:lapin2001] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, developed in the former Soviet Union and introduced into clinical practice in Russia in the 1960s; in the last decade it has been marketed in Europe and the USA as a nootropic. [f:lapin2001] [f:gurley2024]

## metabolism

Data conflict. Soviet studies in the 1960s reported no metabolism and renal excretion of the unchanged drug, but fact sheets of two Russian manufacturers state that up to 95% undergoes hepatic biotransformation to inactive metabolites. After a single 250 mg oral dose in volunteers, 65% was excreted unchanged in urine, with a plasma half-life of 5.3 hours. [f:gurley2024]
