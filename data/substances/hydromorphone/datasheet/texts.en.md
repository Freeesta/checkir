## molecule

Hydromorphone, (4R,4aR,7aR,12bS)-9-hydroxy-3-methyl-1,2,4,4a,5,6,7a,13-octahydro-4,12-methanobenzofuro[3,2-e]isoquinolin-7-one. Formula C17H19NO3, 285.34 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A semisynthetic opiate: the semisynthetic opiate drugs are produced chemically from the natural morphine alkaloids (morphine, codeine and thebaine), and hydromorphone is among them. [f:boonstra2001]

## metabolism

Hydromorphone is metabolised mainly by UGT2B7 to the inactive hydromorphone-3-glucuronide. [f:coates2025] CYP-mediated metabolism is of minor importance; rifampin, an enzyme inducer, raised the glucuronide-to-hydromorphone ratio by 50% after an oral dose and by 42% after an intravenous dose in 12 healthy volunteers. [f:lohela2021]
