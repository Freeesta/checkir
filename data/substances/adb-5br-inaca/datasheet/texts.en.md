## molecule

ADB-5'Br-INACA, (2S)-N-(1-amino-3,3-dimethyl-1-oxobutan-2-yl)-5-bromo-1H-indazole-3-carboxamide: the indazole-3-carboxamide of tert-leucinamide with a bromine on the indazole ring and no alkyl chain on the indazole nitrogen ("tail-less"). Base: C14H17BrN4O2, 353.2 g/mol; PubChem lists no CAS. Identity checked against PubChem (the (S) entry). It is a different compound from the N-butyl analogue ADB-5'Br-BUTINACA. ## origin

A synthetic substance. Tail-less indazole-3-carboxamides such as ADB-INACA are described as precursors that are converted into controlled synthetic cannabinoids and found in mixtures with them; brominated synthetic cannabinoids such as ADB-5'Br-BUTINACA are reported as a ban-evading variant. No source on the use of this specific compound was found. [f:monti2025] [f:deventer2025]

## risks

Mixtures of tail-less precursors with the corresponding final synthetic cannabinoids were found in 28 US forensic casework samples (February 2023 to February 2024) and in 87 samples seized in Scottish prisons (February 2023 to July 2024); the activity of a mixture was set by the concentration of the final compound. [f:monti2025]
