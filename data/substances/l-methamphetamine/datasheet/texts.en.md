## molecule

L-methamphetamine, (2R)-N-methyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C10H15N, 149.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the (R) enantiomer of methamphetamine; the methamphetamine sheet describes the compound without distinguishing the enantiomer. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, the (R) enantiomer of methamphetamine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

For methamphetamine the main routes are aromatic hydroxylation to 4-hydroxymethamphetamine and N-demethylation to amphetamine. [f:schep2010] [f:euda-meth]
