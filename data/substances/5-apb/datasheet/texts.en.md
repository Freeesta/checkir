## molecule

5-APB (5-(2-aminopropyl)benzofuran), 1-(1-benzofuran-5-yl)propan-2-amine. Base: C11H13NO, 175.23 g/mol, CAS 286834-81-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The positional isomer is 6-APB; the N-methyl derivative is 5-MAPB. [f:brandt2020] [f:welter2015]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a benzofuran analogue of amphetamine: 2-aminopropylbenzofurans were sold as analogues of amphetamines. [f:welter2015]
