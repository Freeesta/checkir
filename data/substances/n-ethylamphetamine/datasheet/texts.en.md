## molecule

N-Ethylamphetamine (etilamfetamine), N-ethyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C11H17N, 163.26 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is amphetamine with an ethyl group on the nitrogen, a homologue of methamphetamine, which has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, an N-ethyl analogue of amphetamine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In humans, after oral doses of etilamfetamine the main metabolite in urine was amphetamine; the excretion of both depended on urinary pH, and 22.2 to 54.4% of the dose was excreted unchanged over 72 h. [f:delbeke1986]

In rats the pathway is similar to that of amphetamine and methamphetamine, with p-hydroxyethylamphetamine identified as a novel metabolite. [f:makino1989]
