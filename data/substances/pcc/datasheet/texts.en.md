## molecule

PCC, 1-(piperidin-1-yl)cyclohexane-1-carbonitrile. Formula C12H20N2, 192.30 g/mol, CAS 3867-15-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is an alpha-aminonitrile: a cyclohexane carrying a piperidine ring and a nitrile on the same carbon. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, reported as the major contaminant of illicit phencyclidine (sheet `phencyclidine`). [f:lue1988]

In a smoking simulation with labelled PCC on placebo cigarettes, about 47% of the PCC passed intact into the mainstream smoke and about 58% was cleaved to cyanide and 1-(1-piperidino)-1-cyclohexene; cyanide was detected in all the traps. [f:lue1988]
