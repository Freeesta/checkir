## molecule

4-Bromoamphetamine (para-bromoamphetamine), 1-(4-bromophenyl)propan-2-amine. Formula C9H12BrN, 214.1 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An amphetamine with a bromine atom on position 4 of the phenyl ring; the fluorine analogue is 4-FA (separate sheet). [f:pubchem]
