## molecule

N-Ethylhexedrone (NEH, hexen), 2-(ethylamino)-1-phenylhexan-1-one. Base: C14H21NO, 219.32 g/mol, CAS 802857-66-5. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata]

Hydrochloride: C14H21NO·HCl, 255.78 g/mol, melting point 177.74 °C, white powder, CAS 18410-62-3. The reference spectrum is a diamond ATR one (1 bounce, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-neh]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

N-Ethylhexedrone appeared on the illegal market in late 2015 and, according to UNODC, is among the synthetic cathinones most often seized worldwide. [f:domagalska2021] [f:nunezmontero2025]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-ethyl cathinone with a four-carbon α chain. [f:domagalska2021]

## metabolism

In three patients four metabolites were found in blood and urine: reduced NEH, dealkyl-NEH, reduced dealkyl-NEH and hydroxy-NEH; the elimination half-life was estimated at 19 and 28 h in two of them, and NEH stayed detectable in urine for days. [f:lefeuvre2024]

In volunteers keto reduction, hydroxylation and dealkylation were the common pathways of NEH, with a glucuronide of the parent drug; in mice the most excreted metabolites came from N-dealkylation. [f:massano2024] [f:carrola2020]

## effects

After intranasal self-administration in 8 experienced users, NEH produced psychostimulant-like effects within 20 min, with a return to baseline 3-4 h later. [f:nunezmontero2025]

## doses

The amount self-administered by the participants of the observational study was 20-40 mg intranasally; it is a study dose, not an indication of an amount to take. [f:nunezmontero2025]

## risks

Three patients were admitted to the emergency department; 12-24 h after nasal intake they described anxiety, feelings of persecution, anhedonia, psychomotor slowing and loss of consciousness, two of them needed intubation and ventilation, and a syndrome of inappropriate antidiuretic hormone secretion was diagnosed. [f:lefeuvre2024]

A 21-year-old man died with hyperthermia above 41 °C, tachycardia above 160 beats per minute and acute kidney failure; blood NEH was 145 ng/mL. The white powder found on him had been reported as cocaine hydrochloride by another laboratory. [f:domagalska2021]

## counselling

Ask what the sample was sold as: in the fatal case a powder reported as cocaine hydrochloride turned out to contain NEH; say that what is declared may not be what is inside. [f:domagalska2021]

Ask how the person felt in the 12-24 h after use: anxiety, persecutory thoughts and low mood were described, and NEH stays in the body for days. [f:lefeuvre2024]

Invite not to repeat the intake to chase effects that fade within hours: the UBC guidelines note that early effects can seem mild, and the substance stays in the body much longer than the effects last. [f:nunezmontero2025] [f:lefeuvre2024] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
