## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

In mice 4'-methylacetylfentanyl (10 and 100 mg/kg) decreased locomotion, unlike most of the μ-opioid agonists tested in the same study, and its efficacy was 3.47% of that of fentanyl. [f:varshneya2022]

## risks

The ortho, meta and para isomers of methylacetylfentanyl have been reported to interfere with fentanyl urine drug testing by immunoassay and by LC-MS/MS. [f:barajas2025]

para-Methylacetylfentanyl was one of 11 compounds that cross-reacted in at least one of six ELISA kits tested in whole blood. [f:mastrovito2022]

## interactions

Sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
