## molecule

1P-LSD (1-propionyl-LSD, 1P-LAD), (6aR,9R)-N,N-diethyl-7-methyl-4-propanoyl-6,6a,8,9-tetrahydroindolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide. Base: C23H29N3O2, 379.5 g/mol, CAS 2349358-81-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem] The SWGDRUG monograph describes a tan powder. [f:swgdrug-1plsd]

Bands labelled in the SWGDRUG ATR spectrum of the base: two strong bands in the C=O region, at 1694 and 1635 cm-1 (the propionyl group and the diethylamide), then 1444, 1431, 1400, 1364, 1322, 1269, 1242, 1216, 1152 and 1037 cm-1, and below 800 cm-1 the bands at 769, 755 and 635 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-1plsd] [f:coates2000] [f:smith-amides]

The propionyl group sits on the indole nitrogen, so the indole N-H band of LSD (group range 3380-3490 cm-1) is not expected; the highest band labelled in the SWGDRUG spectrum is at 3115 cm-1. [f:halberstadt2020] [f:coates2000] [f:swgdrug-1plsd]

It has been sold as blotters and as powder; the material characterised was the hemitartrate salt. [f:brandt2016]

## category

Chemical class: lysergamides (family sheet: tryptamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

1-Acetyl-LSD (ALD-52) was developed in the 1950s. 1P-LSD, a closely related non-controlled derivative of LSD, was sold as a 'research chemical' before it was first described in the scientific literature in 2015. [f:brandt2016]

## origin

A synthetic derivative of LSD with a propionyl group on the indole nitrogen; 1-acyl derivatives of this kind (ALD-52, 1P-LSD, 1B-LSD) have appeared as designer drugs. [f:halberstadt2020]

## metabolism

In two volunteers who took 100 µg of 1P-LSD hemitartrate by mouth only LSD was found in serum and urine, with a terminal half-life of about 6.4 hours and an LSD bioavailability close to 100%. After intravenous dosing 1P-LSD was detectable for up to 4.16 hours in serum, while LSD was found in every serum sample. [f:grumann2020]

In rats 1P-LSD is rapidly and efficiently deacylated to LSD. With human liver fractions deacylation to LSD was also seen, together with N-dealkylation and hydroxylation steps catalysed mainly by CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 (nine LSD derivatives studied). [f:halberstadt2020] [f:wagmann2019]

## effects

In two volunteers the psychosensory effects of 1P-LSD and their time course were comparable to those reported for LSD in other studies; 5D-ASC scores were higher after oral than after intravenous administration. [f:grumann2020]

In mice 1P-LSD produced a dose-dependent head-twitch response mediated by the 5-HT2A receptor, with about 38% of the potency of LSD. [f:brandt2016]

In vitro the 1-acyl group lowers the affinity of LSD at 5-HT2A sites by one to two orders of magnitude, yet the compound acts in vivo as a prodrug of LSD. [f:halberstadt2020]

## doses

Dose in the only human study: 100 µg of 1P-LSD hemitartrate, by mouth and by intravenous route, in two volunteers (one controlled self-administration each); no usual range for 1P-LSD was found in the sources read. [f:grumann2020]

## risks

As a prodrug of LSD, 1P-LSD is expected to share the risks of classical hallucinogens: they are relatively well tolerated physiologically, but the most likely risk is overwhelming distress (a 'bad trip') that can lead to dangerous behaviour. [f:grumann2020] [f:johnson2008]

Hallucinogen persisting perception disorder (HPPD) is rarely encountered clinically; it takes two forms, brief flashbacks and a chronic form lasting months to years. [f:halpern2018]

A 2026 in silico study predicted toxicity parameters for 1P-LSD and four related lysergamides and notes that their toxicological profiles remain largely unexplored; these are model predictions, not observations in people. [f:jurowski2026]

## counselling

Say why identity matters: 1P-LSD is sold on blotters like LSD and acts in the body as a prodrug of LSD, and the UBC guidelines recommend analysing the dose to know whether it is LSD, an NBOMe, DOC or something else. [f:brandt2016] [f:grumann2020] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Say that in two volunteers the effects and their time course were comparable to LSD, and that the UBC guidelines advise against raising the dose when the usual effect does not come, because tolerance builds up quickly with repeated doses in a short time. [f:grumann2020] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about mood, health and surroundings: the most likely risk of classical hallucinogens is overwhelming distress, and the UBC guidelines tie it to set and setting and advise people with mental illness or heart and circulation problems to abstain. [f:johnson2008] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
