## molecule

MBDB, 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-N-methylbutan-2-amine. Formula C12H17NO2, 207.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Compared with MDMA it has an ethyl group in place of the methyl on the alpha carbon (butanamine instead of propanamine); MDMA has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: methylenedioxy. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, reported as a street drug in Japan; the abused substance is racemic. [f:nagai2002]

## metabolism

N-demethylation gives 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-2-butanamine (BDB), found in the urine of rats and of one volunteer after oral MBDB; in urine, saliva and sweat MBDB is more concentrated than the metabolite. [f:kintz1997] [f:nagai2002]

In one subject urine tested positive for 36 h after one oral dose, with a peak at 4 h. [f:kintz1997]

In rats 34.7% of the dose was excreted as MBDB within 24 h. [f:nagai2002]
