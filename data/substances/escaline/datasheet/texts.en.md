## molecule

Escaline (4-ethoxy-3,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine), 2-(4-ethoxy-3,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethanamine. Formula C12H19NO3, 225.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The ring carries methoxy groups at positions 3 and 5 as in mescaline and an ethoxy group at position 4 instead of the methoxy. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a mescaline analogue with the 4-methoxy replaced by an ethoxy group. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]
