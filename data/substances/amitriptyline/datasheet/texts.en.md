## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a dibenzocycloheptadiene derivative marketed as a tricyclic antidepressant in tablets. [f:amitriptyline-label]

## metabolism

Amitriptyline is metabolized by N-demethylation (to nortriptyline, itself a tricyclic antidepressant) and by bridge hydroxylation; virtually the whole dose is excreted as glucuronide or sulfate conjugates, with little unchanged drug in the urine. [f:amitriptyline-label] [f:hicks2017]

Exposure to amitriptyline depends on both CYP2D6 and CYP2C19 genotypes; that of nortriptyline on CYP2D6 only. [f:hicks2017]
