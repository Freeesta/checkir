## molecule

Butorphanol, (1S,9R,10S)-17-(cyclobutylmethyl)-17-azatetracyclo[7.5.3.01,10.02,7]heptadeca-2(7),3,5-triene-4,10-diol. Formula C21H29NO2, 327.5 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a synthetic opioid agonist-antagonist analgesic, so it is not placed among the morphinans (morphine and its semi-synthetic derivatives) and no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:gillis1995] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic opioid agonist-antagonist analgesic, in clinical use since the launch of the parenteral formulation in 1978. [f:gillis1995]

## metabolism

Butorphanol is metabolised to hydroxybutorphanol and norbutorphanol. In nine volunteers given 1 mg nasally, conjugated metabolites were not detected in plasma and only trace norbutorphanol was present; hydroxybutorphanol had a half-life of about 15 h. [f:vachharajani1997]

After oral administration butorphanol undergoes significant first-pass metabolism. [f:gillis1995]
