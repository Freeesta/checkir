## molecule

2C-B-FLY, 2-(4-bromo-2,3,6,7-tetrahydrofuro[2,3-f][1]benzofuran-8-yl)ethanamine. Formula C12H14BrNO2, 284.15 g/mol, CAS 178557-21-6. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: 2C series (by analogy with 2C-B). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

In the mouse head-twitch response (a 5-HT2A-mediated behaviour) 2C-B-FLY is active with a potency similar to 2C-B. [f:halberstadt2019]

## origin

A synthetic substance: a rigid analogue of 2C-B (a tetrahydrobenzodifuran) in which the two methoxy groups are incorporated into dihydrofuran rings. [f:halberstadt2019] [f:pubchem]
