## risks

Physiological dependence on benzodiazepines comes with a withdrawal syndrome of sleep disturbance, irritability, anxiety, tremor, sweating and palpitations; seizures and psychotic reactions are also reported at high doses. Withdrawal appears more severe after high doses or after short-acting benzodiazepines. [f:petursson1994]

## interactions

Concurrent use of an opioid and a benzodiazepine is associated with a higher risk of an emergency room visit or hospital admission for opioid overdose (adjusted odds ratio 2.14, 95% confidence interval 2.05-2.24; privately insured US patients 2001-2013, retrospective study: an association, not proof of causation). [f:sun2017]

## counselling

Ask whether the person also takes opioids or heroin: the UBC guidelines say benzodiazepines can depress breathing and that the risk of respiratory arrest rises with opiates; the combination with opioids is linked to more overdose visits. [f:sun2017] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask how long the person has used it and whether they want to stop: after prolonged use abrupt discontinuation exposes them to withdrawal, more severe with high doses or short-acting benzodiazepines. [f:petursson1994]
