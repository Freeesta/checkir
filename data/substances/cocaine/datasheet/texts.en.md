## molecule

Cocaine, methyl (1R,2R,3S,5S)-3-(benzoyloxy)-8-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane-2-carboxylate. Base: C17H21NO4, 303.35 g/mol, CAS 50-36-2. Hydrochloride: C17H21NO4·HCl, 339.81 g/mol, CAS 53-21-4. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

Forms: found as the hydrochloride powder and as the free base ("crack"), derived from the hydrochloride. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

Diagnostic bands (ATR): the hydrochloride has the C=O doublet at ~1728 (methyl ester) and ~1712 cm-1 (benzoate, conjugated), the C-O-C bands at ~1265 and ~1105 cm-1, the out-of-plane band of the monosubstituted ring at ~729 cm-1 and a broad, weak N-H+ envelope near 2540 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-cocaine] [f:rodrigues2013] [f:smith-arom-esters] [f:smith-amine-salts] Assigning the two C=O to the two esters is a proposal to be confirmed. [f:swgdrug-cocaine]

Salt or base: the base has no N-H+ envelope, the C=O doublet widens (~1734 and ~1707 cm-1), the C-O-C bands move up (~1273 and ~1108 cm-1) and the ring band moves down to ~712 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-cocaine] [f:rodrigues2013] [f:mendez-hernandez2020] Principal peaks in KBr, in order of intensity: base 1275, 1700, 1106, 1728, 710, 1040, 1280 cm-1; hydrochloride 1712, 1730, 1276, 1230 (side peak), 732, 1106, 1075, 1025 cm-1. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

Analogues and impurities: cis- and trans-cinnamoylcocaine come with cocaine; the trans isomer has a large band at 1320 cm-1 that the cis isomer lacks, and a band at 1625 cm-1 about as strong as the C=O bands. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

## category

Chemical class: tropane ester, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Albert Niemann isolated cocaine from coca leaves in 1859-1860; it was used in toothache drops, tonics and the original Coca-Cola, and is obsolete as a local anaesthetic in most countries (topical use persists, for example in endoscopic sinus surgery). It is controlled under the 1961 Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs, was placed in US Schedule II in 1970 and in UK Class A in 1971. [f:roque-bravo2022]

## origin

Natural alkaloid found in the leaves of Erythroxylum coca; coca paste is an intermediate. In seized material cocaine comes with cis- and trans-cinnamoylcocaine, leaf alkaloids that leave marks in the IR spectrum. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

## metabolism

Human kinetics (radiolabelled cocaine): absorption half-time 1.1 min smoked and 11.7 min intranasal; intranasal bioavailability 80%; distribution half-life 11 min, elimination half-life 78 min intravenous; low protein binding; volume of distribution 2.70 L/kg. [f:jeffcoat1989] Esterases give benzoylecgonine (CES1) and ecgonine methyl ester (BChE, CES2), both inactive; CYP3A4 N-demethylates a part to norcocaine, active and hepatotoxic. The mean half-life is 40-90 minutes. [f:roque-bravo2022]

In urine benzoylecgonine is 39%, 30% and 16% of the dose after intravenous, intranasal and smoked use; cocaine falls below ~1 ng/mL within 24 h; smoking adds anhydroecgonine methyl ester. [f:cone1998] In chronic users benzoylecgonine fell below 300 ng/mL in urine for the first time at 43.6 ± 17.1 h (16-66 h). [f:preston2002]

With alcohol, 17 ± 6% of an intravenous dose becomes cocaethylene (active, half-life 148 ± 15 min), a marker of combined use. [f:harris2003] [f:roque-bravo2022]

## cuts

Cocaine commonly contains levamisole (and/or tetramisole), phenacetin, lidocaine, caffeine, diltiazem, hydroxyzine, procaine, benzocaine and sugars (mannitol, lactose, glucose). [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

Procaine and benzocaine are aromatic esters too, with a C=O in the same region: an ester C=O alone does not point to cocaine. [f:smith-arom-esters]

## effects

Cocaine blocks the reuptake transporters of dopamine, noradrenaline and serotonin: dopamine drives the euphoria, noradrenaline the sympathomimetic and vasoconstrictor effects; it also blocks voltage-gated sodium channels (local anaesthesia). [f:roque-bravo2022] In humans (PET) intravenous 0.3-0.6 mg/kg blocked 60-77% of dopamine transporters; at least 47% was needed to perceive the effect. [f:volkow1997]

By inhalation the peak comes within 1-3 minutes and the effect lasts 5-15 minutes; intranasal 15-30 minutes; oral 1-2 hours. When it ends, insomnia, anxiety, irritability and dysphoria may follow. [f:roque-bravo2022]

At constant blood levels the euphoria peaks at about 1 hour and fades by 4 hours; the heart-rate effect peaks within 10 minutes and then falls with a half-life of ~31 min toward a plateau. [f:ambre1988]

## doses

A «line» of hydrochloride contains 20-50 mg. Intranasal bioavailability is about 80% (over 90% by inhalation); because of vasoconstriction the intranasal plasma peak is delayed (about 60 minutes). [f:roque-bravo2022]

In controlled studies intranasal doses of 0.06-1.37 mg/kg gave dose-related rises in heart rate, blood pressure and positive effects; a second dose 40 minutes later roughly doubled plasma levels without raising the effects (acute tolerance). [f:foltin2004]

## risks

Cardiovascular complications are adrenergic: acute coronary syndromes, aortic dissection, sudden cardiac death; alcohol and smoking add to the cardiotoxicity. [f:afonso2007] Acute effects include tachycardia, hypertension, hyperthermia, seizures and stroke, even after one dose; blood levels do not reliably predict toxicity. [f:roque-bravo2022]

Norcocaine, an active metabolite, is hepatotoxic. Levamisole used as a cut is associated with vasculopathy and vasculitis with autoimmune manifestations. [f:roque-bravo2022] [f:iorio2024]

## interactions

With alcohol, cocaethylene forms: plasma cocaine rises, euphoria is enhanced and prolonged, heart rate is higher and subjects report more total intoxication. [f:harris2003] [f:mccance-katz1993] [f:cami1998]

Opioids (as in the «speedball») compete with cocaine for carboxylesterases; in Europe opioids are the substance most often found in cocaine-related deaths, and cocaine does not reverse the brain hypoxia of a high heroin dose. [f:roque-bravo2022]

## counselling

Ask whether alcohol is planned: it produces cocaethylene, active and longer lasting, and co-use prolongs the effect. [f:harris2003] [f:cami1998] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about other substances, opioids above all: in Europe they are the substance most often found in cocaine-related deaths. [f:roque-bravo2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Redosing: a second dose roughly doubles blood levels without raising the effect; agree on a limit beforehand. [f:foltin2004] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Prepare for the comedown: insomnia, anxiety and dysphoria are possible, so plan where and how to rest. [f:roque-bravo2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

If the result points to levamisole, say so: vasculitis is possible; new or painful skin lesions are a reason to see a doctor. [f:iorio2024] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
