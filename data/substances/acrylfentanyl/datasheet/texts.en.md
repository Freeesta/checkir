## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

Acryl fentanyl had sub-nanomolar affinity for the human μ-opioid receptor and acted as an agonist in vitro; its effects in mice were blocked by naltrexone and resembled those of morphine. [f:obeng2025]

## doses

Among amide-modified fentanyls only acrylfentanyl and 2-furanylfentanyl approach fentanyl in μ-opioid potency and reinforcing efficacy in animal studies, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:canfield2026]

## risks

In whole blood a fentanyl ELISA read acryl fentanyl at 164% of the response to fentanyl at the concentrations tested. [f:cieri2024]

## interactions

Sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
