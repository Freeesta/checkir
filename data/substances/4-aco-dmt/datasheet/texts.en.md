## molecule

4-AcO-DMT (psilacetin, O-acetylpsilocin, 4-acetoxy-N,N-dimethyltryptamine), 3-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl acetate. Base: C14H18N2O2, 246.3 g/mol, CAS 92292-84-7. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem] Oxalate: C14H18N2O2·C2H2O4, 336 g/mol, melting point 144.2 °C; the SWGDRUG monograph describes an off-white powder (oxalate). [f:swgdrug-4acodmt]

Bands of the oxalate (ATR) labelled in the SWGDRUG spectrum: C=O of the acetyl ester at 1753 cm-1, then 1620, 1495, 1460, 1435 and 1365 cm-1, a strong band at 1194 cm-1, and the bands at 1030, 931, 903, 868, 835, 777, 700, 600 and 561 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-4acodmt] [f:coates2000]

The SWGDRUG spectrum is that of the oxalate, while psilacetin fumarate has been used in studies, so the salt form of a sample may differ from the reference. [f:swgdrug-4acodmt] [f:jones2023]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

4-Acetoxy analogues of psilocybin such as 4-AcO-DMT have appeared as new designer drugs; in 2021 almost nothing was known about their pharmacological effects. [f:klein2021]

Psilacetin had long been suggested as an alternative prodrug of psilocin, but direct in vivo support was lacking until a 2023 mouse study. [f:jones2023]

In 2025 six of eight 'mushroom' gummies bought in Portland, Oregon contained 4-AcO-DMT, and seven contained psilocin. [f:correia2025]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine, known as O-acetylpsilocin because the acetyl group sits on the 4-hydroxy group of psilocin. [f:zhai2022] [f:klein2021]

## metabolism

In mice psilacetin acts as a prodrug of psilocin: after an equimolar dose psilocin exposure from psilacetin fumarate was about 70% of that from psilocybin, and the half-life of psilocin was about 30 minutes from either source. [f:jones2023]

With pooled human liver microsomes 15 metabolites formed (12 phase I, 3 phase II); hydrolysis gave the most abundant one, followed by hydroxylation, N-demethylation, oxidation and glucuronidation, and the β-hydroxylation product is proposed as a biomarker. [f:zhai2022]

## effects

In the cell and mouse experiments 17 tryptamines with a 4-hydroxy or 4-acetoxy group, 4-AcO-DMT among them, acted as full or partial 5-HT2 agonists and all induced head twitches in mice, an LSD-like behavioural profile consistent with psilocybin-like properties. [f:klein2021]

O-acetylation lowered the in vitro 5-HT2A potency of the 4-hydroxy compounds about 10- to 20-fold but changed head-twitch potency little, which suggests deacetylation in vivo. [f:klein2021]

## risks

The tryptamine derivatives studied have psilocybin-like pharmacological properties, which supports their classification as psychedelic drugs. [f:klein2021]

For classical hallucinogens the most likely risk is overwhelming distress (a 'bad trip') that can lead to dangerous behaviour; prolonged psychoses are less common. [f:johnson2008]

Labelling of the gummies analysed in Portland was inaccurate in many products: some that claimed Amanita muscaria extracts contained serotonergic tryptamines, and one labelled psilocybin-free contained psilocybin. [f:correia2025]

## counselling

Say what is known about identity: in Portland six of eight 'mushroom' gummies contained 4-AcO-DMT and labelling was often wrong, so the product name does not tell which tryptamine is in it. [f:correia2025]

Ask about state of mind and company: the UBC guidelines for psilocybin advise using only after enough sleep and in good physical and mental health, in a place where one feels protected, not alone and never with alcohol; 4-AcO-DMT has psilocybin-like pharmacology. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines] [f:klein2021]

Say that dose equivalence with psilocybin is not established in people: in mice an equimolar dose gave about 70% of the psilocin exposure. [f:jones2023]
