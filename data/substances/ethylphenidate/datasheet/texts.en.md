## molecule

Ethylphenidate, ethyl 2-phenyl-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Base: C15H21NO2, 247.33 g/mol, CAS 57413-43-1. Identity checked on PubChem; the entry has unspecified stereocentres. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: fenidati. Expected effect: stimolante. Typical role in samples: principio attivo. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, the ethyl ester analogue of methylphenidate. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

It also forms in the body when methylphenidate and ethanol are taken together, by carboxylesterase-mediated transesterification. [f:dinisoliveira2017]

## metabolism

In horses given ethylphenidate intravenously or orally, the metabolite ritalinic acid was measured in addition to the parent compound, in serum and urine for up to 72 hours; no human study is cited here. [f:knych2018]
