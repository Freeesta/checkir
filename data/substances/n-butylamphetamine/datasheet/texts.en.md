## molecule

N-butylamphetamine, N-(1-phenylpropan-2-yl)butan-1-amine. Formula C13H21N, 191.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is amphetamine with a butyl group on the nitrogen; amphetamine has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, an N-alkylated amphetamine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

With human CYP2D6 in vitro the only metabolite formed is N-butylamphetamine hydroxylated at position 4 of the ring, at a low rate; no N-dealkylation was observed. [f:bach1999]
