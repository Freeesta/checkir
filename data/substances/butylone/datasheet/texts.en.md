## molecule

Butylone (bk-MBDB), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-(methylamino)butan-1-one. Base: C12H15NO3, 221.25 g/mol, CAS 802575-11-7. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata]

Hydrochloride: C12H15NO3·HCl, 257 g/mol, melting point 243.6 °C, white powder; the monograph gives CAS 17762-90-2. The reference spectrum is an ATR one (32 scans, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-butylone]

Butylone is a methylone derivative with a longer α-carbon chain. [f:saha2019]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones. Expected effect: stimulant, entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The first reported death after ingestion of butylone, together with methylone, dates from 2012; the capsule had been sold as "Ecstasy" and the drugs were marketed as "legal highs" labelled "not for human consumption". [f:warrick2012]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a ring-substituted cathinone with a methylenedioxy group. [f:saha2019]

## metabolism

In human urine the main pathways were N-dealkylation, demethylenation followed by O-methylation and reduction of the beta-ketone; the 4-hydroxy-3-methoxy metabolites were the main ones and exist mostly as conjugates. [f:zaitsu2009]

## effects

In vitro butylone inhibits the uptake of dopamine, noradrenaline and serotonin without selectivity, like cocaine, and it also releases 5-HT, like MDMA. [f:simmler2013]

In rats butylone blocks the dopamine transporter but is a substrate of the serotonin transporter; intravenous doses of 1 and 3 mg/kg raised extracellular monoamines and motor activity. [f:saha2019]

In mice the hyperlocomotion caused by butylone was prevented by ketanserin or haloperidol, so it depends on 5-HT2A receptors and on dopamine. [f:lopezarnau2012]

## risks

A 24-year-old woman took a capsule sold as "Ecstasy" that contained only methylone and butylone: she arrived comatose, febrile, tachycardic, hypertensive, tremulous, hyperreflexic and with sustained clonus, and died of multi-organ failure despite cooling and supportive care. [f:warrick2012]

In primary rat hepatocytes butylone was cytotoxic (EC50 1.21 mM) and liver metabolism increased its toxicity. [f:roquebravo2021]

## counselling

Ask what the sample was sold as: the fatal case took butylone and methylone sold as "Ecstasy"; say that what is declared may not be what is inside. [f:warrick2012]

Signs to recognise: high temperature, fast heartbeat, tremor, stiff or jerking muscles, coma; the UBC guidelines say to cool the person and seek help for hyperthermia. [f:warrick2012] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about MDMA, other stimulants and alcohol on the same night: butylone also releases serotonin like MDMA, and the UBC guidelines advise against mixing stimulants and against alcohol. [f:simmler2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
