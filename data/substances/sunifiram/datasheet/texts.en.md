## molecule

Sunifiram (DM235), 1-(4-benzoylpiperazin-1-yl)propan-1-one. Formula C14H18N2O2, 246.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a piperazine-based cognition enhancer (nootropic) of the piracetam type, and no family sheet covers it. checkir has no class for cognition enhancers, so the effect class is a placeholder, not a statement. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:gualtieri2015] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound identified by an academic research group (University of Florence) as a potent cognition enhancer; it was not patented and has been offered by dozens of websites. [f:gualtieri2015]
