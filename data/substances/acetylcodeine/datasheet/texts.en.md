## molecule

Acetylcodeine (6-acetylcodeine), [(4R,4aR,7S,7aR,12bS)-9-methoxy-3-methyl-2,4,4a,7,7a,13-hexahydro-1H-4,12-methanobenzofuro[3,2-e]isoquinolin-7-yl] acetate. Formula C20H23NO4, 341.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the acetate ester of codeine at the 6-hydroxyl group. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue (an impurity of illicitly manufactured heroin). This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:oneal2001] [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

An impurity of illicitly manufactured heroin; it is one of the major impurities found in it and a marker of street heroin. [f:oneal2001] [f:maas2017]
