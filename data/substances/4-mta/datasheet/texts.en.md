## molecule

4-MTA (4-methylthioamphetamine), 1-(4-methylsulfanylphenyl)propan-2-amine. Formula C10H15NS, 181.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An amphetamine with a methylthio group on position 4 of the ring; it is the sulfur analogue of PMA (separate sheet). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a para-substituted amphetamine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver fractions 4-MTA, together with six other phenethylamines, was metabolised mainly by CYP enzymes: the main route of the group was O-dealkylation, followed by N-dealkylation, oxidation and, to a small extent, deamination, with almost no phase II metabolism and at a slow rate. [f:lai2015]
