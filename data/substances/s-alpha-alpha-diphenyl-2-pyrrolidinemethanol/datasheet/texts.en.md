## molecule

(S)-Diphenyl(pyrrolidin-2-yl)methanol, the (S) enantiomer of diphenylprolinol (D2PM). Formula C17H19NO, 253.34 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem (CID 2724899). [f:pubchem]

The PubChem entry of D2PM itself (CID 204386) has no defined stereochemistry; this sheet follows the (S) form. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a pyrrolidine with two phenyl rings on a carbinol carbon. It has been described as an emerging drug of abuse sold as a "legal high". [f:pubchem] [f:meyer2013]

## metabolism

In rat urine, five phase I and two phase II metabolites were identified; hydroxylation at the pyrrolidine and at the diphenyl part were the main metabolic steps. No human data are reported there. [f:meyer2013]
