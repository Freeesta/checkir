## molecule

Clobenzorex, (2S)-N-[(2-chlorophenyl)methyl]-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C16H18ClN, 259.8 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the S enantiomer of amphetamine (dextroamphetamine, own sheet) with a 2-chlorobenzyl group on the nitrogen. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, an anorectic drug that the body metabolizes to d-amphetamine. [f:baden1999]

## metabolism

Clobenzorex is metabolized to d-amphetamine, which is excreted in urine and can make an amphetamine test positive. [f:baden1999]

In 5 subjects taking 30 mg daily for 7 days, urinary amphetamine peaked at about 2900-4700 ng/mL, far above the parent drug (about 8-47 ng/mL, not always detectable). [f:baden1999]

The hydroxylated metabolite 4-hydroxyclobenzorex is excreted at higher levels than amphetamine and for longer than the parent: after a single 30 mg dose it was found in every sample with amphetamine at or above 500 ng/mL, so it separates clobenzorex use from amphetamine use. [f:valtier2000]
