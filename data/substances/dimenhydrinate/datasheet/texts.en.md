## molecule

Dimenhydrinate, 2-benzhydryloxy-N,N-dimethylethanamine;8-chloro-1,3-dimethyl-7H-purine-2,6-dione. Formula C24H28ClN5O3, 470.0 g/mol, CAS 523-87-5. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. It is the salt of diphenhydramine and 8-chlorotheophylline (see the diphenhydramine sheet). The effect axis has no antihistamine class: "depressant/sedative" follows diphenhydramine and is a checkir proposal awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:ehling2019] [f:akutsu2006] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic molecular compound of diphenhydramine and 8-chlorotheophylline, a chlorinated xanthine; the two components are identified with the diphenhydramine sheet and the PubChem entry. [f:ehling2019] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

No human study of dimenhydrinate was found: for the metabolism of the diphenhydramine part see the diphenhydramine sheet, where N-demethylation by CYP2D6 is the main route. [f:akutsu2006]

In a pilot study in six dogs, oral dimenhydrinate gave a systemic availability of diphenhydramine of 22.0%, against 7.8% for oral diphenhydramine; the terminal half-lives were 11.6 and 5.0 hours. These are animal data. [f:ehling2019]
