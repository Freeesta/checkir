## molecule

Cyclobenzaprine, N,N-dimethyl-3-(2-tricyclo[9.4.0.03,8]pentadeca-1(15),3,5,7,9,11,13-heptaenylidene)propan-1-amine. Formula C20H21N, 275.4 g/mol, CAS 303-53-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

## origin

A synthetic tricyclic compound, marketed as tablets for the relief of muscle spasm associated with acute, painful musculoskeletal conditions. [f:cyclobenzaprine-label]

## metabolism

Cyclobenzaprine is extensively metabolized and excreted primarily as glucuronides via the kidney. CYP3A4, CYP1A2 and, to a lesser extent, CYP2D6 mediate N-demethylation, one of its oxidative pathways. [f:cyclobenzaprine-label]

The effective half-life is 18 hours (range 8 to 37 hours) and the mean oral bioavailability is estimated at 33% to 55%. [f:cyclobenzaprine-label]
