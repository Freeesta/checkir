## molecule

2C-B, 2-(4-bromo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethan-1-amine. Base: C10H14BrNO2, 260.13 g/mol, CAS 66142-81-2. Hydrochloride: C10H14BrNO2·HCl, 296.59 g/mol, CAS 56281-37-9. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata, ChEBI and the SWGDRUG monograph. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi] [f:swgdrug-2cb]

Diagnostic bands of the hydrochloride (ATR): C-H, including the OCH3 groups, at 2937, 2897 and 2839 cm-1 over the NH3+ envelope; ring at 1605, 1498 and 1489 cm-1; 1389 cm-1; asymmetric aryl C-O-C at 1209 cm-1 (the strongest band) and symmetric at 1049 and 1028 cm-1; then 847, 775 and 706 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-2cb] [f:smith-ethers] [f:smith-amine-salts]

Analogues: the SWGDRUG monograph notes that, with the parameters used, the IR spectrum does not tell 2C-B from 2C-I. [f:swgdrug-2cb] The other 2,5-dimethoxy phenethylamines of the 2C series have the same aryl ether bands: the 1209 cm-1 band alone points to the group, not to the substance. [f:smith-ethers]
