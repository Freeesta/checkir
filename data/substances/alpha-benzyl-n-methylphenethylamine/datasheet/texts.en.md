## molecule

alpha-Benzyl-N-methylphenethylamine (BNMPA), N-methyl-1,3-diphenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C16H19N, 225.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A phenethylamine with a benzyl group on the carbon that bears the nitrogen and a methyl on the nitrogen; methamphetamine (own sheet) has a methyl on that carbon instead of the benzyl. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound formed as an impurity during the illicit synthesis of methamphetamine (own sheet); it was proposed as a marker of the use of illicitly synthesized methamphetamine. [f:moore1995a]

## metabolism

In a volunteer who took 5 mg, the main urinary metabolites after hydrolysis were the phenyl-hydroxylated BNMPA and the phenyl-hydroxylated N-demethyl compound, with maximum excretion at 4 h; N-demethyl BNMPA and 1,3-diphenyl-2-propanone (DP2P, maximum at 2 h) were minor. Metabolites stayed above the detection limit up to 21 h. [f:moore1995b]

In 2 of 80 urine specimens from methamphetamine users BNMPA or its metabolites were detectable, at trace level or at about 0.03-0.04 mg/L. [f:moore1996]
