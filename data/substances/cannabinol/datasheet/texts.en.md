## origin

Phytocannabinoid of Cannabis sativa; in cannabis it is an oxidation product of THC, and its presence is a sign of long-term storage and THC degradation. [f:tsumura2012]

It is also formed by thermal oxidation of Δ9-THC when vaping in a laboratory e-cigarette. [f:kim2025-vaping]

## effects

CBN is one of the seven most thoroughly investigated phytocannabinoids in molecular pharmacology; its targets lie both within and beyond the endocannabinoid system. [f:turner2017]

Among phytocannabinoids other than THC and CBD, cannabichromene, cannabigerol and cannabinol have shown intriguing pharmacological effects. [f:blebea2024]
