## molecule

TFMPP, 1-[3-(trifluoromethyl)phenyl]piperazine. Formula C11H13F3N2, 230.23 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a phenylpiperazine, while BZP (benzylpiperazine) belongs to the other class of piperazine designer drugs; BZP has its own sheet. [f:arbo2012]

## category

Chemical class: piperazines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound with serotonergic properties; piperazine designer drugs have been sold as ecstasy pills and under names such as "Legal X" and "Legal E". [f:staack2004] [f:arbo2012]

## metabolism

TFMPP is hydroxylated on the aromatic ring; in human cDNA-expressed enzymes CYP2D6, CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 catalysed the reaction, CYP2D6 accounting for about 81% of the net intrinsic clearance. [f:staack2004]

In human liver microsomes with a CYP2D6 poor-metaboliser genotype the metabolite formation was 63% lower than in pooled microsomes. [f:staack2004]
