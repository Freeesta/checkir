## molecule

4,4'-DMAR (4,4'-dimethylaminorex, para-methyl-4-methylaminorex), 4-methyl-5-(4-methylphenyl)-4,5-dihydro-1,3-oxazol-2-amine. Base: C11H14N2O, 190.25 g/mol, CAS 1445569-01-6. Identity checked on PubChem (flat structure, no stereochemistry). [f:pubchem]

It has two chiral centres, so several stereoisomers. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: Other substances. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a methylated derivative of aminorex and 4-methylaminorex. [f:rickli2019]

## metabolism

In rats (10 mg/kg intraperitoneally) four metabolites were identified in plasma and brain, with low brain permeability and very low brain concentrations. [f:lucchetti2017]

The half-life of the parent compound in rats was about 50 min; the metabolite from oxidation of a methyl group (M2) persisted in plasma longer and at higher concentrations, and could be used to evaluate intake in humans. [f:lucchetti2017]
