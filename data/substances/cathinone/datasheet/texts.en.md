## molecule

Cathinone ((S)-cathinone), (2S)-2-amino-1-phenylpropan-1-one. Base: C9H11NO, 149.19 g/mol, CAS 71031-15-7. Identity checked against PubChem (stereochemistry specified: 2S). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Khat, the leaves of Catha edulis, is chewed as a stimulant in eastern and southern Africa and on the Arabian Peninsula; a review reports that over 20 million people use it daily. [f:patel2015] Its effects are attributed to cathinone, cathine and norephedrine. [f:atlabachew2016]

## origin

A natural substance: an alkaloid of khat (Catha edulis). Synthetic cathinones are drugs structurally related to natural cathinone. [f:atlabachew2016] [f:carrola2020]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes and in rat urine several metabolites were common to khat (cathinone) and pseudoephedrine; in both models the unchanged compound was the main species in urine, so the whole parent-metabolite pattern must be read, and cathine alone cannot serve as a specific marker of khat use. [f:park2026]

Cathinone is also a metabolite of amfepramone (diethylpropion) and was measured in the hair of its users. [f:yang2022]
