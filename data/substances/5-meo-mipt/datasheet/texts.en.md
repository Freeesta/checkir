## molecule

5-MeO-MiPT, N-[2-(5-methoxy-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Base: C15H22N2O, 246.35 g/mol, CAS 96096-55-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: 5-methoxytryptamine with a methyl and an isopropyl group on the amine nitrogen. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes nine metabolites formed; the main phase I steps were N-demethylation, 5-O-demethylation, N-depropylation, N-oxidation and indole hydroxylation, followed by glucoside conjugation. [f:zhao2024]

The N-demethylated metabolite (5-MeO-NiPT) and an indole-hydroxylated one were proposed as markers of intake. [f:zhao2024]
