## molecule

Yangonin, 4-methoxy-6-[(E)-2-(4-methoxyphenyl)ethenyl]pyran-2-one. Formula C15H14O4, 258.3 g/mol, CAS 500-62-9. Identity checked on PubChem. Achiral; the double bond has E configuration. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances; a kavalactone (styrylpyrone) of kava. Expected effect: depressant / sedative, proposed from the effects reported for kava (sedation, euphoria, impairment of motor coordination), not measured for yangonin alone. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:berry2019] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A natural substance: a kavalactone of kava (Piper methysticum) root; yangonin is enriched in ethanol extracts of the root and rhizome compared with water extracts. [f:daniels2026] [f:pubchem]
