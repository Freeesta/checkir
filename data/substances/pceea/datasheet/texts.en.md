## molecule

PCEEA, N-(2-ethoxyethyl)-1-phenylcyclohexan-1-amine (N-(1-phenylcyclohexyl)-2-ethoxyethanamine). Base: C16H25NO, 247.4 g/mol, CAS 1072895-05-6. Identity checked against PubChem (one entry, no stereocentres). It is an isomer of PCMPA (N-(1-phenylcyclohexyl)-3-methoxypropanamine), which needs chromatographic separation. [f:pubchem] [f:song2025]

## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a phencyclidine-derived designer drug: a 1-phenylcyclohexylamine carrying an N-(2-ethoxyethyl) group. [f:sauer2008-pceea]

## metabolism

In rat urine PCEEA was transformed by N-dealkylation and O-dealkylation, partly followed by oxidation of the resulting alcohol to the carboxylic acid, and by hydroxylation of the cyclohexyl ring; aromatic hydroxylation of the O-dealkylated metabolites also occurred, and all metabolites were partly excreted in conjugated form. [f:sauer2008-pceea]

With cDNA-expressed human CYPs, O-deethylation of PCEEA was catalysed by CYP2B6, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP3A4, which accounted for 53%, 25%, 4% and 18% of net clearance respectively. [f:sauer2009-pceea]

## effects

Phencyclidine, ketamine and their analogues act as non-competitive antagonists of the NMDA receptor; methoxetamine, for example, has properties similar to those of ketamine and phencyclidine. [f:zanda2016] [f:bey2007]

## risks

Ketamine analogues are increasingly detected on drug markets; cases reported in France were mostly neurological and psychiatric, 61 % serious, and their risks may differ from or exceed those of ketamine. [f:mezaache2026]

Users may be exposed without knowing, when an analogue is sold as another substance such as a synthetic cathinone, cocaine or ketamine. [f:mezaache2026]

## interactions

For ketamine, serious interactions are reported with central nervous system depressants (ethanol, opioids, benzodiazepines); the sources reviewed do not establish whether the same holds for each analogue. [f:kalsi2011]
