## molecule

U-47700, 3,4-dichloro-N-[(1R,2R)-2-(dimethylamino)cyclohexyl]-N-methylbenzamide. Base: C16H22Cl2N2O, 329.3 g/mol. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is a non-fentanyl synthetic opioid, one of the «U compounds». [f:giorgetti2024]

## history

It was discovered by the Upjohn company in the late 1970s. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

It has been found in counterfeit oxycodone tablets. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

In Finland the first mention on a forum is from March 2015; the substance was put under national control in November 2016 and the last fatality was in 2017. [f:kriikku2019]

It is now Schedule I in the United States and similarly controlled around the world. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

## origin

It is a synthetic opioid developed by Upjohn; it is not a natural product. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

## metabolism

The main metabolite is N-desmethyl-U-47700; N,N-bisdesmethyl-U-47700 is also known. [f:nordmeier2022]

In pigs the brain concentration of N-desmethyl-U-47700 was about six times that of the parent compound. [f:nordmeier2022]

In vitro the two metabolites activate the μ receptor less than the parent compound (EC50: 186 nM for U-47700, 3770 nM for N-desmethyl, above 5 μM for bisdesmethyl). [f:nordmeier2022]

## effects

It is a selective agonist of the μ opioid receptor, several times more potent than morphine. [f:vogliardi2018]

The in vivo activity is about 10 times that of morphine. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

## doses

There are no clinical trial data on the safety of U-47700: no human dose has been established. [f:rambaran2017]

## risks

People who survive an overdose present with reduced mental status and slowed breathing, an opioid picture, often with a fast heart rate; people who die often arrive with pulmonary edema. [f:rambaran2017]

After insufflating U-47700 bought online, a man was found unresponsive with severe respiratory depression and recovered with supportive care. [f:vogliardi2018]

In 10 Finnish autopsies the cause of death was accidental poisoning by U-47700, alone or with other substances; blood 0.15-2.0 mg/L, median 0.30. [f:kriikku2019]

## interactions

In three deaths studied, U-47700 or other synthetic opioids were combined with other opioids, such as methadone, and with benzodiazepines, which can have synergistic effects. [f:giorgetti2024]

## counselling

Explain that it is an opioid much more potent than morphine and that clinical data on doses are missing. [f:kyei-baffour2020] [f:rambaran2017]

Explain the signs of an opioid overdose: reduced mental status, slowed breathing, often a fast heart rate. [f:rambaran2017]

Ask about benzodiazepines, methadone and other opioids: in the deaths studied, U-47700 was combined with these substances. [f:giorgetti2024] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask whether the person bought it as oxycodone or another tablet: it has been found in counterfeit oxycodone tablets. [f:kyei-baffour2020]

Explain that immunoassays failed to detect it and that identifying it required chromatographic and spectral techniques. [f:rambaran2017]
