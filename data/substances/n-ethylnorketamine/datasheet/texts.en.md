## molecule

N-Ethylnorketamine (NENK), 2-(2-chlorophenyl)-2-(ethylamino)cyclohexan-1-one. Formula C14H18ClNO, 251.75 g/mol, CAS 1354634-10-8. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It has the ketamine skeleton with an ethyl group in place of the N-methyl group; PubChem also lists it as ethketamine. [f:pubchem]

Like ketamine, it has a cyclohexanone: the C=O gives a band in the region of saturated ketones, and the position of the ring substituent changes the bands below 900 cm-1. No band value is given here because no verified reference spectrum is cited. [f:smith-carbonyl] [f:smith-benzene]

## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines. Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

NENK was synthesized as an analogue of methoxetamine in a study of rapid antidepressant effects in mice; it has since been described as a new synthetic ketamine analogue that has emerged as an abused drug. [f:sayson2019] [f:li2022-nenk]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a ketamine analogue. [f:li2022-nenk]
