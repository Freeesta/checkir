## molecule

25C-NBOMe, 2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethan-1-amine (2C-C-NBOMe). Base: C18H22ClNO3, 335.8 g/mol, CAS 1227608-02-7. Hydrochloride: C18H22ClNO3·HCl, about 372 g/mol, melting point 183.1 °C, white powder. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and the SWGDRUG monograph. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:swgdrug-25c-nbome]

Forms: the SWGDRUG reference material is the hydrochloride, with a diamond ATR spectrum (3 bounces, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-25c-nbome]

The NBOMes are N-benzylmethoxy derivatives of the 2C compounds (2C-I, 2C-B, 2C-C): 25C-NBOMe derives from 2C-C. [f:suzuki2015]

## category

Chemical class: NBOMe (N-benzyl phenethylamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:suzuki2015] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The NBOMes were synthesised for research as potent agonists of the 5-HT2A receptor. [f:suzuki2015]

Several NBOMes have been on sale from online vendors since 2010, with numerous cases of toxicity and several deaths. [f:halberstadt2017]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of a phenethylamine of the 2C series. [f:suzuki2015]

## metabolism

25C-NBOMe is metabolised mainly by O-demethylation, O,O-bis-demethylation and hydroxylation, and combinations of these, and by glucuronidation and sulfation of the phase I metabolites: 69 metabolites were identified in the study. [f:caspar2017]

In an initial screening with human enzymes, CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 took part in hydroxylation and CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 in O-demethylation. [f:caspar2017]

## effects

In the in vitro comparison with 2C-C and LSD, the NBOMes are very potent 5-HT2A agonists (EC50 0.04-0.5 µM) with high affinity for adrenergic α1 receptors (Ki 0.3-0.9 µM); the profile predicts strong hallucinogenic effects like those of LSD and possibly more stimulant ones. [f:rickli2015]

## doses

No dose is given for 25C-NBOMe: the active amount is so small that it cannot be judged by eye, and no dose is safe to suggest. [f:suzuki2015]

## risks

25C-NBOMe was confirmed in 2 of the 20 patients with analytically documented NBOMe toxicity in the review; in the group 3 died (15 %) and 8 of 20 were admitted to intensive care. [f:suzuki2015]

25C-NBOMe has been associated with hallucinogenic effects and severe intoxications in users. [f:caspar2017]

## interactions

5-HT2A agonism contributes to serotonin syndrome: for the NBOMes it has been suggested that the severe reactions are cases of this syndrome, but the typical neuromuscular signs (hyperreflexia, tremor, clonus) were rare in the cases described. [f:suzuki2015]
