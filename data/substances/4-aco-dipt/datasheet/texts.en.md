## molecule

4-AcO-DiPT (4-acetoxy-N,N-diisopropyltryptamine), [3-[2-[di(propan-2-yl)amino]ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl] acetate. Formula C18H26N2O2, 302.4 g/mol, CAS 936015-60-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: the O-acetyl (4-acetoxy) derivative of 4-HO-DiPT, with the acetyl group on the 4-hydroxy group. [f:pubchem] [f:klein2021]

## metabolism

In human hepatocytes incubated with 4-AcO-DiPT for 3 h, six metabolites were identified, formed by ester hydrolysis, glucuronidation, sulfation, N-oxide formation and N-dealkylation. All second-generation metabolites came from the only first-generation one, 4-OH-DiPT (the hydrolysis product); 4-OH-DiPT, 4-OH-iPT and 4-OH-DiPT N-oxide are suggested as biomarkers of 4-AcO-DiPT intake. [f:malaca2022]
