## molecule

Norcocaine, methyl (1R,2R,3S,5S)-3-(benzoyloxy)-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane-2-carboxylate. Base: C16H19NO4, 289.33 g/mol, CAS 18717-72-1. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is cocaine (own sheet) without the methyl on the nitrogen, which stays a secondary N-H. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: Tropane esters (cocaine). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A compound found as an impurity in illicit cocaine (own sheet), among the compounds used to profile samples; it is also a metabolite of cocaine. [f:laposchan2021] [f:liu2016-cocaine] [f:ladona2000]

## metabolism

Norcocaine is formed by N-demethylation of cocaine (own sheet), catalysed mainly by CYP3A in human fetal and adult liver microsomes. [f:ladona2000] [f:roque-bravo2022]
