## molecule

DOC, 1-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)propan-2-amine (4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine). Base: C11H16ClNO2, 229.70 g/mol, CAS 123431-31-2. Hydrochloride: 266 g/mol, CAS 42203-77-0. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:swgdrug-doc]

Forms: the SWGDRUG reference material is the hydrochloride, a white powder, with a diamond ATR spectrum. [f:swgdrug-doc]

Analogues that one band cannot tell apart: DOC belongs to the 2,5-dimethoxy-4-substituted amphetamines together with DOB, DOI, DOM and DOET, which differ in the substituent in position 4; the full reference spectrum is needed. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines (2,4,5-ring-substituted). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Synthetic compound, listed among the ring-substituted amphetamine-type stimulants. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## metabolism

DOC and the other 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamine-derived drugs studied are mainly metabolised by O-demethylation (DOM mainly by hydroxylation of the methyl group); among the human P450 enzymes tested only CYP2D6 was involved, and the amounts of metabolites formed were very small. [f:ewald2008]

## effects

In rats DOC substituted for several hallucinogens used as training drugs but not for methamphetamine; in cells it was a full agonist at 5-HT2A and 5-HT2C receptors in inositol phosphate assays. [f:eshleman2014]

## risks

A fatal case attributed to DOC alone is described: a 37-year-old man with DOC at 377 ng/mL in iliac blood and caffeine as the only other compound detected; the authors note the limited literature. [f:barnett2014]

In a second fatal case DOC was below 10 ng/mL in cardiac blood together with buprenorphine, cocaine and cannabis metabolites; the authors note that the toxic effects of new substances can be additive even at low concentrations. [f:lelievre2022]

Between 2005 and 2024 US forensic laboratories reported 795 submissions positive for DOC (152 at the 2012 peak, 2 in 2023-2024); the authors found three reports of poisonings linked to DOC. [f:palamar2025]

In rodents DOC had rewarding and reinforcing effects. [f:cha2018]

## interactions

DOC and five related 2,5-dimethoxyamphetamines inhibited CYP2D6 competitively in vitro (Ki 7.1-296 µM with the recombinant enzyme and 2.7-19.9 µM in human liver microsomes, for the group); the authors judged interactions with other CYP2D6 substrates possible but rather unlikely. [f:ewald2008]

## counselling

Ask about other substances taken together, such as cannabis and other psychedelics: in a fatal case DOC was found with buprenorphine, cocaine and cannabis metabolites, and the authors suggest that toxic effects can be additive. [f:lelievre2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Explain that the literature on DOC is limited, with few deaths and poisonings described, and that a fatal case had a low blood concentration: these data do not identify an amount below which no harm is expected. [f:barnett2014] [f:lelievre2022] [f:palamar2025]
