## molecule

LSM-775 (lysergic acid morpholide), [(6aR,9R)-7-methyl-6,6a,8,9-tetrahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinolin-9-yl]-morpholin-4-ylmethanone. Formula C20H23N3O2, 337.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A powdered sample was characterised by X-ray crystallography, NMR, mass spectrometry and, among other methods, GC solid-state infrared analysis. [f:brandt2017]

## category

Chemical class: lysergamides (family sheet: tryptamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

LSM-775 appeared on the new psychoactive substance market in 2013; the literature disagrees on its potency and psychoactive properties in humans. [f:brandt2017]

## metabolism

With pooled human liver S9 fraction, LSM-775 underwent N-dealkylation and hydroxylation, and combinations of the two, mainly through CYP1A2 and CYP3A4 (nine LSD derivatives studied); in rat urine neither the drug nor its metabolites were found after expected recreational doses. In vitro data; no human in vivo data. [f:wagmann2019]
