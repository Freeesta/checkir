## molecule

25E-NBOH, 2-[[2-(4-ethyl-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethylamino]methyl]phenol. Formula C19H25NO3, 315.4 g/mol, CAS 1391489-79-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

In place of the 2-methoxybenzyl group of the NBOMes it has a 2-hydroxybenzyl group (a phenol) on the nitrogen of 2C-E (4-ethyl); the NBOH series is described with the same structural layout as the NBOMes. [f:pubchem] [f:pelletier2024]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines (N-benzyl phenethylamines); outside the NBOMe family, which covers only the 2-methoxybenzyl derivatives. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:checkir-classification]

## history

25E-NBOH is one of four NBOH compounds (25B, 25C, 25E, 25I) that appeared on the illicit market. [f:munir2025]

In a case report a powder labelled 25I-NBOH contained 25E-NBOH and MDPHP; 25I-NBOH was not detected. [f:pelletier2024]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of 2C-E. [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In plasma and urine from one intoxication case, seven metabolites of 25E-NBOH were described: two phase I and five phase II. [f:pelletier2024]

Metabolite M6 (m/z 492.223) was the most intense ion in both matrices and is proposed as a consumption marker. [f:pelletier2024]
