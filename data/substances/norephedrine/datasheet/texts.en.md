## molecule

Norephedrine ((1R,2S)-2-amino-1-phenylpropan-1-ol), known as phenylpropanolamine (PPA). Base: C9H13NO, 151.21 g/mol, CAS 14838-15-4. Identity checked on PubChem, which gives the (1R,2S) isomer (CID 10297) for both names. [f:pubchem]

It is ephedrine (own sheet) without the methyl on the nitrogen, a primary amine; cathine (own sheet, (1S,2S)) is its diastereomer. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: Amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A phenethylamine with the same basic structure as ephedrine and cathine (own sheets). [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

PubChem reports hepatic metabolism and a biological half-life of 2.1 to 3.4 h. [f:pubchem]
