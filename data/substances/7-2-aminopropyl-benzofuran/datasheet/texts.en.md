## molecule

7-APB (7-(2-aminopropyl)benzofuran), 1-(1-benzofuran-7-yl)propan-2-amine. Formula C11H13NO, 175.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The aminopropyl chain is on position 7 of the benzofuran, the benzene position next to the ring-fusion carbon bonded to the oxygen; 6-APB (own sheet) carries it on position 6. It is a benzofuran, not an amphetamine ring. [f:pubchem]
