## molecule

UWA-101, 2-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-1-cyclopropyl-N-methylethanamine (α-cyclopropyl analogue of MDMA). Formula C13H17NO2, 219.3 g/mol, CAS 1350821-24-7. Identity checked on PubChem; the stereocentre is not specified. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: methylenedioxy phenethylamines. Expected effect: none psychoactive, since in the study that described it UWA-101 did not show psychoactive properties and did not substitute for MDMA in drug discrimination assays. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:johnston2012] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

UWA-101 was described in 2012 as an analogue of MDMA that enhanced the benefit of L-DOPA in parkinsonian primates; unlike MDMA it did not reduce the viability of serotonergic cells, and it inhibited the reuptake of serotonin and dopamine but not of noradrenaline. [f:johnston2012]

## origin

A synthetic substance, designed as an α-substituted analogue of MDMA bearing an α-cyclopropyl group. [f:johnston2012] [f:pubchem]
