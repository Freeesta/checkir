## category

## history

URB754 and URB602 had been reported as selective MAGL inhibitors. In rat brain cytosol URB754 inhibited the hydrolysis of 2-oleoylglycerol with an IC50 of 48 μM and did not inhibit human recombinant MAGL, so the study concluded that it does not behave as a selective or potent MAGL inhibitor. [f:vandevoorde2007]
