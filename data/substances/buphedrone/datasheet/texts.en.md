## origin

## metabolism

In mice given a single intraperitoneal dose (64 mg/kg), the metabolites most excreted in urine came from N-dealkylation (about 80 µg/mL of urine); ketone reduction, ketone reduction combined with N-dealkylation and 4-aryl hydroxylation were also found, and phase II conjugates (N-acetylated, glucuronides, dicarboxylic acid conjugates) were tentatively identified. [f:carrola2020]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often with lethal outcomes: in 42 reported cases death occurred in nearly half, with higher fatality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
