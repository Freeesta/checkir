## molecule

Lisdexamfetamine, (2S)-2,6-diamino-N-[(2S)-1-phenylpropan-2-yl]hexanamide. Formula C15H25N3O, 263.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a prodrug: dextroamphetamine bound to the amino acid lysine, active only after conversion to d-amphetamine; the amphetamine part has its own sheet (amphetamine). [f:pennick2010] [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:pennick2010] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a stimulant prodrug. [f:pennick2010]

## metabolism

Conversion to d-amphetamine happens mainly in whole blood, and only with red blood cells (rat and human samples); hydrolysis in liver and kidney is slow, probably due to residual blood. [f:pennick2010]

Absorption takes place in the small intestine, probably via the PEPT1 transporter. [f:pennick2010]
