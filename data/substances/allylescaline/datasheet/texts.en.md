## molecule

Allylescaline (4-allyloxy-3,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine), 2-(3,5-dimethoxy-4-prop-2-enoxyphenyl)ethanamine. Formula C13H19NO3, 237.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The ring carries methoxy groups at positions 3 and 5 as in mescaline and an allyloxy group at position 4; escaline has an ethoxy group there. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a mescaline analogue with the 4-methoxy replaced by an allyloxy group. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]
