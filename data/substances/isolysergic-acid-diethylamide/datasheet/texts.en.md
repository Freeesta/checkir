## molecule

iso-LSD (isolysergic acid diethylamide), (6aR,9S)-N,N-diethyl-7-methyl-6,6a,8,9-tetrahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide. Formula C20H25N3O, 323.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the C8 diastereoisomer of LSD: PubChem gives (6aR,9S) for iso-LSD and (6aR,9R) for LSD (sheet `lsd`); the sheets are separate. [f:pubchem]
