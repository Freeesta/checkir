## molecule

Azaperone, 1-(4-fluorophenyl)-4-(4-pyridin-2-ylpiperazin-1-yl)butan-1-one. Formula C19H22FN3O, 327.40 g/mol, CAS 1649-18-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a butyrophenone neuroleptic used as a veterinary sedative in pigs. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:ema-azaperone-mrl] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a veterinary sedative mainly used in pigs. [f:ema-azaperone-mrl]

## metabolism

In pigs azaperone is rapidly absorbed and completely excreted in urine and faeces, with only a small portion as the parent compound. The major metabolite in edible tissue is azaperol, used as the marker residue to control the use of azaperone; some evidence suggests it is pharmacologically less effective than azaperone. [f:ema-azaperone-mrl]

The figures come from the veterinary assessment; human data were not found. [f:ema-azaperone-mrl]
