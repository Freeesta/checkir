## molecule

Modafinil, 2-(diphenylmethylsulfinyl)acetamide. Base: C15H15NO2S, 273.36 g/mol, CAS 68693-11-8. Identity checked on PubChem (flat structure, no stereochemistry). [f:pubchem]

It is a 1:1 racemic compound (the sulfoxide is chiral); the enantiomers do not interconvert and the R enantiomer is armodafinil. [f:dailymed-provigil] [f:sousa2020]

## category

Chemical class: Other substances. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, the active ingredient of medicines (Provigil). [f:dailymed-provigil]

## metabolism

Elimination is about 90% by metabolism, primarily hepatic, with renal elimination of the metabolites: hydrolytic deamidation, S-oxidation, aromatic ring hydroxylation and glucuronide conjugation; less than 10% of a dose is excreted as the parent compound. [f:dailymed-provigil]

Only two metabolites reach appreciable concentrations in plasma, modafinil acid and modafinil sulfone; in preclinical models they were inactive or did not appear to mediate the arousal effects. The effective elimination half-life of modafinil after multiple doses is about 15 h, that of the sulfone 40 h. [f:dailymed-provigil]
