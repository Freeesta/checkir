## molecule

URB597, [3-(3-carbamoylphenyl)phenyl] N-cyclohexylcarbamate (cyclohexylcarbamic acid 3'-carbamoylbiphenyl-3-yl ester). Formula C20H22N2O3, 338.4 g/mol, CAS 546141-08-6. Identity checked on PubChem. Achiral. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: a carbamate research compound, inhibitor of fatty acid amide hydrolase (FAAH) (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: none psychoactive. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:kathuria2003] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

In 2003 a class of potent, selective and systemically active FAAH inhibitors, the enzyme that degrades the endocannabinoid anandamide, was described; in rats these inhibitors showed benzodiazepine-like properties in anxiety tests and raised brain anandamide levels. [f:kathuria2003]

## origin

A synthetic substance: an O-aryl N-cyclohexyl carbamate on a biphenyl core, developed as a FAAH inhibitor. [f:kathuria2003] [f:pubchem]
