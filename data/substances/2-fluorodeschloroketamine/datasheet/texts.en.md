## molecule

2-Fluorodeschloroketamine (2-FDCK), 2-(2-fluorophenyl)-2-(methylamino)cyclohexan-1-one. Formula C13H16FNO, 221.27 g/mol, CAS 111982-50-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It has the ketamine skeleton with fluorine in place of chlorine on the ring; the analytical literature describes it as close to ketamine in structure. [f:pubchem] [f:riess2024]

Like ketamine, it has a cyclohexanone: the C=O gives a band in the region of saturated ketones, and the position of the ring substituent changes the bands below 900 cm-1. No band value is given here because no verified reference spectrum is cited. [f:smith-carbonyl] [f:smith-benzene]

## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines. Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Ketamine analogues such as 2-FDCK are emerging dissociative substances, increasingly detected on drug markets worldwide; the French Addictovigilance Network received cases from 2017 to 2024. [f:mezaache2026]

## origin

Synthetic compound, a ketamine analogue. [f:riess2024]

## metabolism

In one case of self-mutilation and two forensic deaths, blood contained 2-FDCK and its main metabolites nor-2-FDCK, dihydro-nor-2-FDCK and dihydro-2-FDCK. [f:riess2024]

In rats given a single intraperitoneal injection, metabolites were identified in blood, liver and urine; human pharmacokinetic data are not available in the sources reviewed. [f:zhang2026]

## effects

Adverse effects reported to the French Addictovigilance Network for ketamine analogues, mostly 2-FDCK, were mainly neurological and psychiatric, including dissociation and impaired consciousness. [f:mezaache2026]

## risks

Of 56 cases of ketamine analogues reported in France between 2017 and 2024 (52 % involving 2-FDCK), 61 % were serious and 11 % were deaths; the health risks may differ from or exceed those of ketamine. [f:mezaache2026]

Three forensic cases (one self-mutilation, two deaths) had 2-FDCK in peripheral blood at 457, 758 and 5885 µg/L; deschloroketamine was also found in all three. [f:riess2024]

In human cerebral organoids and mouse neurons, 2-FDCK impaired mitochondrial oxidative phosphorylation (in vitro, 30 μM). [f:wang2026]

## counselling

Ask what the person was told the sample is: some cases involved unintended exposure to 2-FDCK sold as synthetic cathinones, cocaine or ketamine, often in chemsex settings, with distressing experiences. [f:mezaache2026]

Explain that 2-FDCK is not a substitute for ketamine: its risks may differ from or exceed those of ketamine, so experience with ketamine does not carry over. [f:mezaache2026]

Mention that deaths and a case of self-mutilation have been reported with blood concentrations far above those of the other cases. [f:riess2024]
