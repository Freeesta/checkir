## molecule

TH-PVP (3',4'-tetramethylene-α-pyrrolidinovalerophenone), 2-pyrrolidin-1-yl-1-(5,6,7,8-tetrahydronaphthalen-2-yl)pentan-1-one. Base: C19H27NO, 285.4 g/mol, CAS 2304915-07-7. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified). [f:pubchem]

It is the tetrahydronaphthyl analogue of α-PVP; `th-pbp` is the homologue with a side chain one carbon shorter. [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

With human liver microsomes 27 metabolites in 12 categories were identified; the main pathways are hydroxylation, β-ketoreduction and their combinations (91.57% of the metabolites). Hydroxylation occurs preferentially on sp3 carbons, particularly at the α-position of the phenyl group, followed by the pyrrolidinyl group. In vitro data, not in humans. [f:luo2025]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
