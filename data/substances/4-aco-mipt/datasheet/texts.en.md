## molecule

4-AcO-MiPT (mipracetin), [3-[2-[methyl(propan-2-yl)amino]ethyl]-1H-indol-4-yl] acetate. Formula C16H22N2O2, 274.36 g/mol, CAS 1024612-25-6. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: the O-acetyl (4-acetoxy) derivative of 4-HO-MiPT, with the acetyl group on the 4-hydroxy group. [f:pubchem] [f:klein2021]

## metabolism

In 17 tryptamines with a 4-hydroxy or 4-acetoxy group, acetylation lowered 5-HT2A potency in vitro about 10- to 20-fold but changed head-twitch potency in mice little, which suggests that the acetylated compounds are deacetylated and act as prodrugs of the 4-hydroxy compound (4-HO-MiPT). No metabolic study of 4-AcO-MiPT itself was found. [f:klein2021]
