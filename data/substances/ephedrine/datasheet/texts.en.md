## molecule

Ephedrine, (1R,2S)-2-(methylamino)-1-phenylpropan-1-ol. Base: C10H15NO, 165.23 g/mol, CAS 299-42-3. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The SWGDRUG monograph describes the enantiomer d-ephedrine (1S,2R): hydrochloride C10H15NO·HCl, 201.69 g/mol, melting point 220.65 °C, white crystals. [f:swgdrug-ephedrine]

Bands of d-ephedrine hydrochloride (ATR): O-H at 3321 cm-1, N-H2+ envelope from 2960 to 2750 cm-1 with a band at 2466 cm-1, ring at 1587 and 1453 cm-1, then 1388, 1048 and 991 cm-1, and the two bands of the monosubstituted ring at 751 and 698 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-ephedrine] [f:smith-amine-salts] [f:smith-benzene]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle or pharmacological adulterant. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Ephedra herb (ma huang) has been used for thousands of years in China, mainly for colds, bronchial asthma and nasal congestion. [f:zheng2023]

In the United States, supplements with ephedra alkaloids were sold for weight loss and energy; adverse-event reports led the FDA to propose limits on dose and duration of use. [f:haller2000]

## origin

A natural alkaloid of the Ephedra species used as ma huang; ephedrine and pseudoephedrine are the main active constituents of the herb. [f:zheng2023]

Ephedrine and pseudoephedrine are among the precursors of methamphetamine, listed in Table I of the 1988 UN Convention, and can remain in the product. [f:euda-meth] [f:unodc-ats2006]

## metabolism

After an oral dose of 0.35 mg/kg of (-)-[14C]ephedrine in 3 volunteers, 97 % was excreted in urine within 48 h, mostly as unchanged ephedrine (53-74 %). [f:sever1975]

N-demethylation accounted for 8-20 % and oxidative deamination for 4-13 % (mainly benzoic acid and 1,2-dihydroxy-1-phenylpropane); no phenolic metabolites were found. [f:sever1975]

The urinary elimination half-life was 5.2 ± 1.2 h for ephedrine and 4.2 ± 1.3 h for norephedrine (6 volunteers, herbal preparation). [f:chan2008]

The half-life of ephedrine was prolonged in the subject with the highest urine pH. [f:haller2002]

## cuts

The UBC guidelines say that speed contains mainly amphetamines and other stimulants such as ephedrine and caffeine, and that ephedrine is often mistaken for amphetamine because of its chemical similarity. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

## effects

A single dose of a supplement with 20 mg of ephedrine alkaloids and 200 mg of caffeine raised systolic pressure by up to 14 mm Hg after 90 minutes and heart rate by up to 15 beats/min after 6 hours in 8 healthy adults. [f:haller2002]

In 12 healthy adults, ma-huang capsules raised heart rate in six, with variable effects on blood pressure. [f:white1997]

## doses

The pharmacokinetics of ma-huang capsules (mean 19.4 mg of ephedrine per four capsules) matched those reported for a 20 mg immediate-release ephedrine tablet. [f:white1997]

## risks

Of 140 adverse-event reports with ephedra-alkaloid supplements, 62 % were judged definitely, probably or possibly related; among these 47 % were cardiovascular and 18 % involved the central nervous system (hypertension, palpitations or tachycardia, stroke, seizures), with 10 deaths and 13 permanent disabilities. [f:haller2000]

Excessive use of ephedra herb causes palpitations, sweating, irritability and insomnia. [f:zheng2023]

## counselling

If the sample was sold as speed, ask what the person thinks it contains: the UBC guidelines list ephedrine among the stimulants found in speed, and ephedrine can also be a residue of methamphetamine preparation. [f:unodc-ats2006] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about palpitations, headache and high blood pressure: ephedra-alkaloid events were mainly cardiovascular (hypertension, tachycardia, stroke) and nervous (seizures). [f:haller2000]

Explain that the effect on pulse can lag: after a single dose systolic pressure peaked at 90 minutes and heart rate at 6 hours; ask about caffeine and other stimulants taken together, since the supplement studied combined ephedrine alkaloids with caffeine. [f:haller2002]
