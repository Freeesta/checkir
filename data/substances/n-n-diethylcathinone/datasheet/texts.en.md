## history

Amfepramone (diethylpropion) is an anti-obesity drug; in China it is controlled and not available as a pharmaceutical product, but is obtainable through the internet or personal import, and its abuse causes serious health problems. [f:yang2021]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an anti-obesity drug. [f:yang2021]

## metabolism

Cathinone is a metabolite of amfepramone: in the hair of 17 users amfepramone was present at a median of 30.2 pg/mg and cathinone at a median of 54.0 pg/mg. [f:yang2021]
