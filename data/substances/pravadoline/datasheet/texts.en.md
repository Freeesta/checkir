## molecule

Pravadoline (WIN 48,098), (4-methoxyphenyl)-[2-methyl-1-(2-morpholin-4-ylethyl)indol-3-yl]methanone. Base: C23H26N2O3, 378.5 g/mol, CAS 92623-83-1. Identity checked against PubChem (main entry). Achiral. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Pravadoline was an (aminoalkyl)indole analgesic of the Sterling Research Group, an inhibitor of cyclooxygenase; its analogues led to a new class of cannabinoid receptor agonists. [f:dambra1992]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an aminoalkylindole with a 4-methoxybenzoyl group and a morpholinoethyl chain on a 2-methylindole. [f:pubchem]

## effects

Pravadoline inhibits prostaglandin synthesis and neuronally stimulated contractions of mouse vas deferens (IC50 0.45 μM). [f:dambra1992]

Six aminoalkylindole analogues related to pravadoline were tested in the mouse: those with a naphthoyl group had all the properties of Δ9-THC; no data on the effects of pravadoline itself in humans were found in the sources used. [f:compton1992]
