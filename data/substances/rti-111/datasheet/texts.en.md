## molecule

RTI-111, methyl (1R,2S,3S,5S)-3-(3,4-dichlorophenyl)-8-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octane-2-carboxylate. Formula C16H19Cl2NO2, 328.2 g/mol, CAS 143965-99-5 (free base). Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a phenyltropane, an analogue of cocaine: the 2-carbomethoxy group of the tropane is kept, while the benzoyloxy group at position 3 is replaced by a 3,4-dichlorophenyl ring bonded directly to the ring. [f:pubchem]
