## molecule

Chlorzoxazone, 5-chloro-3H-1,3-benzoxazol-2-one. Formula C7H4ClNO2, 169.56 g/mol, CAS 95-25-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a centrally acting skeletal muscle relaxant (benzoxazolone). Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:chlorzoxazone-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a chlorinated benzoxazolone muscle relaxant. [f:chlorzoxazone-label]

## metabolism

Chlorzoxazone is rapidly metabolized and excreted in the urine, mainly as the glucuronide; less than 1% of a dose is excreted unchanged in 24 hours. Blood levels are detectable within 30 minutes and peak in most subjects after about 1 to 2 hours. [f:chlorzoxazone-label]
