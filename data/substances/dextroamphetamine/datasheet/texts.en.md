## molecule

Dextroamphetamine, (2S)-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Base: C9H13N, 135.2 g/mol, CAS 51-64-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the S (dextro) enantiomer of amphetamine, which is a racemate of two optical isomers: same formula and connectivity as the racemic amphetamine sheet, with a different configuration at the stereocentre. This sheet is not an alias of amphetamine. [f:zenzedi-label] [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: the dextro isomer of amphetamine, used as a prescription medicine in the form of the sulfate. [f:zenzedi-label]

## metabolism

4-Hydroxyamphetamine and norephedrine are measured in serum as metabolites of amphetamine. Co-administration of CYP2D6 inhibitors may increase exposure to dextroamphetamine. [f:losacker2021] [f:zenzedi-label]

After tablets or sustained-release capsules in 12 healthy subjects the average plasma half-life was about 12 h. [f:zenzedi-label]
