## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

In a locomotor test in mice, ortho-fluoroacrylfentanyl at 0.1, 1 and 10 mg/kg produced dose-dependent increases in locomotion, with a maximum effect of 63.56% of that of fentanyl. [f:varshneya2023-locomotor]

## doses

Potency varies widely between fentanyl analogues and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lovrecic2019]

## risks

In poisonings with fentanyl analogues larger naloxone doses are needed than in a classic heroin overdose. [f:lovrecic2019] [f:armenian2018]

As soon as a compound is regulated new ones appear, which makes detection difficult; some illicit pills with these compounds look like known opioids or benzodiazepines. [f:armenian2018]

## interactions

Benzodiazepines and other sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
