## effects

Designer benzodiazepines act on the GABA-A receptor and produce effects similar to therapeutic benzodiazepines: sedation, hypnosis, anxiolysis, muscle relaxation, euphoria, amnesia and addiction. [f:el-balkhi2023]

They are central nervous system depressants and sedatives that can cause psychomotor impairment. [f:wu2023]

## risks

Relative potency is hard to establish, because it depends on the structure and on the many GABA-A receptor subtypes. [f:el-balkhi2023]

For many of these molecules human metabolism data are scarce, and the specific pathways and the influence of genetic polymorphisms are not clarified. [f:gameli2024] [f:wu2023]

## interactions

Pharmacodynamic interactions with other central nervous system depressants, such as opioids, are extensively reported; pharmacokinetic interactions with opioids are considered less important. [f:el-balkhi2023]

Combined with other sedatives, the risk of overdose increases. [f:wu2023]
