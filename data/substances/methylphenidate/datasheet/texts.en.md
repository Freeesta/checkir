## molecule

Methylphenidate, methyl 2-phenyl-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Base: C14H19NO2, 233.31 g/mol, CAS 113-45-1. Identity checked on PubChem, which gives the flat structure without stereochemistry for the name. [f:pubchem]

It has two chiral centres; the drug label reports data for the d and l enantiomers. [f:dailymed-ritalin]

## category

Chemical class: Phenidates. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, the active ingredient of medicines (Ritalin). [f:dailymed-ritalin]

## metabolism

Methylphenidate is metabolized primarily by de-esterification to alpha-phenyl-piperidine acetic acid (ritalinic acid), which has little or no pharmacologic activity; the hydrolytic esterases are nonmicrosomal and widely distributed throughout the body. [f:dailymed-ritalin]

After oral administration 78% to 97% of the dose is excreted in the urine as metabolites within 48 to 96 h; ritalinic acid is 60% to 86% of the dose and less than 1% is excreted unchanged. [f:dailymed-ritalin]
