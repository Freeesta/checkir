## molecule

Armodafinil, 2-[(R)-benzhydrylsulfinyl]acetamide. Formula C15H15NO2S, 273.4 g/mol, CAS 112111-43-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the R-enantiomer of modafinil (a mixture of R- and S-enantiomers); the S-enantiomer is cleared faster, with a half-life of about 4 h. [f:nuvigil-label]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. The effect class (stimulant) is a checkir classification, since the label describes a wakefulness-promoting medicine whose mechanism is unknown. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:nuvigil-label] [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound marketed in the USA as tablets under the name NUVIGIL. [f:nuvigil-label]

## metabolism

Amide hydrolysis is the most prominent pathway, followed by sulfone formation by CYP3A4/5; aromatic ring hydroxylation with glucuronide conjugation also occurs. Only R-modafinil acid and modafinil sulfone reach appreciable plasma concentrations. [f:nuvigil-label]

Less than 10% of the parent compound is excreted in urine. After oral doses the apparent terminal half-life is about 15 h. [f:nuvigil-label]
