## molecule

25N-NBOMe, 2-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-nitrophenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethanamine. Formula C18H22N2O5, 346.4 g/mol, CAS 1354632-03-3. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: NBOMe. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

In vitro 25N-NBOMe is a 5-HT2A agonist with subnanomolar to low nanomolar potency, similar to LSD. [f:eshleman2018]

## origin

A synthetic substance: 2C-N (nitro at position 4) with a 2-methoxybenzyl group on the nitrogen. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes 14 metabolites of 25N-NBOMe were found; the biotransformations included hydroxylation, O-demethylation, N-dealkylation and nitro reduction, and after phase I the hydroxylated metabolite was the most abundant. [f:seo2018]

In a second microsomal study, the 24 phase I metabolites of 25N-NBOMe included the 2C-N metabolite (without the benzyl group), and reduction of the nitro group with N-acetylation of the resulting amine was specific to this compound among the NBOMes studied. [f:grafinger2018]
