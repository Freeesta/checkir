## molecule

5-IAI (5-iodo-2-aminoindane), 5-iodo-2,3-dihydro-1H-inden-2-amine. Formula C9H10IN, 259.1 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The amine sits on the 2 position of the indane ring and an iodine on the 5 position; the unsubstituted analogue is 2-AI. [f:pubchem]
