## molecule

Benzoylfentanyl, N-phenyl-N-[1-(2-phenylethyl)piperidin-4-yl]benzamide. Base: C26H28N2O, 384.5 g/mol, CAS 2309383-15-9. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

PubChem lists phenylfentanyl as a synonym; the studies cited below use that name. [f:pubchem]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

In mice phenylfentanyl produced antinociception with an ED50 of 55.2 mg/kg against 0.08 mg/kg for fentanyl, and at 100 mg/kg it decreased locomotion. [f:varshneya2021]

## doses

In mice phenylfentanyl was far less potent than fentanyl, but its potency in humans is unknown, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:varshneya2021]

## risks

Phenylfentanyl was one of 11 compounds that cross-reacted in at least one of six ELISA kits tested in whole blood. [f:mastrovito2022]

## interactions

Sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
