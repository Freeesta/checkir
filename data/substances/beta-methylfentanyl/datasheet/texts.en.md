## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

In mice beta-methylfentanyl produced antinociception with an ED50 of 0.74 mg/kg against 0.08 mg/kg for fentanyl; naltrexone raised its ED50 19.2-fold, consistent with a μ-opioid receptor mechanism. [f:varshneya2021]

## doses

In mice beta-methylfentanyl was less potent than fentanyl as an antinociceptive, but its potency in humans is unknown, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:varshneya2021]
