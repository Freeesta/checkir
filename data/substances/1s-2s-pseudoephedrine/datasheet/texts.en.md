## molecule

Pseudoephedrine, (1S,2S)-2-(methylamino)-1-phenylpropan-1-ol. Formula C10H15NO, 165.23 g/mol, CAS 90-82-4. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the (1S,2S) diastereoisomer of ephedrine (sheet `ephedrine`, 1R,2S). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle or pharmacological adulterant. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A natural alkaloid of Ephedra herb, where it is found with ephedrine and methylephedrine. [f:hong2011]

Ephedrine and pseudoephedrine are among the precursors of methamphetamine, listed in Table I of the 1988 UN Convention, and can remain in the product. [f:euda-meth] [f:unodc-ats2006]

## metabolism

After oral doses of pseudoephedrine-containing medicines in healthy volunteers, urine contained pseudoephedrine and its metabolite cathine (norpseudoephedrine). [f:barroso2011]
