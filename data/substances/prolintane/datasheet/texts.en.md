## molecule

Prolintane, 1-(1-phenylpentan-2-yl)pyrrolidine. Formula C15H23N, 217.35 g/mol, CAS 493-92-5; the PubChem entry has unspecified stereochemistry. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a phenethylamine with a propyl group on the alpha carbon and the nitrogen closed in a pyrrolidine ring. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a stimulant used as a drug. It has been found in doping controls in cyclists. [f:pubchem] [f:delbeke1996]

## metabolism

Oxoprolintane is the principal urinary metabolite of prolintane in humans; both can be measured in urine. [f:espartero1997]

In volunteers given the racemic drug, the metabolites in the 24 h urine were identified and quantified; the R and S enantiomers differ mainly in the amounts of the metabolites. [f:rucker1992]
