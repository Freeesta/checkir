## molecule

Methocarbamol, [2-hydroxy-3-(2-methoxyphenoxy)propyl] carbamate. Formula C11H15NO5, 241.24 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a carbamate derivative of guaifenesin, a central nervous system depressant with sedative and skeletal muscle relaxant properties. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:methocarbamol-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, a carbamate derivative of guaifenesin, used as a medicine. [f:methocarbamol-label]

## metabolism

Methocarbamol is metabolized via dealkylation and hydroxylation; conjugation is also likely. Essentially all metabolites are eliminated in the urine, with small amounts of unchanged drug. [f:methocarbamol-label]

In healthy volunteers the mean plasma elimination half-life is between 1 and 2 hours. [f:methocarbamol-label]
