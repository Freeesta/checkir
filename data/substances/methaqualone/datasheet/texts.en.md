## molecule

Methaqualone, 2-methyl-3-(2-methylphenyl)quinazolin-4-one. Formula C16H14N2O, 250.29 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a sedative-hypnotic of the 4-quinazolinone class, introduced in the 1960s as an alternative to barbiturates and withdrawn from the market. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:tusiewicz2024] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance of the 4-quinazolinone class, made as a sedative-hypnotic. [f:tusiewicz2024]

## metabolism

Methaqualone is hydroxylated: five major monohydroxylated metabolites are found in urine, 4'-, 2'-, 3'-, 2- and 6-hydroxymethaqualone, and 4'-hydroxymethaqualone was the major urinary metabolite after 250 mg of methaqualone. [f:prost2001] [f:prost2003]

In human liver microsomes and CYP3A4-only preparations the formation of the 2'- and 2-hydroxy metabolites was stereoselective; CYP2D6 and CYP2C19 do not contribute significantly. [f:prost2003]

In six adults given 200 mg, the hydroxy metabolites were mostly excreted as conjugates; after hydrolysis the 2'- and 3'-hydroxy metabolites were the most abundant. [f:brenner1996]
