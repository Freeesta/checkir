## molecule

Proscaline, 2-(3,5-dimethoxy-4-propoxyphenyl)ethanamine. Formula C13H21NO3, 239.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A mescaline analogue: the 4-methoxy group of mescaline is replaced by a propoxy group, with methoxy groups on positions 3 and 5. [f:pubchem]
