## molecule

APP-CHMINACA (PX-3), N-[(2S)-1-amino-1-oxo-3-phenylpropan-2-yl]-1-(cyclohexylmethyl)indazole-3-carboxamide. Base: C24H28N4O2, 404.5 g/mol, CAS 1185887-14-2. Identity checked on PubChem. Synthetic cannabinoid with an indazole core, a cyclohexylmethyl group and a phenylalaninamide head; one chiral centre (S). [f:pubchem] [f:cooman2019]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes PX-3 had a half-life of 5.2 ± 0.89 min and an intrinsic clearance of 0.133 mL/min/mg; five phase I metabolites were detected. [f:cooman2019]

Oxidative deamination was the biotransformation common to PX-1, PX-2 and PX-3, which share no metabolites. [f:cooman2019]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC and the sources used have no potency data for this compound, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of various recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, admissions for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 versus 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids increases the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
