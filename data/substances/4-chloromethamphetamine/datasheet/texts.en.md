## molecule

4-CMA (4-chloromethamphetamine), 1-(4-chlorophenyl)-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Formula C10H14ClN, 183.7 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the N-methyl analogue of 4-chloroamphetamine and has chlorine where PMMA (separate sheet) has the methoxy group; 4-FMA is the fluorine analogue. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance: the para-chloro analogue of methamphetamine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

In 2018 it was described for the first time in a seized tablet sold as ecstasy, never reported before outside laboratories. [f:blanckaert2018]
