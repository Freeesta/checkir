## category

Chemical class: sugar (disaccharide, grouped under non-psychoactive substances). Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: diluent or excipient. [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Lactose is a disaccharide and the main source of calories in milk. [f:misselwitz2019]

## metabolism

In the small intestine the enzyme lactase hydrolyses lactose into galactose and glucose. [f:misselwitz2019]

## cuts

Lactose is among the sugars reported as cuts of cocaine. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

Internal guidelines of a drug checking service describe inert cuts as substances that add weight and volume. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

## risks

Lactose intolerance is the onset of abdominal pain, bloating and diarrhoea after lactose ingestion in a person with lactose malabsorption; its likelihood depends on the lactose dose, lactase expression and the intestinal microbiome. [f:misselwitz2019]

People with visceral hypersensitivity associated with anxiety or irritable bowel syndrome are at increased risk of this condition. [f:misselwitz2019]
