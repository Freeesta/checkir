## ftir_limits

The spectrum of a mixture reflects its main components. Components below about 5 % of the sample are generally not detectable, and FTIR does not measure concentration. Potent substances active at very low doses, such as fentanyl and its analogues (when found, usually at about 3-10 % w/w) and nitazenes, cannot be ruled out by this analysis. A match is a proposal for an expert to evaluate, not a diagnosis, and it holds only if the identification is correct; complementary tests are advisable. [f:checkir-ftir-limits] Lactose exists as alpha and beta forms and as a monohydrate, with spectra that differ in detail. Sugars, sugar alcohols and cellulose share broad O-H and C-O bands, so identifying them in a mixture is uncertain, and this record is lactose with the anomeric centre unspecified.

## risks

Lactose intolerance is the onset of abdominal pain, bloating and diarrhoea after lactose ingestion in a person with lactose malabsorption; its likelihood depends on the lactose dose, lactase expression and the intestinal microbiome. [f:misselwitz2019]

People with visceral hypersensitivity associated with anxiety or irritable bowel syndrome are at increased risk of this condition. [f:misselwitz2019]
