## molecule

Yohimbine, methyl (1S,15R,18S,19R,20S)-18-hydroxy-1,3,11,12,14,15,16,17,18,19,20,21-dodecahydroyohimban-19-carboxylate. Formula C21H26N2O3, 354.4 g/mol, CAS 146-48-5. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances (an indole alkaloid). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A natural alkaloid of the bark of the evergreen tree yohimbe (Pausinystalia johimbe). It is sold in dietary supplements labelled as containing yohimbe or yohimbine; in a survey of 49 brands in the USA, 19 contained neither rauwolscine nor corynanthine, which suggests a highly processed extract or a synthetic origin. [f:cohen2015]

## metabolism

In 172 people given intravenous yohimbine, the main route was oxidation to 11-hydroxyyohimbine, likely via CYP2D6; metabolism varied more than 1000-fold and 17 people showed none. Genotypes at both CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 were needed to predict it. [f:lecorre2004]
