## molecule

Theobromine, 3,7-dimethylpurine-2,6-dione. Formula C7H8N4O2, 180.16 g/mol, CAS 83-67-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: methylxanthine alkaloid, like caffeine (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:cova2019] [f:checkir-classification]

In animal studies an acute exposure to theobromine has a smaller and delayed effect than caffeine. [f:cova2019]

## origin

A natural methylxanthine: present with caffeine in raw cocoa beans and cocoa products. [f:goya2022]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes theobromine is converted to 3-methylxanthine, 7-methylxanthine and 3,7-dimethyluric acid; CYP1A2 contributes variably to 7-methylxanthine, CYP2E1 to all three pathways. [f:gates1999]
