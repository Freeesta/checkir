## molecule

MDPBP (3,4-methylenedioxy-α-pyrrolidinobutyrophenone), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-pyrrolidin-1-ylbutan-1-one. Base: C15H19NO3, 261.32 g/mol, CAS 784985-33-7. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified there). [f:pubchem]

Hydrochloride: C15H19NO3·HCl, 297 g/mol, melting point 241.9 °C, white powder; the monograph gives CAS 24622-60-4. The reference spectrum is an ATR one (diamond, 3 bounces, 32 scans, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-mdpbp]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cathinone with a methylenedioxy group and a pyrrolidine ring, in the series of homologues MDPPP, MDPBP and MDPV. In a fatal MDPHP case MDPBP was found at much lower concentrations than MDPHP, which the authors read as presence as a synthetic impurity. [f:casati2025]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often with lethal outcomes: in 42 reported cases death occurred in nearly half, with higher fatality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
