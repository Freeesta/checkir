## molecule

Coluracetam, N-(2,3-dimethyl-5,6,7,8-tetrahydrofuro[2,3-b]quinolin-4-yl)-2-(2-oxopyrrolidin-1-yl)acetamide. Formula C19H23N3O3, 341.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a tetrahydrofuroquinoline studied under the code MKC-231 as a high-affinity choline uptake enhancer. Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:bessho1996] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance. It was described as MKC-231 by researchers of Mitsubishi Chemical Corporation, who tested it in rats as a choline uptake enhancer. [f:bessho1996]
