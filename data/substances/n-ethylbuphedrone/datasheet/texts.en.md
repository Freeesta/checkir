## category

## history

3F-NEB, the fluorinated derivative of N-ethylbuphedrone, has recently appeared on recreational markets; in vitro it is a potent dopamine transporter inhibitor. The finding concerns the derivative, not N-ethylbuphedrone itself. [f:pain2026]
