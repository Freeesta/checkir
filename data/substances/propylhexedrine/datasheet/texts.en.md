## molecule

Propylhexedrine, 1-cyclohexyl-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Formula C10H21N, 155.28 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is methamphetamine with the phenyl ring saturated to a cyclohexyl ring; methamphetamine has its own sheet. [f:pubchem]

## category

## origin

A synthetic compound; nasal decongestant inhalers are the most common source of propylhexedrine, sold over the counter. [f:teja2020]
