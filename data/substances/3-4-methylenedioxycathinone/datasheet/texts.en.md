## molecule

3,4-Methylenedioxycathinone (MDC, bk-MDA), 2-amino-1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)propan-1-one. Base: C10H11NO3, 193.2 g/mol, CAS 80535-73-5. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (methylenedioxy, primary amine). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance of the cathinone group; it is also measured as a metabolite of methylone. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:sprega2023]

## metabolism

In a clinical trial with healthy volunteers who took methylone, 3,4-methylenedioxycathinone and 4-hydroxy-3-methoxy-N-methylcathinone (HMMC) were measured in oral fluid as metabolites of methylone. [f:sprega2023]

## risks

They can cause arrhythmia, ischaemia, myocardial injury and sudden cardiac death. [f:gresnigt2026]

## interactions
