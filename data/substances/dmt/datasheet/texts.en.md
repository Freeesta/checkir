## molecule

DMT, N,N-dimethyltryptamine, 2-(1H-indol-3-yl)-N,N-dimethylethanamine. Base: C12H16N2, 188.27 g/mol, CAS 61-50-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Structure: an indole with a dimethylaminoethyl chain; the amine is tertiary. Expected band: indole N-H at 3380-3490 cm-1. There is no reference spectrum of DMT in the public data, so this range only indicates the group. [f:coates2000] [f:nist-webbook]

Appearance in the street: resin, powder, tar-like mass or crystalline material. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

DMT, present in the South American beverage ayahuasca, has been used since ancient times in sociocultural and ritual contexts. [f:araujo2015]

## origin

DMT is a component of ayahuasca, a tea commonly obtained from Banisteriopsis caapi and Psychotria viridis, which combines it with MAO-inhibiting beta-carboline alkaloids. [f:riba2003]

The UBC guidelines also state that tryptamines of this kind are found in plants, animals and humans, but that street material is often of synthetic origin. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

## metabolism

By mouth DMT is labile: in ayahuasca, MAO-inhibiting beta-carboline alkaloids (harmine, harmaline, tetrahydroharmine) prevent its first-pass metabolism and let it reach the circulation. [f:riba2003]

After intravenous DMT the early plasma half-life was 5.0-5.8 minutes and the late half-life 14-16 minutes. [f:vogt2023]

After oral ayahuasca at 0.6 and 0.85 mg/kg of DMT the peak plasma concentrations were 12.14 and 17.44 ng/mL, at a median of 1.5 hours. [f:riba2003]

## effects

Intravenous boluses of 15 and 25 mg induced very intense psychedelic effects that peaked within 2 minutes. Infusions of 0.6 or 1 mg/min induced slowly increasing, dose-dependent effects that reached a plateau after 30 minutes; all effects subsided within 15 minutes of stopping. [f:vogt2023]

Oral ayahuasca produced subjective effects peaking between 1.5 and 2 hours, with perceptual changes and higher ratings of positive mood and activation. [f:riba2003]

## doses

Doses in the intravenous trial: boluses of 15 and 25 mg, or infusions of 0.6 and 1 mg/min. [f:vogt2023]

Doses in the ayahuasca trial: freeze-dried ayahuasca at 0.6 and 0.85 mg/kg of DMT, by mouth. [f:riba2003]

## risks

Both intravenous boluses produced more adverse subjective effects and more anxiety than the infusions. [f:vogt2023]

In a systematic review of 51 publications on DMT, 5-MeO-DMT and zalsupindole the common adverse events were nausea, headache and transient cardiovascular increases. [f:collyer2026]

Diastolic blood pressure rose by 9 mm Hg at 75 minutes after the high ayahuasca dose; systolic pressure and heart rate rose moderately and not significantly. [f:riba2003]

## interactions

By mouth DMT is active only with an MAO inhibitor, as in ayahuasca, where the beta-carboline alkaloids block its first-pass metabolism; with the high ayahuasca dose diastolic pressure rose by 9 mm Hg. [f:riba2003]

## counselling

Ask how it is taken. By mouth DMT works only with an MAO inhibitor, as in ayahuasca; the UBC guidelines add that after MAO inhibitors some everyday foods, such as cheese, can become dangerous. [f:riba2003] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Explain that in the intravenous trials effects peaked within 2 minutes and subsided within 15 minutes of stopping. The UBC guidelines say that with smoked DMT too the intoxication is brief, but side effects can last several hours, with driving ability impaired. [f:vogt2023] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Say that fast, high exposure was the harder experience: boluses gave more anxiety than infusions. The UBC guidelines advise a low first dose without a second one, a protected place and not using alone. [f:vogt2023] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Mention tolerance: in the infusion trial effects stayed stable from 30 to 90 minutes while plasma levels kept rising, a sign of acute tolerance; the UBC guidelines add that with regular use tolerance is high and doses may rise a lot. [f:vogt2023] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
