## molecule

Benzoylbenzylfentanyl, N-(1-benzylpiperidin-4-yl)-N-phenylbenzamide. Base: C25H26N2O, 370.5 g/mol, CAS 95869-86-6. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: fentanyl analogues (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Benzoylbenzylfentanyl is one of the fentanyl analogues reported to the European Monitoring Centre for Drugs and Drug Addiction in recent years. [f:rautio2023]

## origin

A synthetic fentanyl analogue. [f:rautio2023]

## metabolism

In human hepatocytes incubated for up to 5 h, benzoylbenzylfentanyl was mainly metabolised by N-dealkylation, forming the nor-metabolite. [f:rautio2023]

The nor-metabolite seems a suitable biomarker for benzoylbenzylfentanyl. [f:rautio2023]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]
