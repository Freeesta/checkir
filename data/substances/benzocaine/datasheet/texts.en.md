## risks

A single spray of benzocaine may induce methemoglobinemia, and clinical symptoms may appear at relatively low methemoglobin values. Reported complications include hypoxic encephalopathy, myocardial infarction and death. [f:guay2009]

In a Mayo Clinic series of 28 478 transesophageal echocardiograms with topical benzocaine, 19 patients developed methemoglobinemia (mean level 32 %), all with cyanosis and low oxygen saturation; 18 of them received methylene blue. [f:kane2007]

Systemic toxicity of local anaesthetics is a potentially fatal complication; a practice advisory of the American Society of Regional Anesthesia and Pain Medicine describes its prevention, diagnosis and treatment. [f:neal2010]
