## molecule

Tropacocaine, [(1S,5R)-8-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]octan-3-yl] benzoate. Formula C15H19NO2, 245.32 g/mol, CAS 537-26-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the benzoyl ester of pseudotropine (benzoylpseudotropine); the PubChem entry leaves the configuration at C-3 undefined. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tropane ester, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping, because it is an alkaloid of the coca leaf; it is not an opioid. Typical role in samples: residue of the plant. The effect axis follows cocaine: the pharmacology of the compound itself is not described in the sources consulted. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:laposchan2022] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A minor alkaloid of the coca plant, listed with cinnamoylcocaine and norcocaine among the impurities of illicit cocaine that originate from the plant. Cocaine (see the cocaine sheet) is the main alkaloid. [f:laposchan2022]

The ratio of tropacocaine (with the cinnamoylcocaines and norcocaine) to cocaine has been used to compare illicit cocaine samples. [f:janzen1992]
