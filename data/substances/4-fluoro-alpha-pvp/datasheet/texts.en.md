## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
