## molecule

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

The US DEA found that this substance and eight other fentanyl-related substances have been shown to bind and act as μ-opioid receptor agonists. [f:dea-fr2023-26694]
