## molecule

Propylphenidate, propyl 2-phenyl-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Formula C16H23NO2, 261.36 g/mol; the PubChem entry has unspecified stereocentres (threo and erythro forms exist). Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the propyl ester analogue of methylphenidate. [f:pubchem]
