## molecule

Fluorolintane (2-F-DPPy), 1-[1-(2-fluorophenyl)-2-phenylethyl]pyrrolidine. Formula C18H20FN, 269.4 g/mol, CAS 2149042-90-8. Identity checked against PubChem; the entry gives no stereochemistry and the compound is racemic. [f:pubchem] [f:dybek2019]

It is a 1,2-diarylethylamine, with no ketone and no cyclohexane ring. With the fluorine on the phenyl or on the benzyl ring there are six possible racemic positional isomers (2-, 3-, 4-F-DPPy and 2"-, 3"-, 4"-F-DPPy); the synthesis and analytical characterisation of fluorolintane and five of them is reported. [f:pubchem] [f:dybek2019]

No band value is given here because no verified reference spectrum is cited. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances (1,2-diarylethylamine). The arylcyclohexylamine family sheet does not cover diarylethylamines, so no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:wallach2019] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Fluorolintane has recently appeared as a research chemical, sold with purported dissociative effects intended to resemble phencyclidine (PCP) and ketamine. [f:wallach2019] [f:dybek2019]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a 1,2-diarylethylamine like diphenidine and methoxphenidine. [f:wallach2019]
