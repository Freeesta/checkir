## molecule

5F-EMB-PINACA (5F-AEB), ethyl (2S)-2-[[1-(5-fluoropentyl)indazole-3-carbonyl]amino]-3-methylbutanoate (l-valine ethyl ester amide). Base: C20H28FN3O3, 377.5 g/mol, CAS 2365471-51-6. Identity checked against PubChem (the (S) entry, listed as 5F-EMB-PINACA and 5F-AEB). It is the ethyl ester analogue of 5F-AMB and has the same formula as 5F-MDMB-PINACA (sheet 5f-mdmb-pinaca), from which it differs in structure. [f:pubchem] [f:wang2023]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist (indazole-3-carboxamide of a valine ethyl ester with a fluoropentyl chain). [f:jones2025] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In pooled human liver microsomes the most abundant 5F-EMB-PINACA metabolites came from ester hydrolysis plus further steps such as oxidative defluorination and hydroxylation; the extensive phase I metabolism is mediated by several isozymes, which makes interaction unlikely. [f:gampfer2021]

The same ethyl ester forms from 5F-MMB-PINACA in the presence of ethanol by carboxylesterase-mediated transesterification, and shares its hydrolysed metabolite with its parent. [f:wang2023]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

In HepG2 cells 5F-EMB-PINACA showed moderate cytotoxic effects, and hepatotoxicity cannot be excluded. [f:gampfer2021]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
