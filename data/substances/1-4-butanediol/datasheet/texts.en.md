## molecule

1,4-Butanediol, butane-1,4-diol. C4H10O2, 90.12 g/mol, CAS 110-63-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An unbranched C4 diol with a primary hydroxyl group at each end and no carbonyl group. [f:pubchem]


## category

Chemical class: others. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

It is a metabolic precursor of GHB, as is gamma-butyrolactone (GBL), and the three are described as central depressants used for euphoric and relaxant effects. [f:skryabin2023]

## origin

An industrial solvent, also misused for its euphoric effects. [f:liakoni2019]


## metabolism

In humans 1,4-butanediol is converted to GHB by alcohol dehydrogenase (ADH): in six healthy volunteers pretreatment with the ADH inhibitor fomepizole raised the peak plasma concentration and the AUC of 1,4-butanediol, which confirms ADH as the primary pathway. Effects, risks and further metabolism of GHB are on the ghb sheet. [f:liakoni2019]
