## history

URB602 inhibits recombinant rat MAGL, the enzyme that degrades the endocannabinoid 2-arachidonoylglycerol, only weakly (IC50 223 μM). [f:king2007]

In rat brain membranes it also inhibited the hydrolysis of anandamide (IC50 17 μM), so it is not selective for 2-oleoylglycerol hydrolysis. [f:vandevoorde2007]
