## molecule

5-MeO-BFE (dimemebfe), 2-(5-methoxy-1-benzofuran-3-yl)-N,N-dimethylethanamine. Formula C13H17NO2, 219.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The benzofuran analogue of 5-MeO-DMT: the indole nitrogen is replaced by an oxygen, with a 5-methoxy group and a dimethylaminoethyl chain on carbon 3. [f:pubchem]
