## molecule

MDEA (MDE, "Eve"), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-N-ethylpropan-2-amine. Base: C12H17NO2, 207.27 g/mol, CAS 82801-81-8. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

Forms: usually tablets, only occasionally powder; the most common salt of the methylenedioxy-type stimulants is the hydrochloride, but phosphate and bromide salts are also seen. [f:unodc-ats2006]

Diagnostic bands: the methylenedioxy group gives the characteristic C-O-C bands of ethers, but it is shared with MDMA and MDA, so a band of the group does not tell the three apart; compare the full spectrum with the reference one. [f:smith-ethers] [f:unodc-ats2006]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, methylenedioxy subgroup. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Synthetic compound. The precursor classes are those of MDMA: 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanone, safrole (also via isosafrole) and piperonal. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## metabolism

MDEA is metabolised mainly by N-deethylation, to MDA, and by demethylenation, to dihydroxyethylamphetamine. The highest contributions came from CYP3A4 and CYP2D6 at concentrations like those of recreational users; both steps preferred the S-enantiomer, particularly with CYP2C19. [f:meyer2009mdea]

MDA, which MDEA gives by N-deethylation, is itself a drug found on the illicit market. [f:meyer2009mda]

## effects

MDEA can evoke psychomotor stimulation, mild alterations of perception, sensations of closeness and a positive emotional state, as well as sympathomimetic physical effects. [f:freudenmann2004]

In a double-blind, placebo-controlled study with 140 mg orally in 14 healthy volunteers, MDEA produced a partially controllable state of enhanced insight, empathy and peaceful feelings, with general stimulation, increased psychomotor drive and talkativeness. [f:hermle1993]

## doses

The drug content of tablets of the methylenedioxy-type stimulants (MDMA, MDA, MDEA) usually ranges from 40 to 140 mg and varies with the region and over time. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## risks

In the study with 140 mg, one of the 14 volunteers developed a toxic psychosis, one had a dysphoric reaction and one had episodes of anxiety for some days. [f:hermle1993]

Case reports of fatalities with toxicologically confirmed MDEA exist; for serotonergic neurotoxicity the MDEA-specific information comes from animal studies only, and the potential in humans is difficult to estimate. [f:freudenmann2004]

## counselling

Ask what the person thinks they have: tablets sold as ecstasy are often MDMA, MDA or MDEA, structurally similar substances whose effects differ in duration, intensity and dose, so it matters to know which one is being taken. [f:unodc-ats2006] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about past psychiatric episodes and about anxiety: in the study with 140 mg one volunteer in 14 developed a toxic psychosis, one a dysphoric reaction and one days of anxiety. [f:hermle1993]
