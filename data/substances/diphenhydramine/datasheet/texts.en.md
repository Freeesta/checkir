## molecule

Diphenhydramine, 2-benzhydryloxy-N,N-dimethylethanamine. Formula C17H21NO, 255.35 g/mol, CAS 58-73-1. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. It is a widely used over-the-counter antihistamine with a sedative effect. The effect axis has no antihistamine class: "depressant/sedative" is a checkir classification. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant, as a cutting agent in heroin. [f:akutsu2006] [f:lockwood2021] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, sold over the counter as an antihistamine. [f:akutsu2006]

## metabolism

N-demethylation is a main metabolic pathway of diphenhydramine. In vitro, CYP2D6 had the highest N-demethylation activity (Km 1.12 ± 0.21 µM) and CYP1A2, CYP2C9 and CYP2C19 acted as low-affinity components. [f:akutsu2006]

Diphenhydramine is not only an inhibitor but also a high-affinity substrate of CYP2D6. These are in vitro data; no half-life is listed. [f:akutsu2006]

## cuts

Diphenhydramine is described as a common cutting agent in heroin. In a laboratory test, samples at or above 1 mg/mL gave false positives for fentanyl on 20 ng/mL fentanyl test strips. [f:lockwood2021]
