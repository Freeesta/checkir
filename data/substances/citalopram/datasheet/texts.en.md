## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a racemic bicyclic phthalane marketed as an SSRI antidepressant in tablets. [f:citalopram-label]

## metabolism

Citalopram is metabolized to demethylcitalopram, didemethylcitalopram, citalopram-N-oxide and a deaminated propionic acid derivative; in vitro CYP3A4 and CYP2C19 are the primary enzymes in the N-demethylation. [f:citalopram-label]

At steady state the plasma concentrations of the two demethylated metabolites are about one half and one tenth of the parent drug, which is at least 8 times more potent in inhibiting serotonin reuptake; the mean terminal half-life is about 35 h. [f:citalopram-label]
