## molecule

Ergonovine, (6aR,9R)-N-[(2S)-1-hydroxypropan-2-yl]-7-methyl-6,6a,8,9-tetrahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide. Formula C19H23N3O2, 325.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a lysergic acid amide with an (S)-1-hydroxypropan-2-yl group on the amide nitrogen; the PubChem name is the 6aR,9R ergoline form. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: lysergamides. It is not a hallucinogen: its pharmacological use is uterotonic, so no effect on perception is expected and the tryptamines family sheet (classic hallucinogens) is not attached. [f:degroot1998] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

An ergot alkaloid, used as a uterotonic in obstetrics. [f:degroot1998]
