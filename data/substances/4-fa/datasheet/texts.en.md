## molecule

4-FA (4-fluoroamphetamine), 1-(4-fluorophenyl)propan-2-amine. Base: C9H12FN, 153.20 g/mol, CAS 459-02-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem] Hydrochloride: C9H12FN·HCl, 189 g/mol, melting point 156.9 °C, CAS 64609-06-9, white powder. [f:swgdrug-4fa]

Diagnostic bands of the hydrochloride (ATR): N-H3+ envelope with bands from 2983 to 2582 cm-1, ring at 1601 and 1500 cm-1 (the strongest band), intense bands at 1215 and 1203 cm-1 where the C-F stretch is expected, and below 900 cm-1 the bands at 847 and 831 cm-1 (para-disubstituted ring), 766, 555 and 498 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-4fa] [f:smith-amine-salts] [f:smith-halogen] [f:smith-benzene]

Isomers: a para-substituted ring shows one C-H wag between 860 and 790 cm-1 and no ring bend near 690 cm-1, unlike meta rings (wag at 810-750 cm-1 with a ring bend) and monosubstituted ones; this helps to tell 4-FA from 3-FA and amphetamine. [f:smith-benzene]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant and entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

4-FA reached the Dutch drug market in 2007 and its use has kept growing, possibly because users see it as "ecstasy light". [f:hondebrink2018]

The Dutch Monitor Drug-related Incidents registered 939 patients with 4-FA intoxication between January 2009 and June 2017. [f:wijers2018]

## origin

A synthetic substance: the para-fluoro analogue of amphetamine. [f:rickli2015]

## metabolism

After 100 mg (12 volunteers) or 150 mg (5 volunteers) by mouth, 4-FA appeared in urine at 0.7-38 mg/L, with the creatinine-normalised maximum 4 h after ingestion. [f:toennes2020]

Two pathways were identified, at very low concentrations: two diastereomers of 4-fluorophenylpropanolamine and a ring-hydroxylated 4-FA, largely conjugated. [f:toennes2020]

## effects

The clinical effects are comparable to those of amphetamine and MDMA. [f:nugteren2015]

In vitro 4-FA is more serotonergic than amphetamine and releases noradrenaline and dopamine and, like MDMA, serotonin. [f:rickli2015]

## doses

The UBC guidelines give 50-150 mg as the range of use; in a controlled study 12 volunteers took 100 mg and 5 took 150 mg by mouth. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines] [f:toennes2020]

## risks

Of 45 patients with 4-FA exposure reported to the Dutch Poisons Information Center in 2016, all had adverse effects and 8 severe toxicity: 2 fatalities, 4 cerebral haemorrhages (1 fatal), 2 inverted Takotsubo cardiomyopathies, 1 myocardial infarction and 1 acute heart failure, with marked hypertension and tachycardia. [f:hondebrink2018]

Two patients with confirmed 4-FA use had haemorrhagic stroke; both presented with headache and mild hypertension. [f:wijers2018]

In an emergency department, 4-FA mono-intoxication came with headache in 80 % of patients (MDMA 3.3 %) and a systolic pressure of 164 ± 31 mm Hg (MDMA 139 ± 19 mm Hg). [f:gresnigt2022]

## interactions

Six deaths followed the combination of 4-FA and 25C-NBOMe; both can raise serotonin and act as strong stimulants. [f:gerostamoulos2023]

With antidepressants and ADHD medication, interactions are anticipated on pharmacodynamic grounds (monoamine transporters and monoamine oxidases); pharmacokinetic interactions remain speculative, with a single case report. [f:inan2020]

## counselling

Ask whether the person tends to reload: the UBC guidelines suggest waiting at least an hour to judge the effect and say that redosing does not bring the wanted effects and can worsen side effects and recovery; users see 4-FA as "ecstasy light", yet severe toxicity was relatively frequent in a Dutch poisons-centre cohort. [f:hondebrink2018] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Signs to recognise: severe headache, marked hypertension; in two haemorrhagic strokes headache and mild hypertension came first, and the authors advise considering brain imaging for severe headache even without neurological signs. [f:wijers2018]

Ask about high blood pressure and heart disease: the UBC guidelines say that people with hypertension, heart, circulatory, liver or kidney problems or hyperthyroidism should not take these substances; 4-FA raised systolic pressure more than MDMA. [f:gresnigt2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about alcohol and other drugs: the UBC guidelines advise giving up polydrug use because interactions are practically unknown; 4-FA was taken alone in 44 % of cases, with alcohol in 13 % and with other drugs in 43 %. [f:wijers2018] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
