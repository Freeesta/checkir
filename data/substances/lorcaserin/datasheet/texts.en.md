## molecule

Lorcaserin, (5R)-7-chloro-5-methyl-2,3,4,5-tetrahydro-1H-3-benzazepine. Formula C11H14ClN, 195.69 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a benzazepine, a selective serotonin 5-HT2C receptor agonist that increases satiety. Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:sharma2020] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a centrally acting selective 5-HT2C receptor agonist. [f:sharma2020]

## metabolism

Lorcaserin has two major metabolites: the N-carbamoyl glucuronide, excreted in urine, and lorcaserin N-sulfamate, which circulates in the blood. [f:sharma2020]
