## metabolism

In the labelled mass-balance study with tritiated drug, temazepam was well absorbed with minimal (8%) first-pass metabolism; the only significant metabolite in blood was the O-conjugate and no active metabolites were formed. [f:restoril-label]

The decline in blood was biphasic: short half-life 0.4-0.6 h, terminal half-life 3.5-18.4 h (mean 8.8 h) depending on population and method; the metabolites were formed with a half-life of 10 h and excreted with about 2 h. [f:restoril-label]
