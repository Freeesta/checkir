## origin

A synthetic tricyclic compound, marketed as tablets for the treatment of depression. [f:norpramin-label]

## metabolism

Desipramine is metabolized in the liver and about 70% is excreted in the urine. The label names 2-hydroxydesipramine as a metabolite; its ratio to desipramine may be increased in the elderly. [f:norpramin-label]

The rate of metabolism varies widely between individuals, chiefly on a genetically determined basis: up to a 36-fold difference in plasma level is noted at the same oral dose. [f:norpramin-label]
