## molecule

Lorazepam, 7-chloro-5-(2-chlorophenyl)-3-hydroxy-1,3-dihydro-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one. Formula C15H10Cl2N2O2, 321.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, described as a widely used hypnosedative-anxiolytic benzodiazepine. [f:uchaipichat2013]

## metabolism

Lorazepam is cleared mainly by conjugation with glucuronic acid at its 3-hydroxy group; in vitro UGT2B15 is likely to play an important role, together with UGT2B4 and UGT2B7. [f:uchaipichat2013]

Healthy volunteers with the UGT2B15*2/*2 genotype showed 0.58-fold lower systemic clearance of intravenous lorazepam than those with *1/*1. [f:chung2005]

The mean plasma half-life is about 12 hours for unconjugated lorazepam and about 18 hours for lorazepam glucuronide, which has no demonstrable central nervous system activity in animals. [f:lorazepam-label]
