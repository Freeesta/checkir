## molecule

Phenetrazine (3-ethyl-2-phenylmorpholine). Base: C12H17NO, 191.27 g/mol, CAS 100368-98-7. Identity checked on PubChem: the name "phenetrazine" gives 3-ethyl-2-phenylmorpholine, the ethyl homologue of phenmetrazine (3-methyl), not phenmetrazine itself. [f:pubchem]

It has two chiral centres; the flat structure without stereochemistry is used here. [f:pubchem]
