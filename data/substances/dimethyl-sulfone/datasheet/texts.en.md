## molecule

Dimethyl sulfone, also called methylsulfonylmethane or MSM (methylsulfonylmethane), formula C2H6O2S, molar mass 94.14 g/mol, CAS 67-71-0. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI, which agree on formula and InChIKey. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

## category

Chemical class: organosulfur compound (grouped here under other substances). Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: diluent. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## cuts

A drug checking data page of the University of Washington counts MSM as the most common substance used to cut methamphetamine in Washington State, USA, and notes that it is best known as a dietary supplement. [f:uw-adai-methamphetamine2025]

## risks

In dietary use, a review reports that MSM is well tolerated by most people at up to four grams daily with few and mild side effects. This describes a known dietary amount and does not describe an unknown amount in a sample. [f:butawan2017]
