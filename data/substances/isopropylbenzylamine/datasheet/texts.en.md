## molecule

Isopropylbenzylamine (INBA), N-benzylpropan-2-amine. Formula C10H15N, 149.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is an isomer of methamphetamine: same formula, different carbon chain; methamphetamine has its own sheet. [f:pubchem] [f:xu2022]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:lai2024] [f:checkir-classification]

It is used to adulterate methamphetamine and sold as fake «ice»; it is a cut of methamphetamine. [f:xu2022]

## origin

A synthetic compound that stands in for methamphetamine in seized samples. [f:lai2024] [f:xu2022]
