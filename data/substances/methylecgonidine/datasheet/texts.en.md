## molecule

Methylecgonidine, methyl (1R,5S)-8-methyl-8-azabicyclo[3.2.1]oct-2-ene-2-carboxylate. Formula C10H15NO2, 181.23 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tropane esters, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping, because it derives from cocaine; it is not an opioid. Typical role in samples: degradation residue. The effect axis follows the parent compound cocaine: the pharmacology of methylecgonidine itself is only partly described (partial agonist at muscarinic M1 and M3 receptors). This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:romero-norena2024] [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

In 1990 it was reported in the urine of people who smoked cocaine under laboratory conditions, with little or none after intravenous or intranasal cocaine; it was proposed as a marker of cocaine (crack) smoking. [f:jacob1990]

## origin

It is the main pyrolysis product of smoked cocaine (crack, cocaine base paste, freebase), formed when cocaine is heated; see the cocaine sheet. [f:romero-norena2024]

## metabolism

Methylecgonidine is metabolised mainly in the liver, with a half-life of about one hour, and is excreted mainly in the urine. [f:romero-norena2024]
