## molecule

Imipramine, 3-(5,6-dihydrobenzo[b][1]benzazepin-11-yl)-N,N-dimethylpropan-1-amine. Formula C19H24N2, 280.4 g/mol, CAS 50-49-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a member of the dibenzazepine group, supplied as the hydrochloride. [f:imipramine-label]

## category

Chemical class: tricyclic antidepressant, dibenzazepine derivative (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: depressant or sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:imipramine-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: the original tricyclic antidepressant, a member of the dibenzazepine group, supplied in tablets. [f:imipramine-label]

## metabolism

The exposure to imipramine is affected by the genotypes of both polymorphic enzymes CYP2D6 and CYP2C19, whereas that of desipramine is affected by CYP2D6 only. [f:hicks2017]

About 7% to 10% of Caucasians are CYP2D6 poor metabolizers, who have higher than expected plasma concentrations of tricyclic antidepressants at usual doses, up to an 8-fold increase in plasma AUC. [f:imipramine-label]
