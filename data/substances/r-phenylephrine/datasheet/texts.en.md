## molecule

(R)-Phenylephrine, 3-[(1R)-1-hydroxy-2-(methylamino)ethyl]phenol. Formula C9H13NO2, 167.20 g/mol, CAS 59-42-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the (R) enantiomer, with the hydroxyl on the meta position of the phenyl ring. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: active principle or pharmacological adulterant. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, marketed as a medicine with a pressor action (the label used here is for an injectable form). [f:phenylephrine-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

Phenylephrine is extensively metabolised by the liver: only 12% of the dose was excreted unchanged in urine. Deamination by monoamine oxidase is the primary pathway and gives m-hydroxymandelic acid, 57% of the total dose. [f:phenylephrine-label]

After an intravenous infusion the effective half-life was approximately 5 minutes. [f:phenylephrine-label]
