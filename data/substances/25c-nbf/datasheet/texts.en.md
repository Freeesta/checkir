## molecule

25C-NBF, 2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-fluorophenyl)methyl]ethanamine. Formula C17H19ClFNO2, 323.8 g/mol, CAS 1373879-23-2. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It has a 2-fluorobenzyl group on the nitrogen of 2C-C, in place of the 2-methoxybenzyl group of the NBOMes; it is an N-(2-fluorobenzyl) phenethylamine of the 2C series. [f:pubchem] [f:nadal2026]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines (N-benzyl phenethylamines); outside the NBOMe family, which covers only the 2-methoxybenzyl derivatives. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## history

25C-NBF is described as a newly synthesised phenethylamine-type new psychoactive substance. [f:hur2020]

It was later studied in rodents, together with 25B-NBF and 25I-NBF, for its pharmacological profile and antidepressant potential. [f:nadal2026]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-(2-fluorobenzyl) derivative of 2C-C. [f:nadal2026]
