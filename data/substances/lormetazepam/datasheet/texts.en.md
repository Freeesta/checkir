## molecule

Lormetazepam, 7-chloro-5-(2-chlorophenyl)-3-hydroxy-1-methyl-3H-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one. Formula C16H12Cl2N2O2, 335.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a 3-hydroxy-1,4-benzodiazepine; the cited study used it as the medicinal product Noctamid. [f:hildebrand1990]

## metabolism

Lormetazepam is excreted in urine mainly as its 3-OH glucuronide; the glucuronide of lorazepam, the N-demethylated metabolite, was about 5-14% of the dose fraction excreted as lormetazepam glucuronide (five healthy volunteers and five patients with cirrhosis). [f:hildebrand1990]
