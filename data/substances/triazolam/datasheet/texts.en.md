## metabolism

The first step is hydroxylation mediated by cytochrome P450 3A (CYP3A), giving 1-hydroxytriazolam and 4-hydroxytriazolam, which are then conjugated to glucuronides. [f:triazolam-label]

The conjugates, presumably inactive, are excreted mainly in the urine, with little unchanged triazolam; the two primary metabolites accounted for 79.9% of urinary excretion. The mean plasma elimination half-life is 1.5-5.5 h. [f:triazolam-label]
