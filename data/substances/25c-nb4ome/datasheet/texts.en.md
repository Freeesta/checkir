## molecule

25C-NB4OMe, 2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(4-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethanamine. Formula C18H22ClNO3, 335.8 g/mol, CAS 1566571-35-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a positional isomer of 25C-NBOMe (same formula C18H22ClNO3): the methoxy group on the benzyl ring sits at position 4 instead of 2. It is a distinct substance with its own InChIKey and must not be confused with 25C-NBOMe. [f:pubchem]

The methoxybenzyl isomers change the substitution bands of the benzene ring below 900 cm-1; no band value is given because no verified reference spectrum is cited. [f:smith-benzene]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines (N-benzyl phenethylamines); outside the NBOMe family, which covers only the 2-methoxybenzyl derivatives. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:checkir-classification]
