## molecule

Thebaine (paramorphine), (4R,7aR,12bS)-7,9-dimethoxy-3-methyl-2,4,7a,13-tetrahydro-1H-4,12-methanobenzofuro[3,2-e]isoquinoline. Formula C19H21NO3, 311.4 g/mol, CAS 115-37-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

In 40 people poisoned by tea made from thebaine-rich poppy seeds, the picture was neuromuscular excitation with muscle spasm, rigidity and convulsions, like strychnine poisoning. [f:isoardi2023]

## origin

A natural alkaloid of the opium poppy (Papaver somniferum), found in its latex; the top1 poppy mutant accumulates thebaine and oripavine, the precursors of morphine and codeine. [f:millgate2004] [f:eisenreich2019]

Buprenorphine is a semi-synthetic opioid derived from thebaine. [f:elkader2005]

## metabolism

In three patients poisoned by poppy seed tea the apparent elimination half-life of thebaine in blood was 8.3, 11.6 and 14.8 h; acute kidney injury may prolong it. No metabolic pathway is given by the sources consulted. [f:penafiel2023]

Thebaine is not present in heroin, and after poppy seed consumption it stays detectable in urine for up to 72 h. [f:bollini2022]
