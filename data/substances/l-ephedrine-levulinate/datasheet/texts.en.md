## molecule

L-Ephedrine levulinate, (1R,2S)-2-(methylamino)-1-phenylpropyl 4-oxopentanoate. Covalent ester (O-levulinoyl ephedrine), not a salt: PubChem shows the ester bond. C15H21NO3, 263.33 g/mol, CAS 1082659-36-6. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amfetamine. Expected effect: stimolante. Typical role in samples: principio attivo. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound: the hydroxyl of l-ephedrine esterified with levulinic acid (4-oxopentanoic acid). Not registered as a salt form of the existing sheet of ephedrine. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]
