## molecule

Eszopiclone, [(7S)-6-(5-chloro-2-pyridinyl)-5-oxo-7H-pyrrolo[3,4-b]pyrazin-7-yl] 4-methylpiperazine-1-carboxylate. Formula C17H17ClN6O3, 388.8 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a cyclopyrrolone ('Z-drug'), not a benzodiazepine, and the benzodiazepine family sheet does not cover non-benzodiazepine GABA-A agonists, so no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:najib2006] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a nonbenzodiazepine cyclopyrrolone sedative-hypnotic used for insomnia. [f:najib2006]

## metabolism

Eszopiclone is extensively metabolized by oxidation and demethylation; the primary plasma metabolites are (S)-zopiclone-N-oxide, which shows no significant binding to the GABA receptor, and (S)-N-desmethyl zopiclone, which binds with substantially lower potency. In vitro CYP3A4 and CYP2E1 are involved. [f:lunesta-label]

The terminal elimination half-life is about 6 hours; less than 10% of an oral dose is excreted in urine as racemic zopiclone. [f:najib2006]
