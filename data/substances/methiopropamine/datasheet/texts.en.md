## molecule

MPA (methiopropamine), N-methyl-1-(thiophen-2-yl)propan-2-amine. Base: C8H13NS, 155.27 g/mol, CAS 801156-47-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the thiophene analogue of methamphetamine: the benzene ring is replaced by a thiophene. [f:welter2013]

## category

Chemical class: Amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound sold online as a "research chemical"; the first samples were seized by the German police in 2011. [f:welter2013]

## metabolism

In rat and human urine the main pathways are N-demethylation, hydroxylation at the side chain and at the thiophene ring, and combinations of these, followed by glucuronidation and/or sulfation; CYP1A2, CYP2C19, CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 are the main phase I enzymes. [f:welter2013]

In mice, after 10 mg/kg intraperitoneally, MPA was extensively oxidized in the first 9 h mainly to nor-MPA, oxo-MPA and two hydroxylated metabolites; all phase I metabolites underwent phase II metabolism. [f:camuto2021]
