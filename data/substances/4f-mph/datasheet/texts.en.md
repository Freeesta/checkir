## molecule

4F-MPH (4-fluoromethylphenidate), methyl 2-(4-fluorophenyl)-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Formula C14H18FNO2, 251.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem; the entry used has no stereochemistry. [f:pubchem]

It is a ring-fluorinated analogue of methylphenidate. Two powders bought online in 2015 were (±)-threo-4F-MPH in one case and a mixture of (±)-threo and (±)-erythro isomers in the other. [f:mclaughlin2017]

## category

Chemical class: phenidates. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound sold online as a research chemical, an analogue of methylphenidate. [f:mclaughlin2017]

## metabolism

In rat urine 13 metabolites of 4-FMPH were found (12 phase I and 1 phase II). In pooled human S9 incubations the ester was primarily hydrolysed to the corresponding carboxy metabolite, which the authors propose as an analytical target besides the parent compound. [f:manier2020]
