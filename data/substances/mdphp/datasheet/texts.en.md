## molecule

3,4-Methylenedioxy-α-pyrrolidinohexanophenone (MDPHP), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-pyrrolidin-1-ylhexan-1-one. Base: C17H23NO3, 289.4 g/mol, CAS 776994-64-0. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (methylenedioxy). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

MDPHP is a synthetic cathinone closely related to MDPV; in recent years it has drawn attention for the increase in seizures and intoxications in Europe and Italy. [f:bassi2024]

## origin

A synthetic substance: a synthetic cathinone with a methylenedioxy ring, a pyrrolidine group and an alkyl portion of six carbon atoms. [f:casati2025]

## metabolism

Phase I metabolites were identified in urine from authentic cases: demethylenation followed by methylation, oxidation of the pyrrolidine ring, N,N-bisdealkylation of the pyrrolidine to the primary amine and hydroxylation of the aliphatic side chain, also detected in combination. [f:brueckner2025]
