## molecule

5-APDB (5-(2-aminopropyl)-2,3-dihydrobenzofuran), 1-(2,3-dihydro-1-benzofuran-5-yl)propan-2-amine. Base: C11H15NO, 177.24 g/mol, CAS 152624-03-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The furan ring is saturated (2,3-dihydro), unlike 5-APB. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a dihydrobenzofuran analogue of amphetamine, described as a designer drug of the phenethylamine and amphetamine class. [f:park2016]

## metabolism

In rat and human liver microsomes and human hepatocytes, 5-APDB gave three metabolites: two hydroxylated on the benzofuran moiety and a third, described as reduced, which may derive from either by dehydration. [f:park2016]
