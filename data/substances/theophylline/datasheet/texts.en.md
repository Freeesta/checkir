## molecule

Theophylline, 1,3-dimethyl-7H-purine-2,6-dione. Formula C7H8N4O2, 180.16 g/mol, CAS 58-55-9. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: methylxanthine, marketed as a bronchodilator (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant. [f:theophylline-label] [f:checkir-classification]

Theophylline, with sibutramine and caffeine, was confirmed in herbal products sold for weight loss. [f:pascali2018]

## origin

Structurally a methylxanthine, marketed in extended-release tablets for chronic asthma and other chronic lung diseases. [f:theophylline-label]

## metabolism

About 90% of the dose is metabolized in the liver: demethylation to 1-methylxanthine and 3-methylxanthine (CYP1A2) and hydroxylation to 1,3-dimethyluric acid (CYP2E1, CYP3A3); about 6% is N-methylated to caffeine. 3-Methylxanthine and caffeine are the only active metabolites. [f:theophylline-label]

In healthy non-smoking adults aged 16 to 60 the mean half-life is 8.7 h (range 6.1 to 12.8). [f:theophylline-label]
