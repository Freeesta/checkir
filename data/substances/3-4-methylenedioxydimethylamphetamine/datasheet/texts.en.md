## molecule

MDDMA, 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-N,N-dimethylpropan-2-amine. Formula C12H17NO2, 207.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the tertiary amine N,N-dimethyl analogue of MDA; the secondary amine MDMA and the primary amine MDA have their own sheets. It is not the cathinone bk-MDDMA (dimethylone, own sheet). [f:pubchem]
