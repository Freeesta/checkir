## molecule

Meclonazepam, (3S)-5-(2-chlorophenyl)-3-methyl-7-nitro-1,3-dihydro-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one. Formula C16H12ClN3O3, 329.74 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Meclonazepam is a benzodiazepine patented in 1977 to treat parasitic worms, which later appeared as a designer benzodiazepine and drug of abuse. [f:vikingsson2017]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a nitro-benzodiazepine patented as an anthelmintic. [f:vikingsson2017]

## metabolism

The main metabolites in human urine were amino-meclonazepam and acetamido-meclonazepam, with minor peaks of the parent drug, consistent with other nitro-containing benzodiazepines such as clonazepam, flunitrazepam and nitrazepam. [f:vikingsson2017]

In an earlier study 7-acetaminomeclonazepam was the most abundant urinary metabolite and a good target for urine testing. [f:meyer2016]
