## molecule

5-MAPB (5-(2-methylaminopropyl)benzofuran), 1-(1-benzofuran-5-yl)-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Base: C12H15NO, 189.25 g/mol, CAS 1354631-77-8. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem] It is the N-methyl derivative of 5-APB; 6-MAPB is the N-methyl derivative of the isomer 6-APB. [f:brandt2020]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The 2-aminopropylbenzofurans were sold as analogues of amphetamines; the 5-isomers came first and the 6-isomers appeared afterwards. [f:welter2015]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a benzofuran analogue of methamphetamine. [f:brandt2020]

## effects

In rat brain synaptosomes 5-MAPB was a substrate-type releaser at the dopamine, norepinephrine and serotonin transporters, with nanomolar potency and at least threefold more potent than MDA and MDMA. [f:brandt2020]

The 2-aminopropylbenzofurans were consumed for their euphoric and empathogenic effects. [f:welter2015]

## risks

In a fatal co-intoxication, 5-MAPB was 101 ng/mL and alpha-methyltryptamine 4690 ng/mL in post-mortem peripheral blood, with benzodiazepines, ephedrine and norephedrine also present. [f:epain2024]

In a death after two other powders (MDAI and 5-EAPB), 5-MAPB and 5-APB were also found in blood, at 0.089 and 0.546 mg/L. [f:deville2019]

## interactions

In the fatal case above 5-MAPB was found with alpha-methyltryptamine, benzodiazepines, ephedrine and norephedrine; the authors reviewed the literature to interpret the likely role of each molecule in the death. [f:epain2024]

## counselling

Ask what name the person was given: the benzofurans come as position isomers (5- and 6-) and as N-substituted analogues, which routine methods tell apart only with specific procedures. [f:welter2015]

Explain that in rat tests the benzofurans were at least threefold more potent than MDA and MDMA in evoking release; the sources read give no human potency data. [f:brandt2020]

Ask about other substances: in a fatal case 5-MAPB was found with alpha-methyltryptamine and benzodiazepines; avoid presenting this as a causal link. [f:epain2024]
