## ftir_limits

The spectrum of a mixture reflects its main components. Components below about 5 % of the sample are generally not detectable, and FTIR does not measure concentration. Potent substances active at very low doses, such as fentanyl and its analogues (when found, usually at about 3-10 % w/w) and nitazenes, cannot be ruled out by this analysis. A match is a proposal for an expert to evaluate, not a diagnosis, and it holds only if the identification is correct; complementary tests are advisable. [f:checkir-ftir-limits] Paracetamol and phenacetin are structurally close aromatic amides with similar spectra; telling them apart in a mixture needs the whole band pattern, not a single band.

## risks

Hepatic injury and liver failure can follow both intentional and non-intentional overdose of paracetamol (acetaminophen). In the United States it accounts for more than 50 % of overdose-related acute liver failure. [f:yoon2016]

The clinical presentation of paracetamol hepatotoxicity might vary, and N-acetylcysteine therapy is considered the mainstay of treatment. [f:yoon2016]
