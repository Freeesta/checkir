## risks

Hepatic injury and liver failure can follow both intentional and non-intentional overdose of paracetamol (acetaminophen). In the United States it accounts for more than 50 % of overdose-related acute liver failure. [f:yoon2016]

The clinical presentation of paracetamol hepatotoxicity might vary, and N-acetylcysteine therapy is considered the mainstay of treatment. [f:yoon2016]
