## molecule

Isopropyl-U-47700, 3,4-dichloro-N-[(1R,2R)-2-(dimethylamino)cyclohexyl]-N-propan-2-ylbenzamide. Base: C18H26Cl2N2O, 357.3 g/mol, CAS 2748319-16-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances (checkir classification; the UNODC grouping does not name this structure). Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Isopropyl-U-47700 was among more than 20 emerging novel psychoactive substances detected for the first time by reanalysing more than 3000 forensic sample extracts. [f:krotulski2019-mining]

The class of U-47700 was developed in pharmaceutical research programmes in the 1970s, and its members have recently become of concern for overdoses and deaths. [f:fogarty2022-u48800]

## origin

A synthetic substance of the U-47700 class (3,4-dichlorobenzamide cyclohexylamines), not a fentanyl analogue. [f:fogarty2022-u48800]

## effects

In a [35S]GTPγS assay at the μ- and κ-opioid receptors, eight U-opioids including isopropyl-U-47700 were less potent than the reference compounds (hydromorphone and fentanyl at the μ receptor); among them U-47700 was the most potent at the μ receptor, with an EC50 of 111 nM. [f:otte2022]

## doses

For the U-opioids data on toxicology and toxicokinetics are scarce, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:otte2022]

## risks

Overdoses of the U-opioids can lead to respiratory depression and result in death. [f:otte2022]

U-47700-class opioids are known or suspected in illicit drug markets, and fatal cases with other drugs in combination have been described for related compounds. [f:fogarty2022-u48800]

## interactions

Benzodiazepines and other sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
