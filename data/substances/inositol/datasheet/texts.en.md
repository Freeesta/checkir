## risks

A review of non-clinical and clinical data on myo-inositol found that only the highest dose studied (12 g per day) induced mild gastrointestinal side effects such as nausea, flatus and diarrhoea. [f:carlomagno2011]

These data come from studies of administered myo-inositol and do not describe an unknown mixture. [f:carlomagno2011]
