## molecule

DMAA (1,3-dimethylamylamine), 4-methylhexan-2-amine. Formula C7H17N, 115.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A branched aliphatic primary amine with no aromatic ring: a six-carbon chain with the amine on carbon 2 and a methyl group on carbon 4. [f:pubchem]
