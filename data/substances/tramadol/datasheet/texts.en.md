## molecule

Tramadol, cis-(1R,2R)-2-[(dimethylamino)methyl]-1-(3-methoxyphenyl)cyclohexan-1-ol. Base: C16H25NO2, 263.37 g/mol, CAS 27203-92-5. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

The medicine is a racemate of two enantiomers, which contribute to the effect through different mechanisms. [f:grond2004]

## category

Chemical class: other substances in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is a centrally acting analgesic, structurally related to codeine and morphine. [f:grond2004]

The medicine is a Schedule IV controlled substance in the United States. [f:tramadol-label]

## metabolism

Tramadol is metabolised mainly by O- and N-demethylation and by conjugation (glucuronides and sulfates). O-demethylation to the active metabolite M1 (O-desmethyltramadol) depends on CYP2D6; N-demethylation to M2 on CYP2B6 and CYP3A4. [f:grond2004]

M1 binds to μ receptors with higher affinity than tramadol, and is the more potent μ agonist. [f:tramadol-label]

The mean terminal half-life is 6.3 hours for tramadol and 7.4 hours for M1; about 30% of the dose is excreted unchanged in the urine. [f:tramadol-label]

## effects

Tramadol is an opioid agonist and a weak inhibitor of norepinephrine and serotonin reuptake; the analgesic effect is attributed to binding to μ receptors and to this reuptake inhibition. [f:tramadol-label]

(+)-Tramadol and (+)-M1 are μ agonists; (+)-tramadol inhibits serotonin reuptake and (-)-tramadol inhibits norepinephrine reuptake. [f:grond2004]

## doses

As a pain medicine, after titration the prescribing information gives 50-100 mg every 4 to 6 hours as needed, not exceeding 400 mg per day. [f:tramadol-label]

The maximum is 200 mg per day with creatinine clearance below 30 mL/min and 300 mg per day above 75 years of age. [f:tramadol-label]

## risks

Seizures can occur at the recommended dose, and the risk increases in people with epilepsy or a history of seizures and with other drugs. [f:tramadol-label]

In a Danish series of young adults admitted for seizures, 17.7% of the people had taken tramadol; the median dose was 200 mg (50-1200 mg) and among the 62 admissions only two people had a prescription. [f:nielsen2025]

In 71 Egyptian adults with overdose, seizures and serotonin toxicity occurred in 38% and 41% of cases, more often with high plasma concentrations. [f:ali2023]

It can cause serious or fatal respiratory depression, especially at the start or after a dose increase, and dependence. [f:tramadol-label]

## interactions

CYP2D6 inhibitors (for example quinidine, fluoxetine, paroxetine) can raise tramadol and lower M1, with more risk of seizures and serotonin syndrome and, in dependent people, withdrawal symptoms. [f:tramadol-label]

Serotonin syndrome is more likely with SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclic antidepressants, triptans and other serotonergic drugs. [f:tramadol-label]

With benzodiazepines or other central nervous system depressants, alcohol included, profound sedation, respiratory depression, coma and death can occur. [f:tramadol-label]

Use with monoamine oxidase inhibitors, or within 14 days after them, is contraindicated. [f:tramadol-label]

## counselling

Explain that the effect of tramadol varies from person to person because it depends on CYP2D6, which turns it into the active metabolite M1, and that some antidepressants slow this conversion. [f:grond2004] [f:crews2021] [f:tramadol-label]

Ask whether the person has had seizures or has epilepsy, and explain that tramadol seizures can occur even at low doses. [f:tramadol-label] [f:nielsen2025]

Ask whether the person takes antidepressants, triptans or other serotonergic substances: with tramadol, serotonin syndrome is more likely. [f:tramadol-label]

Ask about benzodiazepines, alcohol and other sedatives: combined with tramadol they can cause profound sedation and respiratory depression. [f:tramadol-label]

Explain that tramadol can cause dependence and misuse, with a risk of overdose and death. [f:tramadol-label]
