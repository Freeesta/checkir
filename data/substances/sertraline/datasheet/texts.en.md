## metabolism

Sertraline undergoes extensive first-pass metabolism; the principal initial pathway is N-demethylation to N-desmethylsertraline, which in vitro and in vivo is substantially less active than sertraline. Both compounds undergo oxidative deamination and subsequent reduction, hydroxylation and glucuronide conjugation. [f:sertraline-label]

The average terminal elimination half-life of plasma sertraline is about 26 h; that of N-desmethylsertraline is 62 to 104 h. [f:sertraline-label]
