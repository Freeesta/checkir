## effects

Synthetic cathinones have stimulant and, in part, hallucinogenic properties. [f:machacek2026]

In vitro, among 58 stimulants tested, the N-pyrrolidine cathinones carrying a methylenedioxy group, 3,4-methylenedioxy PV8 among them, showed the highest dopamine-transporter inhibition potency. [f:stalberga2026]
