## effects

Synthetic cathinones have stimulant and, in part, hallucinogenic properties; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:machacek2026]

Racemic pyrovalerone is a selective inhibitor of the dopamine and norepinephrine transporters with little effect on serotonin trafficking; the S enantiomer is the most active. [f:meltzer2006]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
