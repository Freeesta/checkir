## molecule

Cannabidiol (CBD), 2-[(1R,6R)-3-methyl-6-prop-1-en-2-ylcyclohex-2-en-1-yl]-5-pentylbenzene-1,3-diol. Base: C21H30O2, 314.5 g/mol, CAS 13956-29-1. Identity checked on PubChem. Phytocannabinoid with two chiral centres (1R,6R). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Naturally occurring phytocannabinoid of Cannabis sativa, not psychotoxic. [f:beers2021-cbd]

## metabolism

CBD is metabolised mainly by CYP2C19 and CYP3A4, with minor contributions from UGTs. CYP2C19 and CYP2C9 convert it to the main active metabolite, 7-OH-CBD, equipotent with CBD; CYP3A drives its clearance by oxidation at other positions. [f:beers2021-cbd]

## cuts

Synthetic cannabinoids as powders or in solution are sprayed onto non-psychoactive material such as CBD-rich hemp or other plant material. [f:hubner2025]

## effects

CBD is a non-psychoactive compound with potential anti-inflammatory, neuroprotective and anxiolytic properties. [f:blebea2024]

It is a popular consumer product and the active ingredient of Epidiolex, an oral solution approved by the FDA for Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet syndromes. [f:beers2021-cbd]

## risks

Vaping CBD and CBDA with a laboratory e-cigarette formed, among others, psychoactive THC isomers, cannabichromene and CBD quinone; the latter, potentially harmful, was highest at the lowest coil power tested. [f:kim2025-vaping]

## interactions

In human liver microsomes CBD and its metabolite 7-OH-CBD inhibit several cytochrome P450 enzymes; for oral CBD (700 mg) static models predict the largest interactions with drugs metabolised by CYP3A (midazolam, AUC ratio 14.8), CYP2C9 (diclofenac, 9.6) and CYP2C19 (omeprazole, 7.3); the authors call for in vivo studies to verify them. [f:bansal2022]

People taking CBD may be at risk of clinically important cytochrome P450-mediated drug interactions. [f:beers2021-cbd]
