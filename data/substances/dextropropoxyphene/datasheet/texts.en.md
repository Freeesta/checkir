## metabolism

Dextropropoxyphene is N-demethylated to nordextropropoxyphene, the major metabolic pathway, catalysed predominantly by CYP3A4 in human liver microsomes; CYP2D6 is not involved, and its pharmacokinetics did not differ between CYP2D6 extensive and poor metabolizers after a single oral dose of 65 mg.
