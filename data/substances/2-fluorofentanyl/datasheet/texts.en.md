## molecule

2-Fluorofentanyl (ortho-fluorofentanyl), N-(2-fluorophenyl)-N-[1-(2-phenylethyl)piperidin-4-yl]propanamide. Base: C22H27FN2O, 354.5 g/mol, CAS 910616-29-4. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: fentanyl analogues (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic fentanyl analogue with a fluorine on the N-phenyl ring. Fluorofentanyl has three positional isomers (para, ortho, meta); in the Florida casework studied the one identified was para-fluorofentanyl, the most common isomer to have emerged in the USA. [f:truver2022]

## metabolism

In human hepatocytes each fluorofentanyl isomer (ortho, meta, para) gave 14 metabolites; the main one was the N-dealkylation product norfluorofentanyl, with hydroxylated, N-oxide, dihydrodiol and hydroxymethoxy metabolites. [f:gundersen2020]

In an authentic human urine sample with ortho-fluorofentanyl, three metabolites were detected besides the parent compound: the hydroxymethoxy metabolite (most abundant), norfluorofentanyl and a metabolite hydroxylated on the ethylphenyl ring. [f:gundersen2020]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

In mice the aniline-fluorinated regioisomers of fentanyl (ortho-, meta- and para-fluorofentanyl) elicited hyperlocomotion, antinociception and hypoventilation to varying degrees, like prototypical μ-opioid standards. [f:varshneya2023]

## doses

Potency varies widely between fentanyl analogues, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lovrecic2019]

## risks

In poisonings with these compounds larger naloxone doses are needed than in a classic heroin overdose. [f:lovrecic2019] [f:armenian2018]

In a Florida postmortem study (370 suspected overdose cases, October 2020 to April 2021) the LC-QTOF-MS screen was positive for fluorofentanyl in 27 decedents and could not tell the three positional isomers apart. [f:truver2022]

## interactions

Sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
