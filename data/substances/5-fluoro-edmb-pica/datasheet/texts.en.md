## molecule

5-Fluoro-EDMB-PICA, ethyl (2S)-2-[[1-(5-fluoropentyl)indole-3-carbonyl]amino]-3,3-dimethylbutanoate. Base: C22H31FN2O3, 390.5 g/mol, CAS 2666934-54-7. Identity checked against PubChem (entry with the (S) stereocentre). It is the ethyl ester of an indole-3-carboxamide of tert-leucine; the indazole analogue is 5F-EDMB-PINACA. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid of the indole-3-carboxamide type with a tert-leucine ethyl ester group and a 5-fluoropentyl chain. [f:pubchem] [f:giorgetti2022-edmb]

## metabolism

In pooled human liver microsomes 14 phase I and 4 phase II metabolites were identified, through ester hydrolysis, dehydrogenation, hydrolytic defluorination, hydroxylation, dihydroxylation, glucuronidation and combinations; the monohydroxylated metabolites were proposed as biomarkers. [f:gao2024-5f-edmb-pica]

In authentic urine, 10 phase I metabolites were detected, mostly formed by ester hydrolysis; ester hydrolysis, monohydroxylation and defluorination products were suggested as urinary markers. [f:giorgetti2022-edmb]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

In mice 5F-EDMB-PICA produced the effects of the cannabinoid tetrad (hypothermia, analgesia, hypolocomotion, catalepsy) and, at 0.3 mg/kg, a conditioned place preference. [f:li2024-5f-edmb-pica]

Acute administration disrupted anxiety regulation, motor control and spatial memory in adolescent and young adult mice. [f:li2025-5f-edmb-pica]

## doses

No human dose data for this compound were found in the sources used, and potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids (ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are reported up to 140 and 85 times as potent as Δ9-THC), so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In urine collected for abstinence control, 5.4% of the positive samples tested positive for 5F-EDMB-PICA. [f:giorgetti2022-edmb]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis. [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
