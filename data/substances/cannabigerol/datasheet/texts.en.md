## molecule

Cannabigerol (CBG), 2-[(2E)-3,7-dimethylocta-2,6-dienyl]-5-pentylbenzene-1,3-diol. Base: C21H32O2, 316.5 g/mol, CAS 25654-31-3. Identity checked on PubChem. Phytocannabinoid without chiral centres. [f:pubchem]

## origin

Phytocannabinoid of Cannabis sativa. Its precursor is the acid form CBGA, itself a biosynthetic precursor of CBD and THC; heat-catalysed decarboxylation of CBGA gives CBG. [f:calapai2022]

## metabolism

CBG is metabolised in the liver by the enzyme CYP2J2 to hydroxylated and di-oxygenated products. [f:calapai2022]

## effects

CBG lacks psychotomimetic effects; it is considered a partial agonist at CB1 and CB2 receptors and a regulator of endocannabinoid signalling. Preclinical findings show anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, neuroprotective, anti-anxiety and appetite-stimulating effects. [f:calapai2022]

In models of Huntington's disease and epilepsy CBG (5 to 20 mg/kg) showed efficacy. [f:stone2020]
