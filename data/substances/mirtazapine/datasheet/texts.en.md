## metabolism

Mirtazapine is extensively metabolized; the major pathways are demethylation and hydroxylation followed by glucuronide conjugation. In vitro, CYP2D6 and CYP1A2 are involved in the formation of the 8-hydroxy metabolite, and CYP3A in that of the N-desmethyl and N-oxide metabolites. [f:mirtazapine-label]

Several unconjugated metabolites are pharmacologically active but present in plasma at very low levels. The half-life is about 20 to 40 hours; 75% of the dose is eliminated in urine and 15% in feces. [f:mirtazapine-label]
