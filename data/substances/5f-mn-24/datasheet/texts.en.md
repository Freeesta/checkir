## molecule

5F-MN-24 (5F-NNEI), 1-(5-fluoropentyl)-N-naphthalen-1-ylindole-3-carboxamide. Base: C24H23FN2O, 374.4 g/mol, CAS 1445580-60-8. Identity checked against PubChem (5F-MN-24 and 5F-NNEI are the same compound, so this sheet carries both names). It is the indole analogue of 5F-MN-18; achiral. [f:pubchem] [f:apirakkan2020]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist (naphthyl-indole carboxamide with a fluoropentyl chain). [f:apirakkan2020] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes (phase I only) 5F-NNEI followed amide hydrolysis and monohydroxylation at the naphthyl moiety as its main pathways. [f:apirakkan2020]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC, and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
