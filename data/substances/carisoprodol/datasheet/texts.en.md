## molecule

Carisoprodol, [2-(carbamoyloxymethyl)-2-methylpentyl] N-propan-2-ylcarbamate. Formula C12H24N2O4, 260.33 g/mol, CAS 78-44-4. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a carbamate skeletal muscle relaxant; its metabolite meprobamate is a controlled substance. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:carisoprodol-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a dicarbamate muscle relaxant. [f:carisoprodol-label]

## metabolism

The major pathway is hepatic: CYP2C19 forms meprobamate, itself a controlled substance that may cause dependence. The enzyme is polymorphic; poor CYP2C19 metabolizers have about 4-fold higher exposure to carisoprodol and about 50% lower exposure to meprobamate. [f:carisoprodol-label]

The terminal half-life of carisoprodol is about 2 hours; that of meprobamate about 10 hours. [f:carisoprodol-label]
