## origin

A synthetic imidazoline derivative, marketed in a transdermal system for the treatment of hypertension. [f:catapres-tts-label]

## metabolism

About 50% of the absorbed dose is metabolized in the liver; after oral administration 40% to 60% of the absorbed dose is recovered unchanged in the urine within 24 hours. [f:catapres-tts-label]

After intravenous administration the elimination half-life ranges from 12 to 16 hours and rises up to 41 hours in severe renal impairment. The label names no metabolite, so no pathway is listed. [f:catapres-tts-label]
