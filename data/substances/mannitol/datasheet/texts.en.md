## molecule

D-Mannitol ((2R,3R,4R,5R)-hexane-1,2,3,4,5,6-hexol), formula C6H14O6, molar mass 182.17 g/mol, CAS 69-65-8. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI, which agree on formula and InChIKey. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

## category

Chemical class: sugar alcohol (polyol, grouped under non-psychoactive substances). Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: diluent or excipient. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Mannitol is a naturally occurring sugar alcohol and a metabolite of several microbes, where it serves for energy storage, protection from osmotic stress and redox balancing. [f:rajkumar2026]

It is produced industrially by the chemical reduction of invert sugar, a process that also gives sorbitol as a side product, and can be produced by bacterial fermentation. [f:rajkumar2026]

## metabolism

In healthy volunteers, urinary recovery after an oral dose was 8.0-39.6 % of the amount ingested; net absorption in a perfused 30 cm jejunal segment was 3.6 %. After intravenous injection recovery in urine approached 100 % and renal clearance averaged 131 mL/min. [f:laker1982]

Mannitol distributes mainly in the extracellular space. In stool cultures it was degraded to acid and carbon dioxide, and it is naturally present in human urine. [f:laker1982]

## cuts

Mannitol is among the sugars reported as cuts of cocaine. [f:unodc-cocaine2012]

Internal guidelines of a drug checking service report lidocaine and mannitol as the most frequent cuts of cocaine in Italy, and describe inert cuts as substances that add weight and volume. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

## risks

For polyols (sugar alcohols) in general, a systematic review found that malabsorption is dose-dependent and increases when several polyols are ingested together. [f:lenhart2017]

Polyols can induce dose-dependent flatulence, abdominal discomfort and laxative effects in healthy volunteers and in people with irritable bowel syndrome. [f:lenhart2017]
