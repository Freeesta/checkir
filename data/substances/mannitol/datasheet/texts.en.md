## ftir_limits

The spectrum of a mixture reflects its main components. Components below about 5 % of the sample are generally not detectable, and FTIR does not measure concentration. Potent substances active at very low doses, such as fentanyl and its analogues (when found, usually at about 3-10 % w/w) and nitazenes, cannot be ruled out by this analysis. A match is a proposal for an expert to evaluate, not a diagnosis, and it holds only if the identification is correct; complementary tests are advisable. [f:checkir-ftir-limits] Mannitol, inositol, lactose and other sugars share broad O-H and C-O bands, so telling them apart in a mixture is uncertain, and polymorphs of mannitol can give different spectra.

## risks

For polyols (sugar alcohols) in general, a systematic review found that malabsorption is dose-dependent and increases when several polyols are ingested together. [f:lenhart2017]

Polyols can induce dose-dependent flatulence, abdominal discomfort and laxative effects in healthy volunteers and in people with irritable bowel syndrome. [f:lenhart2017]
