## molecule

5F-ADBICA, N-[(2S)-1-amino-3,3-dimethyl-1-oxobutan-2-yl]-1-(5-fluoropentyl)indole-3-carboxamide (l-tert-leucinamide of 5-fluoropentylindole-3-carboxylic acid). Base: C20H28FN3O2, 361.5 g/mol, CAS 1801338-27-1. Identity checked against PubChem (the (S) entry, which carries the name 5F-ADBICA). It is the indole analogue of 5F-ADB-PINACA (indazole) and has a terminal amide where 5F-MDMB-PICA has a methyl ester. [f:pubchem] [f:li2026]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist (indole-3-carboxamide of a tert-leucinamide with a fluoropentyl chain). [f:jones2025] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In pooled human liver microsomes (24 h) 28 metabolites of 5F-ADBICA were identified (21 for 5F-ADB-PINACA); they were also confirmed in a zebrafish model, and this was the first time 5F-ADBICA metabolites were detected in vitro. [f:li2026]

Hydrolytic defluorination (metabolite M19) is proposed as a potential biomarker of 5F-ADBICA. [f:li2026]

## effects

5F-ADBICA acted as a high-potency agonist of CB1 and CB2 receptors in a fluorometric membrane-potential assay. [f:banister2015]

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
