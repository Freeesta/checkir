## molecule

α-PHP (α-pyrrolidinohexanophenone), 1-phenyl-2-(pyrrolidin-1-yl)hexan-1-one. Base: C16H23NO, 245.36 g/mol, CAS 13415-86-6. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata]

Hydrochloride: C16H23NO·HCl, 281 g/mol, tan powder, CAS 13415-59-3; the monograph gives no melting point. The reference spectrum is a diamond ATR one (1 bounce, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-aphp]

α-PHP is the homologue of α-PVP with a one-carbon longer α chain; α-PiHP is its positional isomer, and the two are hard to tell apart by retention time and mass spectrum. [f:davies2023] [f:dinis2024] [f:yeh2024]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

α-PHP appeared on the Japanese illicit drug market in 2014; the 1971 Convention placed it in Schedule II in 2020, and its isomer α-PiHP in March 2023. [f:dinis2024]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a pyrovalerone derivative (pyrrolidinophenone). [f:dinis2024]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes nine phase I metabolites were identified, the first being the reduction of the beta-ketone; after 5 h incubation 71.4 % of α-PHP was still unmetabolised. [f:yeh2024]

## effects

In synaptosomes α-PHP is at least as potent as α-PVP as a dopamine transporter reuptake inhibitor, and its analogues are largely selective for the dopamine over the serotonin transporter. [f:davies2023]

In humans its stimulant effects are comparable to those of α-PVP and MDPV; in mice the effects on behaviour and the elimination of α-PHP differed between males and females. [f:bassi2025aphp]

## risks

Poison centre cases (Pavia) showed psychomotor agitation, tremors and fasciculations, hallucinations, tachycardia, tachypnoea, chest pain, hyperthermia and rhabdomyolysis. [f:bassi2025aphp]

In 33 plasma samples from forensic cases α-PHP was 0.75-128 µg/L (median 16.3); other drugs were found in all cases, and the users tended to act aggressively, possibly because of the high selectivity for dopamine transporter inhibition. [f:brueckner2024]

For the isomer α-PiHP the postmortem blood concentrations (0.3-840 ng/mL) overlap widely between intoxications and deaths. [f:gomonit2026]

## counselling

Say that a result for this substance needs a reference or a method that separates α-PiHP: the isomers share retention time and mass spectrum. [f:yeh2024] [f:gomonit2026]

Ask about agitation, restlessness and aggressive states in previous uses: users of α-PHP tended to act aggressively. [f:brueckner2024]

Ask about other drugs on the same night: they were found in all the forensic cases (opioids, benzodiazepines, pregabalin, alcohol); the UBC guidelines advise against mixing stimulants and against alcohol. [f:brueckner2024] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Signs to recognise: agitation, tremors, hallucinations, fast heartbeat, chest pain, high temperature; the UBC guidelines say to cool the person and seek help for hyperthermia. [f:bassi2025aphp] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
