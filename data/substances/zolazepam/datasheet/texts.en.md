## molecule

Zolazepam, 4-(2-fluorophenyl)-1,3,8-trimethyl-6H-pyrazolo[3,4-e][1,4]diazepin-7-one. C15H15FN4O, 286.30 g/mol, CAS 31352-82-6. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a pyrazolodiazepinone: a 5-aryl-1,4-diazepin-2-one core in which a pyrazole ring replaces the benzene ring of the benzodiazepines. [f:pubchem]

## origin

A synthetic substance used in veterinary anaesthesia, in a fixed combination with tiletamine hydrochloride. [f:telazol-label]


## metabolism

No human metabolism data are in this sheet. After intravenous Telazol (2.2 mg/kg, 1.1 mg/kg of each component) in 12 healthy adult Beagle dogs the mean elimination half-life of zolazepam was 0.41 h, against 0.87 h for tiletamine. [f:telazol-label]
