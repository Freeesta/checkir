## molecule

25I-NBOMe, 2-(4-iodo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)-N-[(2-methoxyphenyl)methyl]ethan-1-amine (2C-I-NBOMe). Base: C18H22INO3, 427.3 g/mol, CAS 919797-19-6. Hydrochloride: C18H22INO3·HCl, about 463 g/mol, melting point 157.3 °C, white powder. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and the SWGDRUG monograph. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:swgdrug-25i-nbome]

Forms: the SWGDRUG reference material is the hydrochloride, with a diamond ATR spectrum (3 bounces, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-25i-nbome]

The NBOMes are N-benzylmethoxy derivatives of the 2C compounds (2C-I, 2C-B, 2C-C): 25I-NBOMe derives from 2C-I. [f:suzuki2015]

## category

Chemical class: NBOMe (N-benzyl phenethylamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:suzuki2015] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The NBOMes were synthesised for research as potent agonists of the 5-HT2A receptor; 25I-NBOMe is the most frequent analogue in the cases described with analytical confirmation. [f:suzuki2015]

Several NBOMes have been on sale from online vendors since 2010, with numerous cases of toxicity and several deaths. [f:halberstadt2017]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of 2C-I. [f:suzuki2015]

## metabolism

The metabolism of the NBOMes is complex and goes through many pathways, which may partly explain the high toxicity observed. [f:herian2023]

For the two close analogues, 25B- and 25C-NBOMe, the main routes are O-demethylation, hydroxylation and conjugation of the phase I metabolites. [f:caspar2017]

## effects

In the in vitro comparison with 2C-I and LSD, the NBOMes are very potent 5-HT2A agonists (EC50 0.04-0.5 µM) with high affinity for adrenergic α1 receptors (Ki 0.3-0.9 µM). [f:rickli2015]

In 7 patients with 25I-NBOMe confirmed in plasma, tachycardia (7), agitation (6), visual and auditory hallucinations (6), hypertension (4), seizures (3) and hyperpyrexia (3) were seen. [f:hill2013-25i]

## doses

No dose is given for 25I-NBOMe: the active amount is so small that it cannot be judged by eye, and no dose is safe to suggest. [f:suzuki2015]

## risks

In 7 patients with 25I-NBOMe confirmed in plasma, all young men, creatine kinase was raised in all, 3 had metabolic acidosis and 1 acute kidney injury. [f:hill2013-25i]

In a series of 10 patients with a mean age of 17, 2 were in status epilepticus, 1 had intraparenchymal haemorrhages with acute kidney injury, 6 were admitted to intensive care and 3 were intubated. [f:hieger2015]

A death in Australia is described with 25I-NBOMe at about 28 µg/L in post-mortem aortic blood, much higher than values reported in non-fatal cases and in other deaths. [f:kueppers2015]

## interactions

A systematic review included 25I-NBOMe, taken sublingually on blotter paper, among the new psychoactive substances associated with serotonin syndrome. [f:schifano2021]
