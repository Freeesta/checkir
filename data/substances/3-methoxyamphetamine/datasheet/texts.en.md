## molecule

3-Methoxyamphetamine (3-MA), 1-(3-methoxyphenyl)propan-2-amine. Formula C10H15NO, 165.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An amphetamine with one methoxy group at position 3 of the phenyl ring; the isomer with the methoxy at position 2 is 2-methoxyamphetamine. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin
