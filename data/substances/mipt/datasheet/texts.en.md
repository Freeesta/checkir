## molecule

MiPT (N-methyl-N-isopropyltryptamine), N-[2-(1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Formula C14H20N2, 216.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is related to DMT, with one methyl replaced by an isopropyl group. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines (family sheet: tryptamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine, an N,N-dialkyltryptamine with a methyl and an isopropyl group on the nitrogen. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]
