## molecule

5-Fluoro-ADB-PINACA, N-[(2S)-1-amino-3,3-dimethyl-1-oxobutan-2-yl]-1-(5-fluoropentyl)indazole-3-carboxamide. Base: C19H27FN4O2, 362.4 g/mol, CAS 1801338-21-5. Identity checked against PubChem (entry with the (S) stereocentre). It is the carboxamide (tert-leucinamide); in part of the literature '5F-ADB' denotes the methyl ester 5F-MDMB-PINACA, a different compound. [f:musile2023] [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid of the indazole-3-carboxamide type with a tert-leucinamide group and a 5-fluoropentyl chain. [f:pubchem] [f:banister2015]

## metabolism

In human hepatocytes 12 major 5F-ADB-PINACA metabolites were identified; the major reactions were oxidative defluorination followed by carboxylation, and the 5-hydroxypentyl and pentanoic acid metabolites were proposed as markers of intake. [f:carlier2017-adb-pinaca]

In pooled human liver microsomes 21 metabolites were identified, and a hydrolytic defluorination product was proposed as a biomarker. [f:li2026-5f-adb-pinaca]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

5F-ADB-PINACA showed the greatest potency at CB1 receptors among the compounds of one pharmacological study. [f:banister2015]

In mice 5F-ADB-PINACA produced dose-dependent hypothermia mediated by CB1 receptors, with much less tolerance after repeated administration than JWH-018. [f:wilson2022-indazole]

## doses

No human dose data for this compound were found in the sources used, and potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids (ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are reported up to 140 and 85 times as potent as Δ9-THC), so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

5F-ADB-PINACA was among the indazole-3-carboxamides identified in seized, consumed or death-related cases in Kuwait in 2018. [f:al-matrouk2019]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis. [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
