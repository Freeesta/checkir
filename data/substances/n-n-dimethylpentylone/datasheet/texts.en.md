## molecule

N,N-Dimethylpentylone (dipentylone, bk-DMBDP), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-(dimethylamino)pentan-1-one. Base: C14H19NO3, 249.3 g/mol, CAS 803614-36-0. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified). [f:pubchem]

It is the N,N-dimethylated derivative of pentylone, of which it is the metabolic precursor. [f:fogarty2025]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Described as a new synthetic cathinone in a series of postmortem toxicology cases; it has been identified in counterfeit tablets sold as "Ecstasy" and "Molly". [f:fogarty2023] [f:fogarty2025]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a cathinone with a methylenedioxy ring and an N,N-dimethylamino group. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

Seven metabolites were identified in the urine of eight patients; the two major pathways proposed are opening of the five-membered (methylenedioxy) ring and reduction of the carbonyl. Pentylone and bisdesmethyl-N,N-dimethylpentylone were detected; the compound appears less potent than its metabolite pentylone. [f:leung2024]

In forensic cases with N,N-dimethylpentylone, pentylone, the primary N-desmethyl metabolite, was found in 98% of cases; in human liver microsomes compounds with an N,N-dimethylamino group show higher N-dealkylation and β-ketone reduction ratios. [f:fogarty2025] [f:yeh2025]

## effects

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

In eight cases of confirmed poisoning all patients had neuropsychiatric and/or cardiovascular symptoms, all with co-ingestion of other substances; all recovered. [f:leung2024]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
