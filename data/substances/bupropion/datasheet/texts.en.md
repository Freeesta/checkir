## molecule

Bupropion, 2-(tert-butylamino)-1-(3-chlorophenyl)propan-1-one (amfebutamone). Formula C13H18ClNO, 239.74 g/mol, CAS 34911-55-2. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a racemic mixture; the activity and pharmacokinetics of the single enantiomers have not been studied. [f:bupropion-label]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. The effect class (stimulant) is a checkir proposal, since the label describes an aminoketone antidepressant whose action is presumed to be noradrenergic and/or dopaminergic; it is awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:bupropion-label] [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound marketed as an aminoketone antidepressant in tablets. [f:bupropion-label]

## metabolism

Three metabolites are active: hydroxybupropion (hydroxylation of the tert-butyl group, CYP2B6 being the principal enzyme in vitro) and the amino-alcohols threohydrobupropion and erythrohydrobupropion (reduction of the carbonyl group, without CYP involvement for threohydrobupropion). Side-chain oxidation gives a glycine conjugate of meta-chlorobenzoic acid, the major urinary metabolite. [f:bupropion-label]

The mean elimination half-life of bupropion is 21 h (SD 9); that of hydroxybupropion is about 20 h (SD 5). [f:bupropion-label]
