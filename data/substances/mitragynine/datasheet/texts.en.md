## molecule

Mitragynine (kratom alkaloid), methyl (E)-2-[(2S,3S,12bS)-3-ethyl-8-methoxy-1,2,3,4,6,7,12,12b-octahydroindolo[2,3-a]quinolizin-2-yl]-3-methoxyprop-2-enoate. Formula C23H30N2O4, 398.5 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the main pharmacologically active alkaloid of kratom; the molecule is a single stereoisomer with a methoxyacrylate side chain. [f:basiliere2020] [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances. Expected effect: stimulant and opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification] At low doses kratom alkaloids act as psychostimulants and at higher doses they mediate an opioid-like effect. [f:horniakova2024]

## origin

Mitragynine is the major indole alkaloid of the leaves of kratom (Mitragyna speciosa), a tropical tree of southern Thailand and the northern Malay Peninsula. [f:ya2019]

## metabolism

In vitro with recombinant human enzymes, 7-hydroxymitragynine was formed only by CYP3A4; 9-O-demethylmitragynine, the most abundant metabolite in vitro and the most prevalent in the urine of kratom users, by CYP2C19, CYP3A4 and CYP2D6; 16-carboxymitragynine by CYP2D6, CYP2C19 and CYP2C18. 7-Hydroxymitragynine has a mu-opioid receptor affinity more than ten times that of morphine. [f:basiliere2020]
