## molecule

2,3-Pentylone, 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-4-yl)-2-(methylamino)pentan-1-one. Base: C13H17NO3, 235.3 g/mol, CAS 2749428-56-4. Identity checked against PubChem and ChEBI; one stereocentre, not specified there. [f:pubchem] [f:chebi] It is the 2,3-methylenedioxy positional isomer of pentylone (3,4): same formula, ring substituted at different positions, so it is not the same compound. [f:pubchem]

Ring-substituted positional isomers share the formula and the C=O region; the out-of-plane ring bands below 900 cm-1 separate them, and only by comparison with a reference. [f:unodc-cathinones2015] [f:smith-benzene]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often with lethal outcomes: in 42 reported cases death occurred in nearly half, with higher fatality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
