## molecule

DBZP (1,4-dibenzylpiperazine), 1,4-dibenzylpiperazine. Formula C18H22N2, 266.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A piperazine ring with a benzyl group on each of the two nitrogens. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: piperazines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]
