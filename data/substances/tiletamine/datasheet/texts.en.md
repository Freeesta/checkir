## molecule

Tiletamine, 2-(ethylamino)-2-thiophen-2-ylcyclohexan-1-one. Formula C12H17NOS, 223.34 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Like ketamine it is an arylcyclohexylamine with a ketone on the cyclohexane ring, with a thiophene ring in place of the phenyl. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines (phencyclidine/ketamine type). Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance used in veterinary anaesthesia, in a fixed combination with zolazepam hydrochloride. [f:telazol-label]

## metabolism

This sheet has no human metabolism data. After intravenous Telazol (2.2 mg/kg, 1.1 mg/kg of each component) in 12 healthy adult Beagle dogs the mean elimination half-life of tiletamine was 0.87 h, against 0.41 h for zolazepam. [f:telazol-label]
