## molecule

Phentermine, 2-methyl-1-phenylpropan-2-amine. Formula C10H15N, 149.23 g/mol, CAS 122-09-8. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It has no stereocentre: it is amphetamine with a second methyl group on the carbon that carries the nitrogen. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, marketed as a medicine (anorectic). [f:phentermine-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In the US prescribing information, phentermine reaches peak concentrations after 3 to 4.4 hours, and its cumulative urinary excretion as phentermine, with uncontrolled urinary pH, was 62% to 85%: it is largely excreted unchanged. [f:phentermine-label]
