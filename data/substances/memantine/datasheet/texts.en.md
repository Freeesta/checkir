## history

Memantine is an NMDA receptor antagonist used for moderate-to-severe Alzheimer's disease; its abuse risk is described as very low. A content analysis of 136 self-reports on reddit.com (2010-2019) found self-medication, nootropic and recreational uses. [f:memantine-misuse2020]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance. [f:namenda-xr-label]

## metabolism

Memantine undergoes partial hepatic metabolism, and the hepatic microsomal CYP450 system does not play a significant role. About 48 % is excreted unchanged in urine; the rest is converted mainly to three polar metabolites with minimal NMDA receptor antagonistic activity: the N-glucuronide, 6-hydroxy memantine and 1-nitroso-deaminated memantine. [f:namenda-xr-label]

The terminal elimination half-life is about 60-80 hours. [f:namenda-xr-label]
