## molecule

Flubromazepam, 7-bromo-5-(2-fluorophenyl)-1,3-dihydro-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one. Formula C15H10BrFN2O, 333.15 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Flubromazepam was reported in 2013 as the second designer benzodiazepine offered on the market, after pyrazolam (2012). [f:moosmann2013]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a designer benzodiazepine sold as a 'research chemical' on internet shops. [f:moosmann2013]

## metabolism

In a study on one volunteer who took 4 mg, with serum and urine followed for up to 31 days, the elimination half-life was estimated at more than 100 hours. The predominant metabolites were a monohydroxylated and a debrominated compound; the first allowed detection of use up to 28 days after intake in urine. [f:moosmann2013]
