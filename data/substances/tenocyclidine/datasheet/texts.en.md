## molecule

Tenocyclidine (TCP), 1-(1-thiophen-2-ylcyclohexyl)piperidine. Formula C15H23NS, 249.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a phencyclidine analogue in which a thiophene ring replaces the phenyl ring; like phencyclidine it has no ketone. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines (phencyclidine/ketamine type). Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a phencyclidine analogue with a thiophene ring. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In rats, urine collected six hours after tenocyclidine contained only one metabolite by GC-MS, 1-thienylcyclohexene; no human metabolism data are in this sheet. [f:petek2010]
