## molecule

Cathine ((+)-norpseudoephedrine), (1S,2S)-2-amino-1-phenylpropan-1-ol. Base: C9H13NO, 151.21 g/mol, CAS 492-39-7. Identity checked against PubChem (stereochemistry specified: 1S,2S). ## origin

A natural substance: an alkaloid of khat (Catha edulis), found with cathinone and norephedrine. [f:atlabachew2016]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes and in rat urine several metabolites were common to khat (cathinone) and pseudoephedrine, so cathine alone cannot serve as a specific marker of khat use. [f:park2026]

## effects

In anaesthetised rats cathine produced marked tachycardia (significant rises in heart rate from 0.1 mg/kg) and smaller blood pressure responses; the actions were greatly attenuated in chemically sympathectomised rats, so cathine acts mainly indirectly, probably by releasing noradrenaline. [f:alsufyani2024]

## risks

The rat study suggests possible implications for adverse cardiovascular actions of khat consumption, particularly of dried khat, in which cathine may predominate over cathinone. [f:alsufyani2024]
