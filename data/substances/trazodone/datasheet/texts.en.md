## origin

## metabolism

In vitro studies in human liver microsomes show that trazodone is metabolized by oxidative cleavage to an active metabolite, m-chlorophenylpiperazine (mCPP), by CYP3A4; other pathways are not well characterized. [f:trazodone-label]

Trazodone is extensively metabolized: less than 1% of an oral dose is excreted unchanged in the urine. [f:trazodone-label]
