## molecule

3,4-Methylenedioxy-α-pyrrolidinopropiophenone (MDPPP), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-pyrrolidin-1-ylpropan-1-one. Base: C14H17NO3, 247.29 g/mol, CAS 783241-66-7. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (methylenedioxy). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

MDPPP belongs to the homologous series of 3,4-methylenedioxy derivatives with a pyrrolidine group (with MDPBP and MDPV, which differ in the length of the alkyl chain); it was detected in a polydrug fatality together with MDPHP, MDPV and MDPBP. [f:casati2025]

## origin

A synthetic substance: a methylenedioxy synthetic cathinone with a pyrrolidine group; derivatives of this group are psychostimulants acting mainly on monoamine transporters. [f:casati2025]
