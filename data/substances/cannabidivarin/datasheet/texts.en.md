## molecule

Cannabidivarin (CBDV), 2-[(1R,6R)-3-methyl-6-prop-1-en-2-ylcyclohex-2-en-1-yl]-5-propylbenzene-1,3-diol. Base: C19H26O2, 286.4 g/mol, CAS 24274-48-4. Identity checked on PubChem. Analogue of CBD with a propyl chain in place of the pentyl chain; two chiral centres (1R,6R). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## effects

In a phase 1 trial in five girls with Rett syndrome and drug-resistant epilepsy, oral CBDV reduced the median monthly seizure frequency by 79%. [f:hurley2022-cbdv]

## doses

The highest dose reached in the study was 10 mg/kg per day of oral solution, in five girls: it is a study dose, not a recommendation. [f:hurley2022-cbdv]

## risks

In the study 91% of adverse events were mild or moderate and none required withdrawal; hypersomnolence and drooling were attributed to CBDV and no serious adverse event was related. [f:hurley2022-cbdv]
