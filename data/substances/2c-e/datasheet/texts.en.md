## molecule

2C-E, 2-(4-ethyl-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethan-1-amine. Base: C12H19NO2, 209.28 g/mol, CAS 71539-34-9. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Bands of the hydrochloride in the SWGDRUG library spectrum (ATR), read from the file to about ±2 cm-1: about 1205 cm-1 (the strongest; asymmetric aryl C-O-C), then about 1043 cm-1 (symmetric C-O-C), 1508, 854 and 1404 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-ir-library] [f:smith-ethers]

The 2,5-dimethoxy ring gives the same aryl ether bands in every 2C compound, so the band at about 1205 cm-1 points to the group, not to the substance; the SWGDRUG monograph of 2C-B notes that, with the parameters used, the IR spectrum does not tell 2C-B from 2C-I. [f:smith-ethers] [f:swgdrug-2cb]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, 2C series. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

2C-E is among the compounds whose synthesis Alexander Shulgin described in PIHKAL (1991). [f:dean2013]

Until the summer of 2012 the only 2C compounds in Schedule I of the US Controlled Substances Act were 2C-B and 2C-T-7; 2C-E was added to that list afterwards. [f:dean2013]

## origin

2C-E is a synthetic substance of the 2C series of phenethylamines, among the designer drugs. [f:dean2013]

## metabolism

In rats 2C-E was mainly metabolised by O-demethylation, N-acetylation, hydroxylation of the ethyl side chain (then oxidation to the ketone) and deamination to alcohols or acids; most metabolites were excreted in conjugated form. [f:theobald2006e]

With human enzymes MAO-A and MAO-B were the major enzymes of deamination, and CYP2D6 contributed only to a very small extent. [f:theobald2007]

## effects

In the 2C series the reported effects combine hallucinogenic and stimulant ones and depend on the dose: stimulation and heightened senses at low doses, hallucinations at moderate doses, unpleasant hallucinations with tachycardia, hypertension and hyperthermia at higher doses. [f:dean2013]

The effects of 2C-E are reportedly more intense than those of 2C-B and 2C-I. [f:dean2013]

In rats 2C-E substituted for at least one hallucinogen in drug discrimination, but not for methamphetamine; in mice it depressed locomotor activity, with stimulation at low doses. [f:eshleman2014]

## doses

The reported oral dose is 10-25 mg with a duration of 8-12 h; the figures are the anecdotal ones of Shulgin's 1991 book, quoted by a review, not results of controlled studies. [f:dean2013]

Products sold online often do not contain the substance or the dose on the label. [f:dean2013]

## risks

Intoxication with 2C drugs presents with hallucinations, agitation, aggression, dysphoria, hypertension, tachycardia, seizures and hyperthermia; the deaths attributed to 2C drugs showed an excited delirium picture. [f:dean2013]

A 19-year-old reported in the media died after snorting 2C-E, with aggression, hyperthermia and multi-organ failure; of ten patients who took a product sold as 2C-I but confirmed as 2C-E, eight had severe agitation or delirium and one died. [f:dean2013]

A fatal toxic leukoencephalopathy after 2C-E was described, with diffuse injury of the cerebral white matter on MRI; in a mass poisoning of 29 people (24-56 years) with 2C-E and bromo-dragonfly, 2C-E was confirmed in seven urine samples, all recovered and some needed mechanical ventilation. [f:sacks2012] [f:iwersen2019]

## interactions

Because MAO-A and MAO-B deaminate the 2C compounds, monoamine oxidase inhibitors can raise their concentrations and the risk of toxicity. [f:dean2013]

## counselling

Ask which 2C compound the person expects: 2C-E has been sold as 2C-I (ten patients, one died) and is reportedly more intense than 2C-B and 2C-I, and the UBC guidelines note that the compounds of the 2C-x group differ in effects and doses. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Hallucinogenic effects depend on set and setting: ask where and with whom it will be taken, whether the person is well and has slept, and suggest not taking it alone; at moderate doses hallucinations appear and at higher ones they can be unpleasant. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Explain that the review quotes 10-25 mg for 2C-E with effects lasting 8-12 h, longer than for 2C-B (4-8 h), and that the UBC guidelines, written for 2C-B, say that a few mg more or less change the effects and suggest approaching the dose gradually. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about MAO inhibitors and other medicines: deamination of 2C-E goes through the MAOs, and the UBC guidelines, written for 2C-B, advise against mixing it with alcohol and medicines, MAO inhibitors in particular. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
