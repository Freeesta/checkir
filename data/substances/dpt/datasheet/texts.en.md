## molecule

DPT, N-[2-(1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-N-propylpropan-1-amine. Formula C16H24N2, 244.37 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An N,N-dialkyltryptamine with two n-propyl groups on the nitrogen and no ring substituent; it is the n-propyl counterpart of DiPT. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]
