## history

Mexedrone is a synthetic cathinone structurally related to mephedrone, of the class of N-alkyl cathinone derivatives. [f:camuto2022]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an analogue of mephedrone. [f:camuto2022]

## metabolism

In vitro (human liver microsomes and recombinant CYP isoforms) the main phase I reactions are hydroxylation and N- and O-dealkylation. The isoform most involved is CYP2C19 (both hydroxylated and dealkylated metabolites), followed by CYP2D6 and CYP1A2 (hydroxylation only). A significant fraction of mexedrone remains unchanged; the unchanged compound and the hydroxylated metabolites are indicated as markers of intake. [f:camuto2022]
