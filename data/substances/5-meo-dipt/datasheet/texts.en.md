## molecule

5-MeO-DiPT, N-[2-(5-methoxy-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-N-propan-2-ylpropan-2-amine. Base: C17H26N2O, 274.4 g/mol, CAS 4021-34-5. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: 5-methoxytryptamine with two isopropyl groups on the amine nitrogen. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes 5-MeO-DIPT was mainly oxidised to the O-demethylated metabolite (5-OH-DIPT) and the N-deisopropylated one (5-MeO-IPT). [f:narimatsu2006]

Among six recombinant enzymes only CYP2D6 showed O-demethylase activity; CYP1A2, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19 and CYP3A4 showed N-deisopropylase activity, and CYP1A2, CYP2C8 and CYP3A4 were the major ones. [f:narimatsu2006]
