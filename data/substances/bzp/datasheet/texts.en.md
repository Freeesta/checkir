## molecule

BZP (1-benzylpiperazine), 1-benzylpiperazine. Formula C11H16N2, 176.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

A piperazine ring with one benzyl group on a nitrogen and a free N-H on the other; DBZP (own sheet) has a benzyl group on both nitrogens. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: piperazines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a piperazine. 1,4-dibenzylpiperazine (DBZP, own sheet) has been detected as a by-product in BZP samples. [f:pubchem] [f:stojanovska2014]

## metabolism

In rat urine the metabolites showed hydroxylation at the aromatic ring and degradation of the piperazine moiety; BZP is also a metabolite of the drug piberaline. [f:staack2002]

After 200 mg by mouth in 7 healthy adults, plasma BZP peaked at 262 ng/mL after 75 min, and its main metabolites 4-OH-BZP and 3-OH-BZP at 7 and 13 ng/mL. The elimination half-life was 5.5 h and BZP could be detected in plasma for up to 30 h. [f:antia2009]
