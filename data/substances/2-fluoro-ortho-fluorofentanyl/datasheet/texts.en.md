## molecule

2-Fluoro-ortho-fluorofentanyl (2,2'-difluorofentanyl), N-(2-fluorophenyl)-N-[1-[2-(2-fluorophenyl)ethyl]piperidin-4-yl]propanamide: ortho-fluorofentanyl with a second fluorine on the phenethyl ring. Base: C22H26F2N2O, 372.5 g/mol, CAS 2748343-87-3. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

## doses

No source used for this sheet reports the potency of this specific compound; potency varies widely between fentanyl analogues, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lovrecic2019]

## risks

In poisonings with these compounds larger naloxone doses are needed than in a classic heroin overdose. [f:lovrecic2019] [f:armenian2018]

## interactions

Sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
