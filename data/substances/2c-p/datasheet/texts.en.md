## molecule

2C-P, 2-(2,5-dimethoxy-4-propylphenyl)ethanamine. Formula C13H21NO2, 223.31 g/mol, CAS 207740-22-5. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: 2C series. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

One of the 2C drugs, known for hallucinogenic properties; they differ in receptor affinity, potency and duration of action. [f:stoller2017]

## origin

A synthetic 2C phenethylamine: 2,5-dimethoxyphenethylamine with n-propyl at position 4. [f:pubchem] [f:stoller2017]

## metabolism

In one patient who took a substance sold as 2C-B, 2C-P was found in two blood samples and the elimination half-life estimated from them was 19 h. [f:stoller2017]
