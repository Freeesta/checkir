## molecule

2-FA, 1-(2-fluorophenyl)propan-2-amine (2-fluoroamphetamine). Base: C9H12FN, 153.20 g/mol, CAS 1716-60-5. Hydrochloride: 189 g/mol, CAS 1626-69-3, white powder. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata; SWGDRUG gives the diamond ATR spectrum of the hydrochloride. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:swgdrug-2fa]

Positional isomers: 2-FA, 3-FA and 4-FA have the same formula and differ only in the position of the fluorine. For the out-of-plane C-H bending band of the ring, ortho-disubstituted rings absorb at 770-735 cm-1; the full spectrum must be compared with the reference one. [f:smith-benzene] [f:swgdrug-2fa]

Isomer identification: in LC-MS/MS casework 3-FA and 4-FA are told apart from 2-FA by retention time and ion ratio, and infrared ion spectroscopy coupled to mass spectrometry has been shown to separate the three isomers. [f:johansen2012] [f:kranenburg2020]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines (fluoroamphetamines). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Synthetic compound, described as a designer drug. [f:johansen2012]

## effects

Animal data only; no controlled human study of 2-FA effects was found. In mice, 2-fluoroamphetamine produced dose- and time-dependent locomotor stimulation with a peak effect similar to methamphetamine, and in rats trained to discriminate methamphetamine from saline it substituted fully for methamphetamine. [f:anchondo2025]

## risks

In three Danish forensic cases (mainly driving under the influence) 2-FA was found in whole blood at 0.028, 0.041 and 0.37 mg/kg; all three also contained amphetamine. [f:johansen2012]

## counselling

Ask which fluoroamphetamine the person expects: 2-FA, 3-FA and 4-FA are positional isomers, and 3-FA and 4-FA co-elute in LC-MS/MS and need the ion ratio to be told apart; 4-FA also inhibits the serotonin transporter more than amphetamine does, and the UBC guidelines describe entactogenic effects only for 4-FA, so the three should not be treated as interchangeable. [f:johansen2012] [f:luethi2019-halogen] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about other substances and medicines: the UBC guidelines advise against combining with alcohol and other stimulants because the effects of interactions are practically unknown; for 4-FA, the closest studied isomer, a review anticipates pharmacodynamic interactions with ADHD medication and antidepressants. [f:inan2020] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
