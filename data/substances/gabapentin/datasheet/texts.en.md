## molecule

Gabapentin, 2-[1-(aminomethyl)cyclohexyl]acetic acid. Formula C9H17NO2, 171.24 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a gabapentinoid, structurally related to γ-aminobutyric acid (GABA), that binds with high affinity to the α2δ subunit of voltage-activated calcium channels. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:neurontin-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, structurally related to GABA, used as a medicine. [f:neurontin-label]

## metabolism

Gabapentin is not appreciably metabolized in humans; it is eliminated from the systemic circulation by renal excretion as unchanged drug. [f:neurontin-label]

The elimination half-life is 5 to 7 hours and is unaltered by dose or multiple dosing; clearance is directly proportional to creatinine clearance. [f:neurontin-label]
