## molecule

4-Fluoro-MDMB-BUTINACA, methyl (2S)-2-[[1-(4-fluorobutyl)indazole-3-carbonyl]amino]-3,3-dimethylbutanoate. Base: C19H26FN3O3, 363.4 g/mol, CAS 2390036-46-9. Identity checked against PubChem. It is the compound written 4F-MDMB-BINACA and, in PubChem, also 4F-ADB; it is not the 4-fluoropentyl isomer described in the sheet 4-fluoro-adb. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

In a systematic review of new psychoactive substances in prisons, 4F-MDMB-BINACA was among the synthetic cannabinoids most often reported in non-biological samples (smuggled mainly on paper or herbal matrices) and in biological samples. [f:vaccaro2022]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist with an indazole core and a C4 N-fluoroalkyl chain. [f:xu2025]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes and zebrafish, 14 metabolites of 4F-MDMB-BINACA were detected (16 for 4F-MDMB-BICA), and the two compounds followed similar metabolic processes. [f:li2022]

In a fatal case the main metabolites came from ester hydrolysis and oxidative defluorination and were found in blood, urine and cerebrospinal fluid; the parent compound is eliminated rapidly. [f:tokarczyk2022]

In vitro, the ester hydrolysis metabolites of 5F-MDMB-PINACA and 4F-MDMB-BINACA were much less potent and efficacious at CB1 and CB2 than the parent compounds and stayed longer in circulation. [f:lie2020]

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

In mice, inhaled or injected 4F-MDMB-BINACA and 4F-MDMB-BICA produced the cannabinoid tetrad effects: analgesia, hypothermia, catalepsy and suppressed locomotion. [f:xu2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC, and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a fatal case with 5F-MDMB-PICA, 4F-MDMB-BINACA was found only in cerebrospinal fluid, at 0.1 ng/mL. [f:tokarczyk2022]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
