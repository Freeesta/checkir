## molecule

GTS-21, 3-[(5E)-5-[(2,4-dimethoxyphenyl)methylidene]-3,4-dihydro-2H-pyridin-6-yl]pyridine. Formula C19H20N2O2, 308.4 g/mol, CAS 148372-04-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the (E) isomer, a 3-benzylidene derivative of anabaseine. [f:kim2007-gts21]

## origin

A synthetic compound: a 2,4-dimethoxybenzylidene derivative of anabaseine. [f:kim2007-gts21]

## metabolism

GTS-21 has two major demethylated metabolites, 4-OH-GTS-21 and 2-OH-GTS-21; 4-OH-GTS-21 has been suggested to contribute to its therapeutic effects. [f:kim2007-gts21]

In healthy male volunteers given repeated doses, plasma Cmax and AUC of GTS-21 and of 4-OH-GTS-21 increased in a dose-related fashion, with considerable intersubject variability that decreased with continued dosing. [f:kitagawa2003-gts21]
