## molecule

Fenethylline, 1,3-dimethyl-7-[2-(1-phenylpropan-2-ylamino)ethyl]purine-2,6-dione. Formula C18H23N5O2, 341.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a theophylline derivative of amphetamine: the amphetamine part has its own sheet (amphetamine); theophylline is a xanthine like caffeine. [f:katselou2016]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:katselou2016] [f:checkir-classification]

The trade name «Captagon» became the street name of tablets seized in Arab countries. [f:katselou2016]

## origin

A synthetic compound, used as a medicine for hyperactivity disorders in children, narcolepsy and depression, then as a drug of abuse under the name «captagon». [f:katselou2016]

## metabolism

During metabolism the molecule releases its two active parts, amphetamine and theophylline, which act in synergy. [f:wenthur2017]

## cuts

Tablets sold as captagon seized in Jordan (124 batches) contained amphetamine and caffeine and no fenethylline. [f:alabdalla2005]
