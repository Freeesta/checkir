## category

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a short-acting barbiturate. [f:pentobarbital-label]

## metabolism

Barbiturates are metabolized mainly by the hepatic microsomal enzyme system; the inactive metabolites are excreted in urine, less commonly in feces, as glucuronic acid conjugates. The plasma half-life of pentobarbital in adults is 15 to 50 hours and appears to be dose dependent. [f:pentobarbital-label]
