## molecule

Doxylamine, N,N-dimethyl-2-(1-phenyl-1-pyridin-2-ylethoxy)ethanamine. Formula C17H22N2O, 270.37 g/mol, CAS 469-21-6. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. The label describes doxylamine succinate as an antihistamine, an ethanolamine derivative. The effect axis has no antihistamine class: "depressant/sedative" is a checkir proposal awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:diclegis-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic antihistamine, marketed in a fixed-dose combination tablet with pyridoxine. [f:diclegis-label]

## metabolism

Doxylamine is biotransformed in the liver by N-dealkylation to its principal metabolites, N-desmethyldoxylamine and N,N-didesmethyldoxylamine, which are excreted by the kidney. [f:diclegis-label]

The terminal elimination half-life of doxylamine is 12.5 hours in the text and 12.6 ± 3.4 hours in the table of the label (single dose, healthy non-pregnant women). [f:diclegis-label]
