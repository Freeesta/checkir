## molecule

Diltiazem ([(2S,3S)-5-[2-(dimethylamino)ethyl]-2-(4-methoxyphenyl)-4-oxo-2,3-dihydro-1,5-benzothiazepin-3-yl] acetate), formula C22H26N2O4S, molar mass 414.5 g/mol. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI, which agree on formula and InChIKey. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

## category

Chemical class: calcium channel blocker, a heart and blood-pressure medicine (grouped here under other substances). Expected effect: no psychoactive effect expected. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In vitro studies show that CYP3A4 carries out the N-demethylation of diltiazem and CYP2D6 its O-demethylation; CYP3A4 plays the more prominent role in the overall metabolism. [f:molden2002b]

Desacetyl diltiazem and N-demethyldesacetyl diltiazem are pharmacologically active metabolites; in people lacking CYP2D6 activity their exposure was at least 5 times higher than in extensive metabolisers. [f:molden2002]

In healthy volunteers the terminal half-life of diltiazem was about 6 hours (5.85 to 6.27 hours) after single and repeated oral doses. [f:hoglund1989]

## risks

Poisoning with calcium channel blockers such as diltiazem causes significant myocardial depression, bradycardia and hypotension; with sustained-release products the peak toxicity can be delayed by several hours. [f:graudins2016]

An analysis of 67 spontaneous reports of cocaine used together with calcium channel blockers (30 of them with diltiazem) led the authors to suggest caution because of a possible increase of toxicity and lethality, also when the blocker is present as an adulterant in cocaine. [f:chiappini2025]

## interactions

In volunteers, diltiazem raised the exposure to oral midazolam, a CYP3A4 substrate, 4.1-fold (1.6-fold with intravenous midazolam); diltiazem and its metabolite N-desmethyldiltiazem inhibit CYP3A4. [f:zhang2009]

Reports of cocaine taken together with diltiazem, many with opioids and alcohol also involved, are summarised in a pharmacovigilance analysis whose authors advise caution. [f:chiappini2025]

## counselling

Explain that diltiazem is a heart medicine sometimes found in cocaine: spontaneous reports of cocaine used together with it suggest greater toxicity and lethality, and the authors advise caution. Ask whether the person takes a calcium channel blocker as a prescribed treatment. [f:chiappini2025]
