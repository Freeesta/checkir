## molecule

Lauflumide (flmodafinil, CRL-40,940), 2-[bis(4-fluorophenyl)methylsulfinyl]acetamide. Formula C15H13F2NO2S, 309.3 g/mol, CAS 90280-13-0. Identity checked against PubChem (the stereochemistry at the sulfoxide is not specified in the entry). [f:pubchem]

It is the bis(4-fluoro) analogue of modafinil. CRL-40,941 (fladrafinil, its own sheet) is the N-hydroxy analogue and acts as a prodrug of flmodafinil. [f:krug2026] [f:dowling2017]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. The effect class (stimulant) is a checkir proposal, since the compound is described as a wake-promoting compound and sold as an alternative to modafinil; it is awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:luca2018] [f:krug2026] [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Under the code NLS-4, lauflumide was tested in mice as a wake-promoting compound: 64 mg/kg gave a longer wakefulness than modafinil at 150 mg/kg. [f:luca2018]

Flmodafinil and fladrafinil belong to the class S6 stimulants of the WADA Prohibited List. [f:krug2026]

## origin

A synthetic compound, the bis(4-fluoro) analogue of modafinil. [f:krug2026]

## metabolism

Six volunteers ingested 20 mg of flmodafinil: flmodafinil acid and flmodafinil sulfone were the metabolites detected in urine and blood. [f:krug2026]

The unchanged compound reached urinary maxima of 191-891 ng/mL and blood maxima of 45-98 ng/mL; the authors note long detection windows for the intact drug and its metabolites. [f:krug2026]
