## molecule

Racemorphan, (±)-3-hydroxy-17-methylmorphinan. Formula C17H23NO, 257.37 g/mol, CAS 297-90-5. Identity checked against PubChem; the entry gives no stereochemistry, being the racemate. [f:pubchem]

It is the racemate of levorphanol (levo-3-hydroxy-N-methylmorphinan, a strong opioid) and dextrorphan, its mirror image, which is also the O-demethylated metabolite of dextromethorphan. [f:pubchem] [f:prommer2007] [f:lofaro2023]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans. Expected effect: opioid, since one of its two components, levorphanol, is a strong opioid agonist. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:prommer2007] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic morphinan, the racemate of 3-hydroxy-N-methylmorphinan. [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

Levorphanol, one of the two components, is glucuronidated in the liver and the glucuronides are excreted by the kidney; its long half-life favours accumulation. [f:prommer2007]
