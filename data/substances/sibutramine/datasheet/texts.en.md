## molecule

Sibutramine, 1-[1-(4-chlorophenyl)cyclobutyl]-N,N,3-trimethylbutan-1-amine. Formula C17H26ClN, 279.8 g/mol, CAS 106650-56-0. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: cyclobutylalkylamine appetite suppressant, withdrawn from the market (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: pharmacological adulterant. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:carles2025] [f:checkir-classification]

Among FDA warnings on adulterated dietary supplements from 2007 to 2016, sibutramine was the hidden ingredient in 269 of 317 weight-loss products (84.9%). [f:tucker2018] In a Marseille case series all 12 weight-loss supplements analysed contained it, 7.5 to 15.4 mg per unit. [f:carles2025]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance, withdrawn from the market for cardiovascular risks; it is found as an unapproved, undeclared ingredient of supplements. [f:carles2025] [f:tucker2018]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes CYP2B6 is the main enzyme forming the two active metabolites: M1 (monodesmethylsibutramine) from sibutramine and M2 (didesmethylsibutramine) from M1; CYP2C19, CYP3A4 and CYP3A5 contribute to M1 only at high substrate concentrations. [f:bae2008]

After a single 15 mg dose, the AUC of sibutramine was 18.5% higher in CYP2C19 intermediate and 252.2% higher in poor metabolizers than in extensive metabolizers. [f:kim2009]
