## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cathinone with a methylenedioxy group and a dimethylamino group; it has the same formula as ethylone and butylone (C12H15NO3). [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

For nine methylenedioxy cathinones, dimethylone among them, incubated with human liver microsomes, cytosol and UDP-glucuronic acid, the key pathways were N-dealkylation, β-ketone reduction, aliphatic hydroxylation, demethylenation and glucuronide conjugation; those with an N,N-dimethylamino group showed higher N-dealkylation and β-ketone reduction ratios. [f:yeh2025]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often with lethal outcomes: in 42 reported cases death occurred in nearly half, with higher fatality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
