## molecule

2,3-MDPV, 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-4-yl)-2-pyrrolidin-1-ylpentan-1-one. Base: C16H21NO3, 275.3 g/mol, CAS 1427205-89-7. Identity checked against PubChem; one stereocentre, not specified there. [f:pubchem] It is the 2,3-methylenedioxy positional isomer of MDPV (3,4-MDPV): same formula, ring substituted at different positions, so it is not the same compound. [f:pubchem]

Compared with MDPV the GC retention is shorter; the mass spectra are equivalent (base peak m/z 126) and the two isomers are told apart by vapour-phase IR bands between 1500 and 1200 cm-1 (GC-IR data, not the ATR spectrum of the solid). [f:abiedalla2016]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often with lethal outcomes: in 42 reported cases death occurred in nearly half, with higher fatality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
