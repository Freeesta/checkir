## molecule

MDMA, 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-N-methylpropan-2-amine. Base: C11H15NO2, 193.24 g/mol, CAS 42542-10-9 (racemate). Hydrochloride: C11H15NO2·HCl, 229.70 g/mol, CAS 64057-70-1. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi]

Forms: the most common salt is the hydrochloride, but phosphate and bromide salts are also seen; usually in tablets, less often as powder, and generally as the racemate. [f:unodc-ats2006]

Diagnostic bands of the hydrochloride: the three methylenedioxy bands, ~1244 (asymmetric C-O-C), ~1032 (symmetric C-O-C) and ~930 cm-1, must be read together; then the ring at ~1489, the CH2 at ~1442 and the out-of-plane band of the 1,2,4-trisubstituted ring at ~800 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-mdma] [f:smith-ethers] [f:smith-benzene] In KBr an N-H2+ sub-band appears near 2450 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-mdma] [f:smith-amine-salts]

Salt or base: the base has no N-H2+ envelope between about 2400 and 3000 cm-1; the SWGDRUG monograph gives the spectrum of the base in ATR and of the hydrochloride in KBr. [f:swgdrug-mdma] [f:smith-amine-salts]

Analogues that one band cannot tell apart: the three methylenedioxy bands point to the group, which MDA, MDEA, MBDB and methylenedioxy cathinones such as methylone share; the full reference spectrum is needed. [f:unodc-ats2006] [f:unodc-cathinones2015] [f:smith-ethers]

Frequent cuts: tablets contain adulterants, cutting agents, colours, excipients and binders; in some regions caffeine and ketamine are often found in amphetamine-type samples. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, methylenedioxy subgroup. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]
