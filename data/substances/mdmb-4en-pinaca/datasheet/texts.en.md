## molecule

MDMB-4en-PINACA, methyl (2S)-3,3-dimethyl-2-[(1-pent-4-enylindazole-3-carbonyl)amino]butanoate (MDMB-PENINACA). Base: C20H27N3O3, 357.4 g/mol, CAS 2504100-70-1. Identity checked against PubChem (the (S) entry). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist (indazole-3-carboxamide of a valine-derived ester with a pent-4-enyl chain). [f:watanabe2019] [f:pubchem]

Tail-less indazole-3-carboxamides such as MDMB-INACA are described as its precursors in a production route found at a clandestine site; the precursor remained in all products of the recreated route, and precursor-product mixtures were found in 28 US casework samples and 87 samples seized in Scottish prisons. [f:monti2025]

## metabolism

In human hepatocytes, liver microsomes, urine and blood 32 metabolites were detected; the alkene of the pentenyl chain is mainly converted to a dihydrodiol, most likely via epoxidation, and most metabolites come from ester hydrolysis and/or dihydrodiol formation. [f:watanabe2019]

The two most abundant in hepatocytes, ester hydrolysis with dihydrodiol (M8) and ester hydrolysis (M30), were also the two found in urine and are proposed as urinary markers. [f:watanabe2019]

In authentic urine, ester hydrolysis and hydroxylation were the major pathways; the existence of ADB-4en-PINACA makes the biomarkers in use not very specific for MDMB-4en-PINACA intake. [f:gu2022]

## cuts

No cutting agent was found in the sources used. The compound is met infused on paper: infused papers with it reached Scottish prisons in June 2019 [f:norman2020], and a letter sent to a German prisoner carried 77 μg/cm2 of it with 5F-ADB and AP-237 [f:giorgetti2022].

It was found in herbal material in Turkey in April 2019 [f:erolozturk2021], and in 2024 one of 31 edible products seized by UK police contained it (mean 11.27 mg per edible) instead of THC [f:gardner2026].

## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

In vitro MDMB-4en-PINACA is a potent CB1 receptor agonist, with an EC50 of 4.3 nM. [f:kronstrand2022]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC, and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

A case report describes severe MDMB-4en-PINACA toxicity after use of an electronic vaping device. [f:osman2026]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
