## metabolism

Olanzapine is highly metabolized: 7% of an oral dose is recovered in urine as unchanged drug, 57% in urine and 30% in feces in total. The major circulating metabolites are the 10-N-glucuronide (44% of the olanzapine concentration at steady state) and 4'-N-desmethylolanzapine (31%); both lack pharmacological activity at the concentrations observed. [f:olanzapine-label]

Direct glucuronidation and CYP-mediated oxidation are the primary pathways; in vitro CYP1A2, CYP2D6 and the flavin-containing monooxygenase system are involved in oxidation, CYP2D6 being a minor pathway in vivo. The half-life ranges from 21 to 54 hours (mean 30). [f:olanzapine-label]
