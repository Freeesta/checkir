## metabolism

Fluoxetine is extensively metabolized in the liver to norfluoxetine and a number of other unidentified metabolites; norfluoxetine, formed by demethylation, is the only identified active metabolite, and S-norfluoxetine has serotonin-uptake inhibiting activity essentially equivalent to fluoxetine. [f:fluoxetine-label]

The elimination half-life is 1 to 3 days after acute administration and 4 to 6 days after chronic administration for fluoxetine, and 4 to 16 days for norfluoxetine. About 7% of the population has reduced CYP2D6 activity (poor metabolizers), who metabolize S-fluoxetine more slowly. [f:fluoxetine-label]
