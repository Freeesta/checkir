## molecule

Flualprazolam, 8-chloro-6-(2-fluorophenyl)-1-methyl-4H-[1,2,4]triazolo[4,3-a][1,4]benzodiazepine. Base: C17H12ClFN4, 326.8 g/mol, CAS 28910-91-0. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is a potent designer benzodiazepine. [f:wagmann2020]

## history

It has no approved medical indication and is clandestinely produced to circumvent regulations. [f:canfield2023]

Between 2020 and 2025 in Australia it was among the most detected novel benzodiazepines. [f:freestone2026]

## origin

It is a synthetic substance that differs from alprazolam by a single fluorine atom. [f:canfield2023]

## metabolism

Seven metabolites were identified in vitro; the enzymes involved include CYP3A4 and UGT1A4. [f:wagmann2020]

In urine the most abundant signals are the glucuronides of alpha-hydroxy and 4-hydroxy flualprazolam and of the parent compound, far more abundant than unchanged flualprazolam. [f:wagmann2020]

In the rat the half-life of flualprazolam is nearly double that of alprazolam. [f:canfield2023]

## effects

Flualprazolam has been associated with sedation, loss of consciousness, memory loss and disinhibition. [f:wagmann2020]

## risks

In the rat fluorination increased half-life and volume of distribution compared with alprazolam, with greater exposure and a potential for greater toxicity. [f:canfield2023]

Health responses are hindered by limited pharmacological data and detection challenges. [f:freestone2026]

## interactions

Because CYP3A4 is involved, interactions with other drugs or with food cannot be excluded. [f:wagmann2020]

For designer benzodiazepines, pharmacodynamic interactions with other central nervous system depressants, such as opioids, are widely documented. [f:el-balkhi2023]

## counselling

Explain that flualprazolam has no approved medical use and that data are limited: it is not equivalent to alprazolam, because fluorination increased half-life and volume of distribution in the rat. [f:canfield2023] [f:freestone2026]

Ask whether there have been memory gaps, loss of consciousness or disinhibition: these effects are described for flualprazolam. [f:wagmann2020]
