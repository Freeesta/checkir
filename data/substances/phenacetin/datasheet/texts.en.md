## category

## risks

IARC classifies phenacetin as carcinogenic to humans (Group 1): it causes cancer of the renal pelvis and of the ureter. [f:iarc-100a-phenacetin]

Phenacetin was extensively used in analgesic mixtures until it was implicated in kidney disease (nephropathy) due to abuse of analgesics. It was withdrawn from the market in Canada in 1978, in the United Kingdom in 1980 and in the United States in 1983, and over-the-counter sales of phenacetin-containing analgesics are prohibited in most countries. [f:iarc-100a-phenacetin]

In humans, N-acetyl-p-aminophenol (paracetamol) is the major urinary metabolite after oral phenacetin. [f:iarc-100a-phenacetin]
