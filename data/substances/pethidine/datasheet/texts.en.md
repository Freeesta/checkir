## molecule

Pethidine, ethyl 1-methyl-4-phenylpiperidine-4-carboxylate. Formula C15H21NO2, 247.33 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

The medicine (meperidine) is a Schedule II controlled substance in the United States. [f:demerol-label]

## history

It was initially synthesised as an anticholinergic agent and was soon found to have analgesic properties. [f:latta2002]

## origin

A synthetic opioid analgesic. [f:latta2002]

## metabolism

Meperidine is metabolised in the liver to normeperidine, mainly by CYP3A4 and CYP2B6 (in vitro data). The half-life of meperidine is 2 to 5 hours and that of normeperidine 15 to 30 hours; both are excreted by the kidneys. [f:demerol-label]

The label links prolonged use to accumulation of normeperidine and to a risk of seizures. [f:demerol-label]
