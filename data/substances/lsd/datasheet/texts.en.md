## molecule

LSD, (6aR,9R)-N,N-diethyl-7-methyl-6,6a,8,9-tetrahydro-4H-indolo[4,3-fg]quinoline-9-carboxamide. Base: C20H25N3O, 323.4 g/mol, CAS 50-37-3. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Structure: an ergoline ring system with an indole, a tertiary diethylamide and a tertiary amine. Expected bands: indole N-H at 3380-3490 cm-1 and the amide C=O at 1620-1690 cm-1. There is no reference spectrum of LSD in the public data, so these ranges only indicate the groups. [f:coates2000] [f:nist-webbook] [f:smith-amides]

Street forms: sheets of absorbent paper, less often microdots, gelatine squares ('window panes') or capsules. LSD is light-sensitive in solution and more stable in dosage units. [f:euda-lsd]

## category

Chemical class: lysergamides (family sheet: tryptamines). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

LSD was synthesised in 1938 and its psychoactive effects were discovered in 1943. In the 1950s and 1960s it was used in psychiatric research and psychotherapy; from the mid-1960s it became an illegal drug. [f:passie2008]

Recreational use became popular between the 1960s and the 1980s and is now less common. [f:euda-lsd]

## origin

EUDA describes LSD as a semi-synthetic hallucinogen. Most of it is produced outside Europe, while the preparation of dosage units by dipping or spotting paper squares is more widespread. [f:euda-lsd]

## metabolism

In vitro, CYP2D6, CYP2E1 and CYP3A4 contribute to the formation of nor-LSD, and CYP1A2, CYP2C9, CYP2E1 and CYP3A4 to 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD. Nor-LSD is probably active; 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD is inactive. [f:luethi2019-lsd]

People with no functional CYP2D6 had longer LSD half-lives and about 75% higher plasma exposure (AUC) to LSD and to 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD than carriers of functional CYP2D6 (81 healthy subjects pooled from 4 trials). [f:vizeli2021]

After 85 and 170 µg by mouth the elimination half-lives were 3.7 and 4.0 hours. Within 24 hours 1% of the dose was recovered unchanged in urine and 16% as 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD. [f:holze2023]

## effects

In 28 healthy volunteers the subjective effects lasted 9.3 ± 3.2 hours after 85 µg and 11 ± 3.7 hours after 170 µg; the maximal effect was rated 77% and 87%. [f:holze2023]

The pharmacology of LSD is complex and its mechanisms of action are still not completely understood. [f:passie2008]

LSD and nor-LSD are active at several serotonin receptors in vitro; 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD has much weaker affinity and activity. [f:luethi2019-lsd]

## doses

Doses in the trial: 85 and 170 µg by mouth. Peak concentrations were 1.8 and 3.4 ng/mL, reached after 1.7 hours on average; exposure was proportional to the dose. [f:holze2023]

The UBC guidelines give 50-150 µg as the usual range and 150-400 µg for very intense experiences, with onset after 20-60 minutes and effects lasting 8-12 hours; the trial doses fall in these two ranges. [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines] [f:holze2023]

## risks

Hallucinogens are relatively well tolerated physiologically and are not considered drugs of dependence, but they carry unique psychological risks. The most likely is overwhelming distress during drug action (a 'bad trip'), which can lead to dangerous behaviour; prolonged psychoses are less common. [f:johnson2008]

LSD is physiologically well tolerated in a medically supervised setting, but complications can easily result from uncontrolled use by laypeople. [f:passie2008]

Hallucinogen persisting perception disorder (HPPD), the re-experiencing of perceptual disturbances after the drug has worn off, is rarely encountered clinically; it takes two forms, brief flashbacks and a chronic form lasting months to years. [f:halpern2018]

NBOMe compounds have been sold as LSD on blotter paper; two deaths of teenagers in Indiana in 2014 were related to 25B-NBOMe and 25I-NBOMe. [f:shanks2015]

## interactions

Among 62 online reports of lithium taken with classic psychedelics such as LSD or psilocybin, 47% involved seizures; none of 34 reports with lamotrigine did. These are self-reports, not controlled data. [f:nayak2021]

In vitro, rifampicin clearly increased the formation of nor-LSD and of 2-oxo-3-hydroxy-LSD; the authors state that interaction studies in humans are needed. [f:luethi2019-lsd]

## counselling

Explain why identity matters: NBOMe compounds have been sold as LSD on blotter paper and linked to deaths, and the UBC guidelines recommend having the dose analysed to know whether it is LSD, an NBOMe, DOC or something else. [f:shanks2015] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Say that the effects are long, on average 9-11 hours in trials, and that the UBC guidelines advise against raising the dose when the usual effect does not come, because with repeated doses in a short time tolerance builds up quickly. [f:holze2023] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about mood, health and surroundings: the most likely risk is overwhelming distress, and the UBC guidelines tie it to set and setting and advise people with mental illness or heart and circulation problems to abstain. [f:johnson2008] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about medicines, above all lithium: seizures were reported in 47% of 62 online reports of lithium with classic psychedelics. [f:nayak2021]

Talk about the days after: HPPD is rare, and the UBC guidelines suggest at least one day of quiet to process the experience. [f:halpern2018] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
