## molecule

Ethylone (bk-MDEA, 3,4-methylenedioxy-N-ethylcathinone), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-(ethylamino)propan-1-one. Base: C12H15NO3, 221.25 g/mol, CAS 1112937-64-0. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem] Hydrochloride: C12H15NO3·HCl, 257 g/mol, melting range 235.6-242.6 °C. [f:swgdrug-ethylone]

Bands of the hydrochloride (ATR) labelled in the SWGDRUG spectrum: aryl ketone C=O at 1674 cm-1, ring at 1605 cm-1, N-H2+ envelope with bands at 2978, 2710 and 2459 cm-1, then 1452, 1389, 1356, 1298 and 1257 cm-1, and the bands at 1119, 1090, 1038, 993 and 933 cm-1, in the region of the methylenedioxy and aryl ether groups. [f:swgdrug-ethylone] [f:smith-ethers] [f:smith-amine-salts]

Ethylone hydrochloride exists as two polymorphs that FTIR can tell apart, so a library spectrum may match only one of them. [f:maheux2016] Ethylone and butylone are isomers that LC-MS-MS can resolve. [f:strickland2019]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Laboratories began to notice ethylone-positive products in late 2011. [f:lee2015]

Since 2012 illicit shipments of ethylone hydrochloride have been intercepted with increasing frequency at the Canadian border. [f:maheux2016]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a cathinone with a methylenedioxy ring. Seized exhibits showed several minor impurities and the two polymorphs of the hydrochloride, in one case mixed with methylone hydrochloride. [f:maheux2016]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes nine methylenedioxy cathinones, ethylone among them, were metabolised by N-dealkylation, β-ketone reduction, aliphatic hydroxylation, demethylenation and glucuronide conjugation. [f:yeh2025]

In 34,561 urine specimens the methylenedioxy cathinones were reduced at the carbonyl to a much lesser extent than the other cathinones and were best detected as such in free urine. [f:uralets2014]

## effects

In rats ethylone, dibutylone and N-ethylpentylone were self-administered, so they function as reinforcers with addictive potential; the reinforcing potency was methamphetamine > N-ethylpentylone ≈ dibutylone > ethylone. [f:lai2022]

The sources read give no controlled data on the effects of ethylone in humans. [f:lee2015]

## risks

A forensic toxicology laboratory at the University of Florida reported nine post-mortem cases with ethylone: seven had measurable blood concentrations, from 38 to 2,572 ng/mL. In five cases with ethylone above 400 ng/mL no other drug was found apart from ethanol, a cannabis metabolite and, in one, doxylamine. [f:lee2015]

The authors say the findings help to evaluate a toxic concentration range. [f:lee2015]

## interactions

In the nine cases other substances were found together with ethylone: ethanol, alprazolam, benzoylecgonine, diphenhydramine, morphine, tramadol and a cannabis metabolite; the report does not establish a causal link. [f:lee2015]

## counselling

Ask what the person expects: ethylone and butylone are isomers, and the hydrochloride has two polymorphs, so an IR result needs a reference of the same form; a mixture with methylone has been described. [f:strickland2019] [f:maheux2016]

Ask about repeated intakes: in rats ethylone is a reinforcer, and the UBC guidelines warn that cathinone effects seem minimal at first and that repeated intakes lead to overdose. [f:lai2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about alcohol, benzodiazepines, opioids and other drugs: they were found together with ethylone in the post-mortem cases; avoid presenting this as a causal link. [f:lee2015] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

For the test: cathinones give no colour with the Marquis reagent and immunoassay tests for methamphetamine can give false positives with some of them. [f:euda-cathinones]
