## molecule

2-MeO-diphenidine (methoxphenidine, MXP), 1-[1-(2-methoxyphenyl)-2-phenylethyl]piperidine. Formula C20H25NO, 295.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a 1,2-diarylethylamine (an analogue of diphenidine), not an arylcyclohexylamine, and the arylcyclohexylamines family sheet covers phencyclidine- and ketamine-type compounds, so no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:hofer2014] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, available since 2013 as a 'research chemical' sold as an alternative to the dissociative anaesthetics methoxetamine and ketamine. [f:elliott2015]

## metabolism

In post-mortem blood and urine from three fatal cases, the proposed main metabolite was hydroxy-2-MXP, with the hydroxyl group on the piperidine ring; O-desmethyl-2-MXP and a hydroxylated O-desmethyl metabolite were also detected. No half-life or enzymes are reported. [f:elliott2015]
