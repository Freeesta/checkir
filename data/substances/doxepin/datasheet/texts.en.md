## molecule

Doxepin, 3-(6H-benzo[c][1]benzoxepin-11-ylidene)-N,N-dimethylpropan-1-amine. Formula C19H21NO, 279.4 g/mol, CAS 1668-19-5. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. The label describes doxepin tablets indicated for insomnia characterized by difficulty with sleep maintenance. The effect axis has no hypnotic class: "depressant/sedative" is a checkir proposal awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:silenor-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, marketed as tablets for the treatment of insomnia. [f:silenor-label]

## metabolism

Doxepin is extensively metabolized by oxidation and demethylation; the primary metabolite is N-desmethyldoxepin (nordoxepin), further converted to glucuronide conjugates. In vitro CYP2C19 and CYP2D6 are the major enzymes and CYP1A2 and CYP2C9 are involved to a lesser extent. [f:silenor-label]

Less than 3% of a dose is excreted in the urine as parent compound or nordoxepin, mainly it is excreted as glucuronides. The apparent terminal half-life is 15.3 hours for doxepin and 31 hours for nordoxepin. [f:silenor-label]
