## molecule

Carbamazepine, benzo[b][1]benzazepine-11-carboxamide. Formula C15H12N2O, 236.27 g/mol, CAS 298-46-4. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is an antiepileptic (dibenzazepine carboxamide) also used for trigeminal neuralgia. Expected effect: none psychoactive. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:tegretol-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: an anticonvulsant chemically unrelated to other antiepileptic drugs. [f:tegretol-label]

## metabolism

Carbamazepine is metabolized in the liver; CYP3A4 is the major isoform forming carbamazepine-10,11-epoxide, which is anticonvulsant in animal models, and microsomal epoxide hydrolase forms the 10,11-transdiol from it. [f:tegretol-label]

After a radiolabelled oral dose 72% of the radioactivity was in the urine and 28% in the faeces, largely as hydroxylated and conjugated metabolites, with 3% unchanged. [f:tegretol-label]

The drug induces its own metabolism: the initial half-life of 25 to 65 hours falls to 12 to 17 hours on repeated doses. [f:tegretol-label]
