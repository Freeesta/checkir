## molecule

4-Methyl-N-ethylnorephedrine, 2-(ethylamino)-1-(4-methylphenyl)propan-1-ol. Formula C12H19NO, 193.28 g/mol, CAS 1340234-30-1. Identity checked on PubChem by structure (CID 64125548; the entry has unspecified stereocentres and no entry carries this name). [f:pubchem]

It is 4-MEC (sheet `4-mec`) with the keto group reduced to a hydroxyl group. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Not a commercial substance: it is the structure obtained when 4-MEC loses its keto group by reduction, a metabolic reaction described in humans. [f:helfer2014]

## metabolism

In human urine and liver microsomes, 4-MEC (sheet `4-mec`) is metabolised by reduction of the keto group, N-deethylation and oxidation of the 4-methyl group, and combinations of these steps; the keto-reduced (dihydro) metabolites correspond to this structure. [f:helfer2014]
