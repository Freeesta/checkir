## molecule

Promethazine, N,N-dimethyl-1-phenothiazin-10-ylpropan-2-amine. Formula C17H20N2S, 284.4 g/mol, CAS 60-87-7. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is a racemic compound, a phenothiazine derivative, marketed as the hydrochloride. [f:promethazine-label]

## category

Chemical class: phenothiazine derivative with antihistaminic, sedative and antiemetic action (grouped here under other substances); no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: depressant or sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:promethazine-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a racemic phenothiazine derivative marketed in tablets and other forms. [f:promethazine-label]

## metabolism

Promethazine is metabolized by the liver to a variety of compounds; the sulfoxides of promethazine and of N-demethylpromethazine are the predominant metabolites appearing in the urine. [f:promethazine-label]
