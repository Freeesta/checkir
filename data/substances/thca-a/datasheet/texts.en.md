## molecule

THCA-A (Δ9-tetrahydrocannabinolic acid A), (6aR,10aR)-1-hydroxy-6,6,9-trimethyl-3-pentyl-6a,7,8,10a-tetrahydrobenzo[c]chromene-2-carboxylic acid. Base: C22H30O4, 358.5 g/mol, CAS 23978-85-0. Identity checked against PubChem (entry with the (6aR,10aR) stereochemistry). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Phytocannabinoid of Cannabis sativa, one of the main ingredients of the plant; it is converted by decarboxylation into the psychoactive Δ9-THC when heated. [f:hofert2024-thca]

Its decarboxylation follows first-order kinetics with no side reactions or by-products, and its rate constants were twice those of cannabidiolic acid and cannabigerolic acid. [f:wang2016-decarboxylation]

## metabolism

During cannabis consumption a varying proportion of THCA-A is absorbed into the body. In serum/plasma samples of drivers suspected of cannabis consumption, THCA-A was quantifiable in 407 (47.1 %) of 865 samples with THC above the German analytical cutoff of 1 ng/mL; it may be an additional marker of consumption frequency and acuteness, to be confirmed by further studies. [f:hofert2024-thca]

## effects

THCA-A lacks cannabimimetic effects: it showed little affinity or efficacy at CB1 and CB2 (approximate Ki 3.1 μM and 12.5 μM), whereas THC had 62-fold and 125-fold greater affinity; the THCA-A standard contained 2 % THC, so part of the binding may be an artefact of decarboxylation. [f:mcpartland2017-thca]
