## molecule

O-Desmethyl-cis-tramadol, 3-[2-[(dimethylamino)methyl]-1-hydroxycyclohexyl]phenol. Formula C15H23NO2, 249.35 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. The PubChem entry does not specify the stereochemistry. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is the O-demethylated form of tramadol; O-desmethyltramadol (M1) is the main active metabolite of tramadol (see the tramadol sheet) and binds to μ-opioid receptors with higher affinity than the parent compound. [f:barbosa2016] [f:tramadol-label]

## metabolism

Tramadol is converted to O-desmethyltramadol (M1) mainly by CYP2D6; M1 then undergoes mainly glucuronidation. [f:barbosa2016]

The mean terminal plasma half-life of racemic M1 after an oral dose of tramadol is 7.4 hours. [f:tramadol-label]
