## molecule

3,4-Dimethylmethcathinone (3,4-DMMC), 1-(3,4-dimethylphenyl)-2-(methylamino)propan-1-one. Base: C12H17NO, 191.27 g/mol, CAS 1082110-00-6. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

3,4-DMMC is a new psychoactive substance whose recreational use and trade had recently increased when the first rat toxicokinetic study was published. [f:rouxinol2019]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a new psychoactive substance of the cathinone class. [f:rouxinol2019]

## metabolism

In Wistar rats given 3,4-DMMC intraperitoneally, two metabolites were detected in all tissues analysed after 1 h; after 24 h the drug was found only in urine, together with five metabolites, all tentatively identified. [f:rouxinol2019]
