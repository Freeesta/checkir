## molecule

Benzylfentanyl, N-(1-benzylpiperidin-4-yl)-N-phenylpropanamide. Base: C21H26N2O, 322.4 g/mol, CAS 1474-02-8. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: fentanyl analogues (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Benzylfentanyl was among the novel synthetic opioids found in hair of a Swiss cohort of 439 opioid users. [f:polke2024]

On one-dollar bills collected in 13 US cities in 2022, benzylfentanyl, acetylfentanyl and 4-anilino-N-phenethylpiperidine were detected on 15% of samples in amounts of no more than 0.69 μg per bill. [f:hewes2024]

## origin

A synthetic opioid listed among the novel synthetic opioids of a hair analysis method. [f:polke2024]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]
