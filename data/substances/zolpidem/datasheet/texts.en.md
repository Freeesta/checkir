## molecule

Zolpidem, N,N-dimethyl-2-[6-methyl-2-(4-methylphenyl)imidazo[1,2-a]pyridin-3-yl]acetamide. C19H21N3O, 307.4 g/mol, CAS 82626-48-0. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An imidazopyridine, not a benzodiazepine: it has no benzodiazepine ring. [f:pubchem] [f:vonmoltke1999]

A synthetic substance, a pharmaceutical hypnotic. [f:vonmoltke1999] [f:checkir-classification]


## metabolism

In human liver microsomes and in expressed enzymes zolpidem is hydroxylated to three metabolites, of which M-3 accounts for more than 80% of net intrinsic clearance; the projected contributions of the enzymes are 61% CYP3A4, 22% CYP2C9, 14% CYP1A2 and less than 3% each for CYP2D6 and CYP2C19. [f:vonmoltke1999]

According to the US label zolpidem is converted to inactive metabolites eliminated mainly in the urine; the mean elimination half-life is 2.5 h (range 1.4-3.8) for the 10 mg tablet. [f:ambien-label]
