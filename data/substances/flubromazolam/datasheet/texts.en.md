## molecule

Flubromazolam, 8-bromo-6-(2-fluorophenyl)-1-methyl-4H-[1,2,4]triazolo[4,3-a][1,4]benzodiazepine. Base: C17H12BrFN4, 371.2 g/mol, CAS 612526-40-6. Identity checked against PubChem and Wikidata. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines in the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is a highly potent designer benzodiazepine. [f:huppertz2018]

## history

It has been offered on the Internet since 2014. [f:huppertz2018]

Between 2020 and 2025 in Australia it was among the most detected novel benzodiazepines. [f:freestone2026]

## origin

It is a synthetic substance, the triazolo analogue of flubromazepam. [f:huppertz2018]

## metabolism

After 0.5 mg taken by a single volunteer, flubromazolam and its mono-hydroxylated metabolite stayed detectable in urine for up to 6.5 and 8 days; glucuronides were also found. [f:huppertz2018]

The estimated terminal half-life in that person is 10-20 hours; the serum peak was 8 ng/mL at 8 hours after ingestion. [f:huppertz2018]

## effects

Flubromazolam is described as a highly potent designer benzodiazepine. [f:huppertz2018]

## doses

The only dose described is from a study: 0.5 mg in a capsule, taken by a single volunteer. [f:huppertz2018]

## risks

A 36-year-old man had prolonged bradycardia after flubromazolam bought online. [f:bohnenberger2019]

Designer products have no enforced quality standards: adverse effects are unpredictable and can be toxic. [f:bohnenberger2019]

In the rat, compared with alprazolam, flualprazolam and flubromazolam showed about double the volume of distribution and clearance, with greater exposure and a potential for greater toxicity. [f:canfield2023]

## interactions

For designer benzodiazepines, pharmacodynamic interactions with other central nervous system depressants, such as opioids, are widely documented. [f:el-balkhi2023]

## counselling

Explain that it is a highly potent designer benzodiazepine, sold without quality controls, with unpredictable adverse effects. [f:huppertz2018] [f:bohnenberger2019]

Ask when the person last took it and explain that, in an estimate from a single person, the half-life is 10-20 hours and the substance stays detectable in urine for days. [f:huppertz2018]

Ask whether there has been a very slow heartbeat or fainting: prolonged bradycardia has been described after flubromazolam. [f:bohnenberger2019]
