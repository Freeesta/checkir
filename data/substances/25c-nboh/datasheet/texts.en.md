## molecule

25C-NBOH, 2-[[2-(4-chloro-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethylamino]methyl]phenol. Formula C17H20ClNO3, 321.8 g/mol, CAS 1391488-16-6. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

In place of the 2-methoxybenzyl group of the NBOMes it has a 2-hydroxybenzyl group (a phenol) on the nitrogen of 2C-C; the NBOH series is described with the same structural layout as the NBOMes. [f:pubchem] [f:pelletier2024]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines (N-benzyl phenethylamines); outside the NBOMe family, which covers only the 2-methoxybenzyl derivatives. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:checkir-classification]

## history

25C-NBOH is one of four NBOH compounds (25B, 25C, 25E, 25I) that appeared on the illicit market, similar in structure to the 25X-NBOMes. [f:munir2025]

## origin

A synthetic substance, an N-benzyl derivative of 2C-C. [f:pubchem]
