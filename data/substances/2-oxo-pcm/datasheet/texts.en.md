## category

Chemical class: arylcyclohexylamines. Expected effect: dissociative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Synthetic compound, a ketamine analogue: ketamine without the chlorine on the ring. [f:mezaache2026]

## metabolism

In rat brain after a high dose, hydroxylated and demethylated metabolites of deschloroketamine were found, among them norDXE (the N-demethylated metabolite), with a higher concentration of (R)-norDXE than of (S)-norDXE. [f:paskanova2026]
