## molecule

MDA (tenamfetamine), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)propan-2-amine. Base: C10H13NO2, 179.22 g/mol, CAS 4764-17-4. Hydrochloride: C10H13NO2·HCl, 215.7 g/mol, CAS 6292-91-7. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and ChEBI. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:chebi] [f:swgdrug-mda]

Forms: the base is an oil; SWGDRUG gives ATR spectra of the hydrochloride and of the sulfate. The methylenedioxy-type stimulants are usually found as tablets and only occasionally as powder, and the most common salt is the hydrochloride. [f:swgdrug-mda] [f:unodc-ats2006]

Diagnostic bands: the methylenedioxy group gives the characteristic C-O-C bands of ethers, but it is shared with MDMA and MDEA, so a band of the group does not tell the three apart; compare the full spectrum with the reference one. [f:smith-ethers] [f:unodc-ats2006] [f:swgdrug-mda]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, methylenedioxy subgroup. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Synthetic compound. The precursor classes are those of MDMA: 3,4-methylenedioxyphenyl-2-propanone, safrole (also via isosafrole) and piperonal. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## metabolism

MDA is mainly demethylenated to the catechol dihydroxyamphetamine; among the human P450 enzymes tested, CYP2D6 contributed most at concentrations like those of recreational users, and the S-enantiomer was transformed preferentially. [f:meyer2009mda]

After 1.4 mg/kg of oral racemic MDA in healthy volunteers, Cmax was 229 ± 39 µg/L for MDA and 92 ± 61 µg/L for the metabolite 4-hydroxy-3-methoxyamphetamine (HMA). [f:baggott2019]

## effects

In a double-blind, placebo-controlled study with 1.4 mg/kg of oral racemic MDA, the self-reported effects shared features with MDMA and with classical psychedelics and lasted longer than those of MDMA: still elevated at 8 hours, while those of MDMA had resolved by 6 hours. [f:baggott2019]

MDA shares some of the entactogenic effects of MDMA but also has stimulant effects and, at higher doses, psychedelic properties. [f:kolaczynska2022]

Among users who handed in ecstasy tablets for analysis in the Netherlands, MDA was reported as not appreciated as a replacement for MDMA (self-reported data). [f:brunt2012]

## doses

The drug content of tablets of the methylenedioxy-type stimulants (MDMA, MDA, MDEA) usually ranges from 40 to 140 mg and varies with the region and over time. [f:unodc-ats2006]

## risks

In the same study MDA induced robust increases in heart rate and blood pressure and increased cortisol and prolactin to a degree similar to MDMA; it was well tolerated by the participants. [f:baggott2019]

## counselling

Explain that the effects of MDA last longer than those of MDMA: in a controlled study they were still elevated at 8 hours, while those of MDMA had resolved by 6 hours, so the time to wait is not the one known for MDMA. [f:baggott2019]

Ask what the person thinks they have: tablets sold as ecstasy are often MDMA, MDA or MDEA, structurally similar substances whose effects differ in duration, intensity and dose, so it matters to know which one is being taken. [f:unodc-ats2006] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about heart and blood-pressure conditions: in healthy volunteers MDA raised heart rate and blood pressure markedly. [f:baggott2019] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
