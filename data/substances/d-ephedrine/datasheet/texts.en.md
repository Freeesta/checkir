## molecule

d-Ephedrine ((+)-ephedrine), (1S,2R)-2-(methylamino)-1-phenylpropan-1-ol. Formula C10H15NO, 165.23 g/mol, CAS 321-98-2. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

It is the enantiomer of (−)-ephedrine, the (1R,2S) form of the ephedrine sheet. The four isomers are (1R,2S)- and (1S,2R)-ephedrine and (1S,2S)- and (1R,2R)-pseudoephedrine, so (+)-pseudoephedrine, (1S,2S), is a different compound. [f:rice2018]

The SWGDRUG monograph named d-Ephedrine describes the (1S,2R) enantiomer; the IR bands of the ephedrine sheet come from it. [f:swgdrug-ephedrine]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines, as for the ephedrine sheet. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

In human liver microsome assays (1S,2R)-(+)-ephedrine was not a human-derived product; it was found in wastewater effluent and formed from (−)-ephedrine in river-simulating microcosms, apparently through microbial processes. [f:rice2018]
