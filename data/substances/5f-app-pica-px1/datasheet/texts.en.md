## molecule

5F-APP-PICA (PX-1, SRF-30), N-[(2S)-1-amino-1-oxo-3-phenylpropan-2-yl]-1-(5-fluoropentyl)indole-3-carboxamide (l-phenylalaninamide of 5-fluoropentylindole-3-carboxylic acid). Base: C23H26FN3O2, 395.5 g/mol, CAS 2221100-71-4. Identity checked against PubChem (the (S) entry, where PX-1 and 5F-APP-PICA are listed together). It has a terminal amide, unlike 5F-MPP-PICA (sheet 5f-mpp-pica), which has the phenylalanine methyl ester. [f:pubchem] [f:dahm2021]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cannabinoids (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist (indole-3-carboxamide of a phenylalaninamide with a fluoropentyl chain). [f:jones2025] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes PX-1 had 30 metabolites (PX-2: 15); the main ones were amide-hydrolysed and indole-monohydroxylated, and CYP2E1 was responsible for many pathways, particularly for PX-1. [f:dahm2021]

Another microsome study found 10 metabolites, with simultaneous defluorination and monohydroxylation of the pentyl chain (M1) as the primary product, further oxidised to a carboxypentyl metabolite; three monohydroxylated metabolites, one keeping the fluoropentyl chain, were proposed as consumption markers. [f:presley2020]

PX-1 was completely metabolised by human hepatocytes (12 metabolites) [f:fabregat2019-px1] and its half-life in microsomes was 15.1 ± 1.02 min. [f:cooman2019]

## effects

In a NanoLuc bioassay 5F-APP-PICA showed lower potency and maximal response than AMB-FUBINACA at CB1 and CB2 receptors. [f:fabregat2019-px1]

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

For ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA, full CB1 agonism explains altered perception, agitation, anxiety, paranoia, hallucinations, loss of consciousness, chest pain, hypertension, tachycardia and seizures; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## doses

Potency varies widely among synthetic cannabinoids: ADB-FUBINACA and AMB-FUBINACA are up to 140 and 85 times more potent than Δ9-THC and no potency data for this compound were found in the sources used, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lobato-freitas2021]

## risks

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
