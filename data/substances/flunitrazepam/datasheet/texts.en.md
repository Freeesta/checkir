## molecule

Flunitrazepam, 5-(2-fluorophenyl)-1-methyl-7-nitro-3H-1,4-benzodiazepin-2-one. Formula C16H12FN3O3, 313.28 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: benzodiazepines. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative). Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance: a potent hypnotic, sedative and amnestic benzodiazepine used to treat insomnia and as a pre-anaesthetic agent. [f:dinis-oliveira2017a]

## metabolism

Main pathways are N-demethylation, 3-hydroxylation and nitro-reduction followed by N-acetylation, giving N-desmethylflunitrazepam, 3-hydroxyflunitrazepam, 7-aminoflunitrazepam and 7-acetamidoflunitrazepam. [f:dinis-oliveira2017a]

In human liver microsomes 3-hydroxylation accounted for more than 80% of the intrinsic clearance, and CYP3A accounted for most of it for both 3-hydroxylation and N-demethylation; recombinant CYP2C19 also N-demethylated it (in vitro result). [f:hesse2001]
