## molecule

Pentylone (bk-MBDP), 1-(1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)-2-(methylamino)pentan-1-one. Base: C13H17NO3, 235.3 g/mol, CAS 698963-77-8. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified). [f:pubchem]

The 2,3-methylenedioxy isomer is a different compound (sheet `2-3-pentylone`); the mass, NMR and IR spectra of pentylone are described in the literature together with its isocathinone by-products. [f:pubchem] [f:westphal2012]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a cathinone with a methylenedioxy ring and a three-carbon side chain. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes, for nine methylenedioxy cathinones including pentylone the key pathways are N-dealkylation, β-ketone reduction, aliphatic hydroxylation, demethylenation and glucuronide conjugation; in vitro data, not in humans. [f:yeh2025]

Pentylone is also the primary N-desmethyl metabolite of N,N-dimethylpentylone. [f:fogarty2025]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
