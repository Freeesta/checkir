## molecule

MDAI (5,6-methylenedioxy-2-aminoindane), 6,7-dihydro-5H-cyclopenta[f][1,3]benzodioxol-6-amine. Base: C10H11NO2, 177.20 g/mol, CAS 132741-81-2. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem] Hydrochloride: C10H11NO2·HCl, 213 g/mol, beige powder. [f:swgdrug-mdai]

Bands of the hydrochloride (diamond ATR) labelled in the SWGDRUG spectrum: N-H3+ envelope with a band at 2521 cm-1, then 1468 cm-1, and the bands at 1257, 1238, 1146, 1034 and 931 cm-1, in the region of the methylenedioxy and aryl ether groups, and a band at 469 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-mdai] [f:smith-ethers] [f:smith-amine-salts]

## category

Chemical class: aminoindanes. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

Aminoindanes were first synthesised for medical use, for example as anti-Parkinsonian drugs, and later as compounds that might facilitate psychotherapy. [f:pinterova2017]

MDAI was developed in the 1990s but was not widely abused until 2010, when mephedrone was banned in the UK and MDAI was advertised as a legal alternative. [f:gallagher2012]

## origin

A fully synthetic substance, an analogue of MDMA. [f:gallagher2012]

Internet-sourced products sold as MDAI have been shown to contain mephedrone or mixed inorganic substances and no MDAI. [f:gallagher2012]

## metabolism

In rats the main pathways were oxidative demethylenation followed by O-methylation and N-acetylation, giving five metabolites found mostly as glucuronides and sulphates; most of the dose was excreted unchanged. [f:zidkova2017]

In six volunteers MDAI was detectable in serum from about 20 minutes after ingestion for at least 4 days, and in urine for at least 6 days. [f:angerer2024]

## effects

In six healthy volunteers MDAI produced subjective effects comparable with those of 125 mg MDMA and raised blood pressure similarly, without raising heart rate or body temperature; it increased cortisol and prolactin. [f:angerer2024]

In human transporter cells MDAI preferentially inhibited the serotonin and norepinephrine transporters and released serotonin. [f:simmler2014]

## doses

In a study of six healthy volunteers MDAI was given at 3.0 mg/kg (180-228 mg), not blinded and without placebo. [f:angerer2024]

## risks

A man was found dead after two powders, 68% MDAI and 87% 5-EAPB; blood contained MDAI 2.09 mg/L and 5-EAPB 6.45 mg/L, and the cause of death was attributed to these substances. [f:deville2019]

A fatal case involved MDAI and 2-MAPB; no relevant post-mortem redistribution of either was seen between peripheral and heart blood. [f:staeheli2017]

A review reports deaths related to aminoindanes and fatal toxic effects in animal studies. [f:pinterova2017]

## interactions

Because the mechanism is mainly serotonergic, MDAI may pose a significant risk of serotonin syndrome at high doses or when combined with other drugs. [f:pinterova2017]

## counselling

Ask what name the person was given: products sold as MDAI have contained mephedrone or inorganic substances, and under the "ecstasy" label the UBC guidelines note structurally similar substances with different effects, duration and doses. [f:gallagher2012] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about other substances and medicines acting on serotonin: the review warns of serotonin syndrome at high doses or in combination. [f:pinterova2017]

If the person asks about urine tests, explain that after 3.0 mg/kg MDAI stayed detectable in urine for at least 6 days in six volunteers. [f:angerer2024]
