## molecule

O6-Acetylmorphine, [(4R,4aR,7S,7aR,12bS)-9-hydroxy-3-methyl-2,4,4a,7,7a,13-hexahydro-1H-4,12-methanobenzofuro[3,2-e]isoquinolin-7-yl] acetate. Formula C19H21NO4, 327.4 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: synthesis or degradation residue. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

It is the first metabolite of heroin (see the heroin and morphine sheets), and seized heroin samples also contain it, together with acetylcodeine and morphine. [f:milella2023] [f:tittarelli2020]

## origin

A semisynthetic opioid, the 6-acetyl ester of morphine, an alkaloid of opium; in the body it forms from heroin by deacetylation. [f:pubchem] [f:milella2023]

## metabolism

Heroin is deacetylated rapidly to 6-monoacetylmorphine (6-MAM) and then to morphine by plasma cholinesterases and liver carboxylesterases; morphine is glucuronidated, and morphine-6-glucuronide is active. 6-MAM is itself an active metabolite of heroin. [f:milella2023]
