## molecule

6-APB (6-(2-aminopropyl)benzofuran, Benzo-Fury), 1-(1-benzofuran-6-yl)propan-2-amine. Base: C11H13NO, 175.23 g/mol, CAS 286834-85-3. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem] Hydrochloride: C11H13NO·HCl, 212 g/mol, white powder, melting point 164.7 °C. [f:swgdrug-6apb]

6-APB and 6-MAPB can be told apart from their position isomers 5-APB and 5-MAPB in urine by GC-MS retention times, after solid phase extraction and derivatisation. [f:welter2015]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines. Expected effect: entactogen. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

The 2-aminopropylbenzofurans were sold as analogues of amphetamines; the 5-isomers came first and 6-APB appeared afterwards. [f:welter2015]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a benzofuran analogue of amphetamine. [f:brandt2020]

6-APB was one of 11 new psychoactive substances found in 65 seized chocolate samples. [f:song2023]

## metabolism

In rat urine and human liver preparations the main metabolite of 6-APB besides the parent drug was 4-carboxymethyl-3-hydroxyamphetamine. [f:welter2015]

The N-demethylation of 6-MAPB to 6-APB involved CYP1A2, CYP2D6 and CYP3A4. [f:welter2015]

In healthy volunteers who took active doses, the parent drug in urine was a suitable marker of intake at a 50 ng/mL reporting limit. [f:olesti2020]

## effects

In rat brain synaptosomes 6-APB was a substrate-type releaser at the dopamine, norepinephrine and serotonin transporters, at least threefold more potent than MDA and MDMA. In rats it raised extracellular dopamine and serotonin, more potently than MDA. [f:brandt2020]

The 2-aminopropylbenzofurans were consumed for their euphoric and empathogenic effects. [f:welter2015]

## risks

In a fatal intoxication with 5-APB and 6-APB, peripheral blood contained 850 ng/mL 5-APB and 300 ng/mL 6-APB; the authors note that published fatal cases mostly involved both. [f:hofmann2022]

In human liver and primary rat hepatocyte cells 6-APB was cytotoxic, less than 5-APB, with oxidative stress, mitochondrial disruption and apoptosis (in vitro data). [f:roquebravo2020]

## interactions

In rat hepatocytes, co-incubation with CYP450 inhibitors showed that metabolism, in particular by CYP3A4, contributed to the toxic effects of 6-APB and 5-APB; the sources read give no human interaction data. [f:roquebravo2020]

## counselling

Ask what name the person was given: 6-APB has a position isomer, 5-APB, and published fatal cases mostly involved both. [f:welter2015] [f:hofmann2022]

Explain that in rat tests the benzofurans were at least threefold more potent than MDA and MDMA in evoking release; the sources read give no human potency data. [f:brandt2020]

Ask in what form the product came: 6-APB has been found in seized chocolate. [f:song2023]
