## molecule

4-Fluoroethylphenidate (4F-EPH), ethyl 2-(4-fluorophenyl)-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Formula C15H20FNO2, 265.32 g/mol, CAS 2160555-59-7. Identity checked on PubChem; the entry has unspecified stereocentres. [f:pubchem]

The molecule has two stereocentres, so a threo and an erythro form exist; samples of the threo form and of mixtures of both have been reported. [f:sakamoto2023]

## category

Chemical class: phenidates. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a ring-fluorinated analogue of ethylphenidate, among the methylphenidate analogues sold as new psychoactive substances. [f:carlier2019]
