## molecule

2C-T-7, 2-[2,5-dimethoxy-4-(propylsulfanyl)phenyl]ethan-1-amine. Base: C13H21NO2S, 255.38 g/mol, CAS 207740-26-9. Hydrochloride: C13H21NO2S·HCl, about 291 g/mol, off-white powder. Identity checked against PubChem, Wikidata and the SWGDRUG monograph. [f:pubchem] [f:wikidata] [f:swgdrug-2ct7]

Forms: the SWGDRUG reference material is the hydrochloride, with a diamond ATR spectrum (3 bounces, 4 cm-1 resolution). [f:swgdrug-2ct7] The SWGDRUG monograph of 2C-T-2 notes that vapour-phase IR cannot tell 2C-T-2 from 2C-T-7. [f:swgdrug-2ct2]

The 2,5-dimethoxy phenethylamines of the 2C series share the aryl ether bands: a single band points to the group, not to the substance. [f:smith-ethers]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, 2C series (4-thio derivatives). Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:halberstadt2022] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

2C-T-2 and 2C-T-7 produce psychedelic effects in humans and have been distributed as recreational drugs. [f:halberstadt2022]

## origin

A synthetic substance (designer drug) of the 2C series, with a propylthio group at position 4 of the ring. [f:theobald2006]

## metabolism

In rat studies the main phase I steps for the 2Cs, 2C-T-7 included, were deamination and O-demethylation. With human enzymes expressed in vitro, deamination goes mainly through MAO-A and MAO-B; CYP2D6 contributes only to a very small extent. [f:theobald2006]

In rat urine the main metabolite is the N-acetylated sulfoxide with β-hydroxylation. [f:kanamori2016]

## effects

It produces psychedelic effects in humans; 4-thio-substituted phenylalkylamines have nanomolar affinity for 5-HT2 receptor subtypes and are partial agonists at 5-HT2A. [f:halberstadt2022]

In the mouse, potency measured with the head-twitch response rises when the 4-methylthio group of the parent 2C-T is extended by one to three methylene units, as in 2C-T-2 and 2C-T-7. [f:halberstadt2022]

## risks

One death is described: a 20-year-old man died in an emergency room after reportedly insufflating 35 mg; post-mortem concentrations were 57 ng/mL in heart blood, 100 ng/mL in femoral blood, 1120 ng/mL in urine and 854 ng/g in liver. [f:curtis2003]

In vitro 2C-T-7 was cytotoxic to SH-SY5Y neuronal cells and rat cortical cultures, with mitochondrial dysfunction and imbalance of intracellular calcium; these are cell data, not data on people. [f:gil-martins2024]

## interactions

Deamination depends mainly on MAO-A and MAO-B: given the enzymes involved, 2C-T-7 is likely to be susceptible to interactions with MAO inhibitors. [f:theobald2006]

In vitro 13 of the 17 substances tested, including 2C-T-2 and 2C-T-7, inhibited MAO-A and 11 inhibited MAO-B (IC50 10 to 125 µM for 7 substances on MAO-A and 1.7 to 180 µM for 9 on MAO-B); the study does not rule out a pharmacological contribution of the inhibition, and the values for single substances are not in the abstract. [f:wagmann2018]

## counselling

Ask about the route of use and whether the amount is known: in the published fatal case the person had reportedly insufflated the substance. [f:curtis2003]

Ask about medicines, in particular MAO inhibitors, and about alcohol, LSD, psilocybin and MDMA: metabolism goes through the MAOs and the UBC guidelines advise against these mixes (with MDMA seizures can occur). [f:theobald2006] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

If the person knows other 2C substances, point out that potency varies between members of the 4-thio series (mouse data); the UBC guidelines note that 2C-X substances act at small amounts and suggest approaching the dose gradually. [f:halberstadt2022] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
