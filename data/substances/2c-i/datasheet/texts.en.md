## molecule

2C-I, 2-(4-iodo-2,5-dimethoxyphenyl)ethan-1-amine. Base: C10H14INO2, 307.13 g/mol, CAS 69587-11-7. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

Bands of the hydrochloride in the SWGDRUG library spectrum (ATR), read from the file to about ±2 cm-1: about 1207 cm-1 (the strongest; asymmetric aryl C-O-C), then about 1024 cm-1 and 1045 cm-1 (symmetric C-O-C), 1485, 850 and 1381 cm-1. Two lots of the library agree within 2 cm-1. [f:swgdrug-ir-library] [f:smith-ethers]

The 2,5-dimethoxy ring gives the same aryl ether bands in every 2C compound, so the band at about 1205 cm-1 points to the group, not to the substance; the SWGDRUG monograph of 2C-B notes that, with the parameters used, the IR spectrum does not tell 2C-B from 2C-I. [f:smith-ethers] [f:swgdrug-2cb]

## category

Chemical class: phenethylamines, 2C series. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## history

2C-I is among the compounds whose synthesis Alexander Shulgin described in PIHKAL (1991); the iodine at position 4 is among the substitutions that increase the hallucinogenic effect. [f:dean2013]

Until the summer of 2012 the only 2C compounds in Schedule I of the US Controlled Substances Act were 2C-B and 2C-T-7; 2C-I was added to that list afterwards. [f:dean2013]

## origin

2C-I is a synthetic substance of the 2C series of phenethylamines, among the designer drugs. [f:dean2013]

## metabolism

In rats 2C-I was metabolised by O-demethylation at position 2 and 5, followed by N-acetylation or by deamination to the acid or the alcohol, and directly by deamination; 2C-I and most metabolites were partly excreted in conjugated form. [f:theobald2006i]

With human enzymes MAO-A and MAO-B were the major enzymes of deamination of 2C-I. [f:theobald2007]

## effects

In the 2C series the reported effects combine hallucinogenic and stimulant ones and depend on the dose: stimulation and heightened senses at low doses, hallucinations at moderate doses, unpleasant hallucinations with tachycardia, hypertension and hyperthermia at higher doses. [f:dean2013]

In rats 2C-I substituted for at least one hallucinogen in drug discrimination, but not for methamphetamine; in cell assays it was a full agonist at the 5-HT2A receptor in the inositol phosphate assay, an antagonist in the arachidonic acid assay, and had moderate affinity for the serotonin transporter. [f:eshleman2014]

## doses

The reported oral dose is 14-22 mg with a duration of 6-10 h; the figures are the anecdotal ones of Shulgin's 1991 book, quoted by a review, not results of controlled studies. [f:dean2013]

Products sold online often do not contain the substance or the dose on the label. [f:dean2013]

## risks

Intoxication with 2C drugs presents with hallucinations, agitation, aggression, dysphoria, hypertension, tachycardia, seizures and hyperthermia; the deaths attributed to 2C drugs showed an excited delirium picture. [f:dean2013]

A hemorrhagic stroke occurred in a 39-year-old woman who, according to her friends, had taken cocaine, MDMA and 2C-I (urine: 2C-I and MDA); the cause was an underlying cerebrovascular abnormality, Moyamoya, described as secondary to substance abuse. [f:drees2009]

A product sold as 2C-I and bought online was confirmed as 2C-E in ten patients, with severe agitation or delirium in eight and one death. [f:dean2013]

## interactions

Because MAO-A and MAO-B deaminate the 2C compounds, monoamine oxidase inhibitors can raise their concentrations and the risk of toxicity. [f:dean2013]

## counselling

Ask which 2C compound the person expects: 2C-E has been sold as 2C-I (ten patients, one died), and the IR spectrum of 2C-I is not told apart from that of 2C-B in the SWGDRUG monograph, so the result alone does not separate them. [f:dean2013] [f:swgdrug-2cb]

Hallucinogenic effects depend on set and setting: ask where and with whom it will be taken, whether the person is well and has slept, and suggest not taking it alone; at moderate doses hallucinations appear and at higher ones they can be unpleasant. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Explain that the review quotes 14-22 mg for 2C-I with effects lasting 6-10 h, and that the UBC guidelines, written for 2C-B, say that a few mg more or less change the effects and suggest approaching the dose gradually. [f:dean2013] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]

Ask about other substances and MAO inhibitors: deamination of 2C-I goes through the MAOs, and a stroke was described in a woman who had taken cocaine, MDMA and 2C-I; the UBC guidelines, written for 2C-B, advise against mixing it with alcohol and medicines. [f:theobald2007] [f:drees2009] [f:ubc-counselling-guidelines]
