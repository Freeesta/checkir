## molecule

Crotonylfentanyl, (E)-N-phenyl-N-[1-(2-phenylethyl)piperidin-4-yl]but-2-enamide. Base: C23H28N2O, 348.5 g/mol. Identity checked against PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: fentanyl analogues (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a fentanyl analogue with an N-acyl alkenyl side chain. [f:murakami2026]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes crotonylfentanyl, with its alkenyl N-acyl chain, deviated from the structure-metabolism trend seen in the alkyl analogues (acetylfentanyl, fentanyl, butyrylfentanyl, valerylfentanyl). [f:murakami2026]

## effects

Fentanyl analogues are full agonists at the μ-opioid receptor; the typical clinical effects are miosis and respiratory and central nervous system depression. [f:armenian2018]

## doses

The potency of fentanyl analogues varies widely between compounds, so no dose is safe to suggest. [f:lovrecic2019]

## risks

In poisonings with fentanyl analogues larger naloxone doses are needed than in a classic heroin overdose. [f:lovrecic2019] [f:armenian2018]

## interactions

Benzodiazepines and other sedatives mixed with opioids are a risk factor for overdose. [f:kerensky2017]
