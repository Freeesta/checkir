## molecule

4-MA (4-methylamphetamine), 1-(4-methylphenyl)propan-2-amine. Formula C10H15N, 149.2 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

An amphetamine with a methyl group on position 4 of the phenyl ring; not to be confused with methamphetamine, which has the methyl on the nitrogen. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: amphetamines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, originally developed as an appetite suppressant and dropped because of side effects; it resurfaced in Europe as a new psychoactive substance. [f:blanckaert2013] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

The authors of a review suggest that a slower metabolism of 4-MA and its monoamine oxidase inhibiting properties may contribute to its unusual toxicity. [f:blanckaert2013]

## cuts

In Europe 4-MA was mostly found together with amphetamine: it was present in about 11.5% of the Dutch speed samples tested. [f:blanckaert2013]
