## origin

A synthetic compound marketed as capsules for the treatment of ADHD. [f:atomoxetine-label]

## metabolism

The main oxidative pathway, in all CYP2D6 metabolizer types, is CYP2D6-mediated 4-hydroxylation to 4-hydroxyatomoxetine, equipotent to atomoxetine as a norepinephrine transporter inhibitor and then glucuronidated. N-desmethylatomoxetine is formed by CYP2C19 and other CYP enzymes and is about 20-fold less active. [f:atomoxetine-label]

More than 80% of the dose is excreted in urine as 4-hydroxyatomoxetine-O-glucuronide and less than 3% as unchanged drug. The half-life is 5.2 h in extensive and 21.6 h in CYP2D6 poor metabolizers. [f:atomoxetine-label]
