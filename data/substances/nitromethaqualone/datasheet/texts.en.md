## molecule

Nitromethaqualone, 3-(2-methoxy-4-nitrophenyl)-2-methylquinazolin-4-one. Formula C16H13N3O4, 311.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a quinazolin-4-one of the methaqualone type; the benzodiazepine family sheet does not cover it, so no family sheet is attached. Expected effect: central nervous system depressant (sedative), by structural analogy with methaqualone. Typical role in samples: active principle. ## metabolism

In humans and rats the nitro group is reduced to the amino derivative, partly acetylated; in humans the quinazolinone ring is also cleaved to 2-methoxy-4-nitroaniline. In rats, the 2-methyl group is oxidised to hydroxymethyl, and the hydroxymethyl metabolites are excreted partly as glucuronides. Protracted excretion points to an extensive enterohepatic circulation. [f:vanboven1982]
