## molecule

Tetrahydrocannabivarin (Δ9-THCV), (6aR,10aR)-6,6,9-trimethyl-3-propyl-6a,7,8,10a-tetrahydrobenzo[c]chromen-1-ol. Base: C19H26O2, 286.4 g/mol, CAS 31262-37-0. Identity checked against PubChem (entry with the (6aR,10aR) stereochemistry). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: plant-based substances (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: cannabinoid. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

Phytocannabinoid of cannabis, structurally similar to THC; it is becoming popular on the North American cannabis market and is usually, and incorrectly, classed as an intoxicating analogue of THC. [f:haghdoost2024-thcv]

## metabolism

After oral administration of Δ8-THCV to healthy participants (the Δ8 isomer, not Δ9-THCV), the main urinary metabolites were 11-nor-9-carboxy-Δ8-THCV, 11-hydroxy-Δ8-THCV and Δ9-THCV-COOH, extensively glucuronidated. [f:sempio2026-thcv]

In those urine samples 70 of 80 pooled samples were reported positive by a cannabinoid immunoassay targeting Δ9-THC-COOH while negative for Δ9-THC-COOH by LC-MS/MS, so the metabolites cross-react with the immunoassay. [f:sempio2026-thcv]

## effects

THCV has been reported to reduce blood sugar and act as an appetite suppressant in preclinical studies, with few human studies; its pharmacological activity and physiological effects at the doses reported differ from those of THC. [f:haghdoost2024-thcv]
