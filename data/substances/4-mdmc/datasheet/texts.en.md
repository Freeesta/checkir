## molecule

4-MDMC (4-Methyl-N,N-dimethylcathinone), 2-(dimethylamino)-1-(4-methylphenyl)propan-1-one. Base: C12H17NO, 191.27 g/mol, CAS 1157738-08-3. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified there). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: synthetic cathinones (UNODC grouping). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance, a synthetic cathinone: 4-methyl-N,N-dimethylcathinone, i.e. the N,N-dimethylated cathinone with a methyl in position 4 of the phenyl ring. [f:pubchem] [f:lopes2020]

## metabolism

In human and rat liver microsomes with phase I and glucuronidation cofactors, several phase I metabolites and glucuronides were identified; the authors show that 4-MDMC can act as a prodrug of 4-MMC (mephedrone). In vitro data, not in humans. [f:lopes2020]

## effects

Synthetic cathinones have stimulant and, in part, hallucinogenic properties; no data on the effects of this compound in humans were found in the sources used. [f:machacek2026]

## risks

Synthetic cathinones are associated with acute symptomatic seizures, often fatal: of 42 reported cases, death occurred in almost half, with higher lethality among cathinone users. [f:machacek2026]

## interactions

In most cases with seizures, serotonin and sympathomimetic toxidromes were present. [f:machacek2026]
