## molecule

Methylnaphthidate (HDMP-28), methyl 2-(naphthalen-2-yl)-2-(piperidin-2-yl)acetate. Base: C18H21NO2, 283.37 g/mol, CAS 231299-82-4 (threo). Identity checked on PubChem, which lists the threo form with relative stereochemistry (CID 9817261); the flat structure without stereochemistry is used here (CID 49823708). [f:pubchem]

It is the analogue of methylphenidate (own sheet) with a naphthyl in place of the phenyl; it has two chiral centres. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: Phenidates. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a phenidate. [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

An in vitro study with equine liver microsomes and the fungus Cunninghamella elegans identified 31 metabolites (22 phase I and 9 phase II): hydroxylated, hydrogenated and hydrolyzed analogues, and glucuronic acid conjugates. The authors note that the metabolites in vivo might not be the same. [f:muhammed2024]
