## molecule

## category

Chemical class: other substances; no family sheet is attached. It is a designer anabolic steroid. The effect axis has no steroid class: "no psychoactive effect expected" is a checkir proposal awaiting review. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:geldof2016] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic designer steroid made of two methasterone molecules linked by an azine group, found in a product sold as "Xtreme DMZ". PubChem lists it as mebolazine. [f:geldof2016] [f:pubchem]

## metabolism

In a stability study dimethazine degraded to methasterone. In human liver microsomes and in a chimeric mouse model, methasterone and six further metabolites (M1 to M6), all of them methasterone metabolites, were detected besides the parent compound. [f:geldof2016]

M1 and M2 were found only in the glucuronide-conjugated fraction. The authors conclude that screening for methasterone and its metabolites should be enough to detect misuse. There are no human administration data. [f:geldof2016]
