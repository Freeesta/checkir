## molecule

5-MeO-DALT, N-[2-(5-methoxy-1H-indol-3-yl)ethyl]-N-prop-2-enylprop-2-en-1-amine. Base: C17H22N2O, 270.37 g/mol, CAS 928822-98-4. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: 5-methoxytryptamine with two allyl groups on the amine nitrogen. [f:pubchem] [f:checkir-classification]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes 11 metabolites formed; the main phase I steps were N-oxidation, aromatic hydroxylation, N-dealkylation and O-demethylation, and phase II glucuronidation followed hydroxylation or O-demethylation. [f:meng2025]

The aromatic-hydroxylated and N-depropylated metabolites and the hydroxylated glucuronide were proposed as markers of intake. [f:meng2025]
