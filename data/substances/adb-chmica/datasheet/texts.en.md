## effects

Synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonists are potent full agonists. [f:jones2025]

In 16 emergency-department patients with confirmed ADB-CHMINACA intake, after inhalation, the most common signs were panic attacks, tachycardia, agitation, vomiting and seizures; one patient developed posterior reversible encephalopathy syndrome. [f:hermanns-clausen2026]

## doses

Serum concentrations of ADB-CHMINACA in the 16 patients ranged from 0.2 to 31 ng/mL, without a clear correlation with the severity of poisoning; no dose is safe to suggest. [f:hermanns-clausen2026]

## risks

The poisoning was moderate to severe and all 16 patients recovered; the patient with encephalopathy recovered after 4 days. [f:hermanns-clausen2026]

In mice, MAB-CHMINACA was associated with acute behavioural toxicity and central nervous system lesions (neuronal vacuolisation, necrosis, oedema, inflammatory changes) in a descriptive, hypothesis-generating study. [f:filipiuc2026]

Synthetic cannabinoids have been found as adulterants of several recreational substances, including cannabis and opioids, with unpredictable clinical outcomes. [f:jones2025]

In a cohort comparison, hospitalisations for synthetic cannabinoid psychosis lasted longer than those for cannabis psychosis (43.45 vs 22.91 days). [f:ricci2026]

## interactions

Adulteration of cannabis and opioids with synthetic cannabinoids raises the potential for drug interactions. [f:jones2025]
