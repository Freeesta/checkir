## molecule

DB-MDBP, 1-[(2,2-difluoro-1,3-benzodioxol-5-yl)methyl]piperazine. Formula C12H14F2N2O2, 256.3 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem, which lists DB-MDBP and DF-MDBP as names of this compound. [f:pubchem]

A piperazine with a benzyl group on one nitrogen; the benzene ring carries a difluoromethylenedioxy group, the difluoro analogue of the methylenedioxy group of MDBP (1-(3,4-methylenedioxybenzyl)piperazine). [f:uchiyama2015]

## category

Chemical class: piperazines. Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic compound, a piperazine. It was identified as a new psychoactive substance in illicit products distributed in Japan in 2015, as DF-MDBP. [f:uchiyama2015]
