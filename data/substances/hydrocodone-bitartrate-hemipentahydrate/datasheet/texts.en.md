## molecule

Hydrocodone bitartrate hemipentahydrate: the bitartrate salt of hydrocodone with 2.5 water molecules per salt unit (the PubChem entry holds two hydrocodone, two tartaric acid and five water). Salt formula C44H64N2O23, 989.0 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

The base is hydrocodone, (4R,4aR,7aR,12bS)-9-methoxy-3-methyl-1,2,4,4a,5,6,7a,13-octahydro-4,12-methanobenzofuro[3,2-e]isoquinolin-7-one. Formula C18H21NO3, 299.4 g/mol, InChIKey LLPOLZWFYMWNKH-CMKMFDCUSA-N. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: morphinans, among the plant-based substances of the UNODC grouping. Expected effect: opioid. Typical role in samples: active principle. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A semisynthetic opiate: the semisynthetic opiate drugs are produced chemically from the natural morphine alkaloids (morphine, codeine and thebaine), and hydrocodone is among them. [f:boonstra2001]

## metabolism

In human liver microsomes hydrocodone is O-demethylated to hydromorphone, mainly by CYP2D6, and N-demethylated to norhydrocodone, attributed to CYP3A4. [f:hutchinson2004] Norhydrocodone is a major inactive metabolite and hydromorphone a minor active one. [f:kapil2015]

In 76924 urine specimens from patients on chronic opioid therapy the mean mole fractions were 0.39 hydrocodone, 0.12 hydromorphone and 0.49 norhydrocodone. [f:barakat2014]
