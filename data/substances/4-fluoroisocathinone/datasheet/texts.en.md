## molecule

4-Fluoroisocathinone (1-amino-1-(4-fluorophenyl)acetone), 1-amino-1-(4-fluorophenyl)propan-2-one. Base: C9H10FNO, 167.18 g/mol, CAS 1270532-42-7. Identity checked against PubChem (racemic entry; one stereocentre, not specified there). [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances; not a cathinone (see origin). Expected effect: stimulant. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic substance. In isocathinones the amine sits on the benzylic carbon and the carbonyl in position 2 (1-amino-1-arylpropan-2-one), not α to the carbonyl as in cathinones; for pentedrone and pentylone the isocathinones are described as typical by-products of cathinone synthesis. Here the phenyl ring carries a fluorine atom in position 4. [f:westphal2012] [f:pubchem]
