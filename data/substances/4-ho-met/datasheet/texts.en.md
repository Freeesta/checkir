## molecule

4-HO-MET (metocin), 3-[2-[ethyl(methyl)amino]ethyl]-1H-indol-4-ol. Formula C13H18N2O, 218.29 g/mol, CAS 77872-41-4. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: tryptamines. Expected effect: psychedelic. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:unodc-ewa] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic tryptamine: a 4-hydroxy-N,N-dialkyltryptamine, the same type as psilocin (4-HO-DMT) with different N-alkyl groups. [f:pubchem] [f:klein2021]

## metabolism

In the blood of a patient who had taken 4-HO-MET, four metabolites were detected: N-demethyl-, oxo- and hydroxy-4-HO-MET and the N-oxide; they were found only with high-resolution LC-MS/MS. [f:wagmann2022]
