## molecule

Meprobamate, [2-(carbamoyloxymethyl)-2-methylpentyl] carbamate. Formula C9H18N2O4, 218.25 g/mol. Identity checked on PubChem. [f:pubchem]

## category

Chemical class: other substances. It is a carbamate anxiolytic, indicated for anxiety disorders. Expected effect: depressant / sedative. Typical role in samples: active principle. This classification is a checkir proposal awaiting review. [f:meprobamate-label] [f:checkir-classification]

## origin

A synthetic pharmaceutical substance used as a medicine for anxiety disorders. [f:meprobamate-label]

## metabolism

Meprobamate is metabolized in the liver and excreted by the kidney. [f:meprobamate-label]

In healthy volunteers given carisoprodol, of which meprobamate is the main active metabolite, the half-life of meprobamate was 9 ± 1.9 hours after a single 350 mg dose of carisoprodol. [f:calvo2022]
