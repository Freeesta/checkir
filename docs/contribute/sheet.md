# Propose or correct a substance sheet
Italiano: [sheet.it.md](sheet.it.md)

For chemists and health workers. Sheets are published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

1. Open the form "Substance sheet": [new issue](https://github.com/Freeesta/checkir/issues/new?template=sheet.yml).
2. Say which substance and which section you want to add or correct, and write the proposed text.
3. **Sources are required** for every statement (a paper, a monograph, an official report). Give links or full references.
4. Tick the declaration boxes and submit.

## Review
- A chemist checks the analytical content (bands, forms, limits of FTIR).
- **A listed reviewer always approves the health texts** (effects, risks, harm-reduction points). No maintainer approves them alone. Until a section is approved, the program shows "text under review" instead.
- Doses only follow the project rules and are never given for potent substances.

## Rules
Write in short sentences, one fact each. Say "the sample", not "your drug". Never write "safe", "pure" or "clean". No synthesis, extraction, potentiation or cutting instructions; no vendors or places to buy; no personal data. Always keep the FTIR limits: components below about 5 % are not seen, potent substances at low doses are not excluded, no quantities.
